# Focused user questions

These questions define what the paper ontology and extraction must support.
They are requirements, not proposed fields or a required output format.
The broader system question set remains in `docs/COMPETENCY_QUESTIONS.md`.

## Find and understand one or more papers

1. What did this paper try to do, and which contributions did it make?
2. Which concepts, constructs, theories, frameworks and practical topics did it address?
3. Which EuroQol and non-EuroQol instruments did it develop, adapt, use, score, compare or produce?
4. Which exact instrument families, versions, variants and language versions were involved?
5. Does the paper contain different phases, samples, tasks or analyses, and how do they relate?
6. Who supplied the evidence, who or what did their answers describe, and for which population or decision context were the results intended?
7. In which countries, languages, clinical areas or health conditions, age groups, care settings and policy settings did the work occur?
8. How were participants or other evidence sources selected, recruited, included and excluded; how many contributed at each important stage; and which were analyzed?
9. What study design and detailed domain methods did each study component use?
10. For valuation work, did it use cTTO, another TTO form, DCE, DCE with duration, PTO, VAS or another task, and under which protocol or framing?
11. How were instruments and tasks administered: digital, web, app, paper, interview, telephone, self-report, proxy report or another mode?
12. Which important statistical, modeling, scoring, mapping, psychometric or qualitative methods were used, for what purpose, and what type of inference did they support?
13. What did the study compare, and which material differences in population, timing, language, mode, scoring route or other conditions affect interpretation?
14. Which products did the work create, what stage had each product reached, and what future steps remained?
15. What were the principal findings and the authors' main interpretation?
16. Which limitations did the authors report, and which uncertainties, conflicting source statements or transfer limits remain after extraction?
17. What scientific, practical, policy, implementation or instrument-development implications did the authors report, and did the paper document any actual use or effect?
18. Which future work or evidence gaps did the paper identify?
19. Did the paper reuse a sample, data set, protocol, value set, mapping function or model from another study?

## Ask across the corpus

20. Which studies match a combination of instrument, version, language, population, country, condition, method, administration mode and purpose?
21. How do methods and analytic choices differ across valuation, psychometric, mapping, translation, population-norm, implementation and other EuroQol study families?
22. What does the corpus report about a given instrument, method, population, property or use context, and where do findings agree or differ?
23. Which value sets, translations, mappings, protocols, instruments, bolt-ons, population norms and other products exist, and what is their reported maturity?
24. Which populations, countries, languages, settings and administration modes have been studied?
25. Which papers use independent evidence, and which reuse or extend the same evidence?
26. How has EuroQol research changed over time?
27. Which combinations of population, instrument, language, method, setting and research question are absent or weakly covered in this corpus?

## Supplied by other layers

The semantic extraction agent does not reconstruct DOI, title, authors, affiliations, dates, journal, licence, funding, references, citation counts or project metadata when a deterministic source provides them.
These layers remain available for combined user queries.
