## Material close calls

- G083 is `HEALTH_ECONOMIC_EVALUATION`, not methods research. The study
  compares costs and QALYs for dialysis decisions; this is the earlier row.
- S024 and D013 are `METHODS_RESEARCH`. S024 compares crosswalk and native
  value-set methods. D013 reuses three selected datasets to assess kaizen-task
  performance; it does not systematically identify prior evidence.
- S058 is `METHODS_RESEARCH`, not instrument-version development. Its stated
  contribution is selection of a domain-identification approach, not new
  instrument content.
- C002 and C012 are `INSTRUMENT_VERSION_DEVELOPMENT`, not measurement-property
  evaluation. A new short form or new bolt-on content is a principal output.
- C004 is `POPULATION_REFERENCE_DESCRIPTION`, not health-outcome research. Its
  stated aim is to present population health-status data for Swedish
  adolescents.
- C006 is `APPLIED_USE_RESEARCH`, not health-outcome research. The principal
  decision concerns provision and use of nerve blocks; clinical outcomes are
  secondary.
- C010 remains `VALUE_SET_DEVELOPMENT`. The retracted publication status does
  not change the reported study aim or family.
- D005 is `METHODS_RESEARCH`, not conceptual-framework development. It proposes
  and demonstrates an operational effectiveness-scaling method.
- D012 is `CONCEPTUAL_FRAMEWORK_DEVELOPMENT`, not measurement-property
  evaluation. The explicit principal output is a lay quality-of-life
  framework; comparison with EQ-HWB is secondary.
