## Proposed ontology changes

All changes below remain proposals.

### P01 — `HEALTH_OUTCOME_RESEARCH` primary family

Use when the main contribution is an empirical description, comparison, determinant analysis, or trajectory of health, HRQoL, symptoms, functioning, or well-being in a patient or condition-defined population, and not norms/reference data, measurement performance, implementation, synthesis, or economic evaluation. Evidence: S040 and S091. S099 remains `POPULATION_REFERENCE_DESCRIPTION` because it provides national reference estimates.

### P02 — `PARTICIPATORY_DESIGN` method function

Use when intended users or stakeholders jointly create, refine, or select a product, service, workflow, display, or implementation program. Keep `QUALITATIVE_DATA_COLLECTION` and `QUALITATIVE_ANALYSIS` in separate method-use records. Evidence: S057.

### P03 — stakeholder-involvement structure

Add a structure that records stakeholder group, study part, activity or role, claimed influence on the work, and evidence locator. Do not set controlled role values until repeated papers support stable distinctions. Evidence: S057 and round-1 gap G168.

## Round-1 regression risks

- P01 is additive and does not invalidate a round-1 family assignment. G125 remains `POPULATION_REFERENCE_DESCRIPTION` because its purpose and output are population norms/reference data. The other round-1 applications also retain their assigned families.
- P02 does not invalidate round-1 method functions or families. G010 and G131 need source evidence of joint product creation before it can apply; their round-1 summaries do not establish that condition.
- P03 directly extends the open G168 issue about youth co-researcher involvement. It can resolve that gap after source review, but it does not change G168's family, purposes, or existing method uses.
- G160 interviewer-score construction and all other round-1 uncertainties and source conflicts remain unchanged. No proposal deletes or redefines an existing controlled value.
