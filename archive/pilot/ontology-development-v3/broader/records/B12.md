# B12: Indonesian FACIT-COST validation

## Identity and study type

- DOI: `10.1186/s12955-024-02303-5`.
- Purposes: measurement-property evaluation; translated-instrument validation;
  longitudinal clinical outcomes.
- Design: single-center prospective cohort.
- EuroQol support: data-collection funding, project `1644-RA`.

## Population and samples

- Adult women receiving breast-cancer treatment at Hasan Sadikin General
  Hospital, Bandung, Indonesia, September 2023 to March 2024.
- Baseline: 300 participants, mean age 51 years, 96.8% response.
- Follow-up: 148 active-treatment participants after mean 5.8 weeks and 150
  final-cycle participants after mean 11.6 weeks.

## Concepts and themes

- financial toxicity; cancer burden; health inequality; patient financial
  coping; cross-cultural measurement; longitudinal change; personalized care.

## Instruments and administration

- Official Indonesian FACIT-COST version 2, 12 items; items 1 to 11 form the 0
  to 44 score and item FT12 is a global hardship item.
- EQ-HWB, EQ-5D-5L, WEMWBS, and FACT-G were also administered, but their
  results were reserved for other reports.
- Fixed-order paper questionnaires, patient self-completion in Indonesian, with
  research-assistant explanation available.
- Nurse questionnaire and hospital records supplied clinical data.

## Methods, protocol, and task design

- Tested distribution, structural validity, internal consistency,
  known-groups validity, test-retest reliability, and responsiveness.
- FT12 change classified participants as unchanged, improved, or worsened and
  anchored reliability and responsiveness analyses.
- T1 and T2 follow-up groups were combined after they showed little difference.

## Analysis and statistical models

- Principal component analysis with parallel analysis and Promax rotation.
- Confirmatory factor models with diagonally weighted least squares; selected a
  two-factor model.
- Cronbach alpha; McDonald omega; Mann-Whitney; Kruskal-Wallis; Gwet AC2; ICC;
  effect size; standardized response mean; 1,000 bootstrap samples for model
  fit.

## Products

- Validation evidence for the existing official Indonesian FACIT-COST v2; no
  new translation was made in this study.

## Outcomes or measurement properties

- Structural validity; internal consistency; known-groups validity; test-
  retest reliability; responsiveness; floor and ceiling effects.

## Principal findings and interpretation

- The selected two-factor model fit well: RMSEA 0.042, SRMR 0.049, CFI 0.99,
  and TLI 0.99.
- Factor alpha values were 0.774 to 0.882 and omega values were 0.786 to 0.888.
- Scores distinguished groups by age, education, residence, income,
  employment, metastasis, symptoms, and coping strategies.
- Instrument-level test-retest ICC was 0.96.
- Responsiveness was large for improvement and small to borderline moderate for
  worsening.
- The authors support use for Indonesian breast-cancer care and financial-aid
  referral.

## Limitations and source issues

- The sample included women with one cancer type at one public hospital, with
  99.7% insurance coverage.
- Some subgroup analyses can be underpowered.
- Follow-up was short. Larger, broader, and longer studies are needed.
- EQ instruments were administered but did not supply results in this article.
  They must not inherit FACIT-COST findings.
