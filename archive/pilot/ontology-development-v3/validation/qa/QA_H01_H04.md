## Observed ontology gaps and unnecessary fields across H01–H04

- **Gap — DCE design metadata:** H02 needs searchable fields for design-construction method, software, prior assumptions, overlap structure, and analysis subset. These details are currently forced into general task-design prose.
- **Gap — cohort/source-dataset provenance:** H04 shows the need to distinguish a source cohort, a diagnostic subgroup, a deduplicated analytic sample, and a merged dataset total.
- **Gap — method-to-product multiplicity:** H03 shows that one preference-elicitation method can produce both individual and aggregate utility products with different reliability status.
- **Unnecessary in the core ontology:** extraction date, manual-extraction label, review status, and repeated source/provenance prose are audit metadata. They should remain available, but as provenance fields rather than research-content concepts.
