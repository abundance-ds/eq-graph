After the POPS 2 pilot demonstrated feasibility for the full POPS 2 project, the aim of this proposed project is to obtain and archive EQ-5D-5L general population datasets and produce a new edition of population health data, including norms and inequality indicators based on EQ-5D-5L surveys conducted in representative samples of general populations. Specifically, the following are the key objectives: Component 1: a) create a database archive of EQ-5D-5L data from general population surveys b) standardize the data structure and document a user guide Component 2: a) generate EQ-5D-5L population norms b) generate basic inequality indicators, both from epidemiological and economic origin c) make basic cross-country comparison of EQ-5D-5L population data Component 2 of the project will be supported by a separate methods pilot project (EQ Project 1600-RA) which began earlier in 2023 to establish the analytic and reporting approach for the main project. The major deliverables are 1) the POPS 2 data archive, 2) documentation for archive data use, 3) a report reporting the standardized country-level EQ-5D-5L norms, and 4) at least one publications in peer reviewed journals. The POPS 2 archive can drive future research activities for the HISIG, such as in-depth analyses of health inequalities and cross-country comparisons. The project would also have implications for other working Groups, such as the Valuation and the Education & Outreach Working Groups. This project will also collaborate actively with other ongoing EuroQol funded efforts to use EQ-instruments on the population level.
