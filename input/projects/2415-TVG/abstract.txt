Funding is requested to allow Nils Gutacker to visit several EuroQol members and local experts in the routine collection and use of PROM data at their respective universities for 3 weeks in 2026. The purpose of this visit is to deepen research collaborations, develop grant applications on comparative health system performance assessment and determinants of PROM instrument completion, and to promote knowledge exchange between Canadian policymakers and Gutacker, reflecting their respective expertise in PROM-based system management in Canada and England.
