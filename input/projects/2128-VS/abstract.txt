Serbia is a middle-income country located in South Eastern Europe, where the valuation set for health-related quality of life has never been done before. It is unknown how the Serbian population will value different health conditions and what their preferences are, which would be very important from the perspective of growing interest in the process of Health Technology Assessment (HTA) and valuation of Patient-Reported Outcome Measures.  The objective of this study is to produce a preference-based national value set for EQ-5D-5L instrument, strictly using an EuroQol Valuation Technology (EQ-VT) protocol and hybrid techniques combining Time-Trade-Off (TTO) with a Discrete Choice Experiment (DCE). According to the protocol, the required sample for a country valuation study is 1000 participants who are nationally representative in terms of age, sex, region, and type of settlement they live in (urban/rural area). Quota sample will be determined by the company for market research in Serbia who will also organize data collection in the field. Interviewers will be a mix of students and professional interviewers who will receive training from a previously trained Principal Investigator and another researcher from the core team, during ToT by EuroQoL Research Foundation. Training will be followed by practicing and piloting the study. Interviews will be done in a combination of face-to-face and online interviews. Every step of the process will be carefully monitored and supervised by members of our research team, on a daily basis, in order to ensure quality control. Meetings with national stakeholders are planned for the beginning and the end of the project. Three interim meetings with interviewers are planned during data collection, after achieving 25%, 50% and 75% of target sample, along with continuous consultations and reporting EuroQoL Research Foundation.
