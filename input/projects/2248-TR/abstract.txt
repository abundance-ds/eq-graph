Currently there is only a paper version of the EQ-HWB-9 questionnaire. YPrime was contracted to create a digital prototype that included 3 routes of the item-by item EQ-HWB-9 layout and to test it on appropriate users. The project gathered patient feedback on the digitised EQ-HWB-9 through moderated 1:1 user testing sessions. Focus areas included task completion, user sentiment, device behavior, accessibility, and design variances. Sixteen participants interacted with the prototypes on their personal mobile (69%) or tablet device (31%). Participants were over 60 years old (range 62-81 years) and had one or more qualifying conditions/disabilities, which included limited hand mobility to health condition, stroke or injury (7/16); colour vision deficiency (4/16); difficulty with concentration (3/16); reading disability (3/16). Data privacy agreements were signed, and verbal consent was obtained for recording interviews. All participants were able to independently complete the testing. They found it easy to engage with, reported acceptability of the instructions, were satisfied with the length of the questionnaire, and expressed some preferences. The digital designs adhered to the Electronic Clinical Outcome Assessment (eCOA) Consortium best practice recommendations surrounding electronic data. Fonts and approach used were consistent across all screens; no items were preselected when the respondent opened the questionnaire or any screen within it; scrolling should be allowed but all response options on a given screen must be viewed before the navigation button becomes available;  skipping should also be allowed but a validation message should be in place to have the participant confirm if they chose to skip intentionally or if they did so in error.
