Background: Recent stakeholder engagement suggests that young people, including children and adolescents, should be involved in health state valuations of paediatric health utility measures. However, valuation tasks may be too demanding for young people to complete, and adaptations may be required to make these tasks more accessible to them. Aims: This project aims to co-design an improved way of including children and young people in paediatric health state valuation tasks by adapting the existing EQ-5D-Y valuation protocol. Methods: This study will utilise a co-design methodology, whereby, parents, children and adolescents, paediatric experts (e.g., paediatricians, child psychologists, and ethicists) and valuation experts will be asked to co-design an improved, child-friendly version of the EQ-5D-Y valuation and interview protocol. During co-design, participants will help to design and refine an updated EQ-5D-Y valuation and interview protocol for young people (this is known as the ‘prototype’).
