Background: The EuroQol methodology (EQ-5D) has been used several times in Georgia, with a recent surge in interest in economic evaluation alongside their world-leading viral hepatitis elimination program. In 2021, the EQ-5D-5L was included in a nationally representative health survey in Georgia. However, the absence of a Georgian value set for either the EQ-5D-3L or 5L limits its application in health policy and economic evaluation.Objective: This study aimed to develop a Georgian value set for EQ-5D-3L and EQ-5D-5L to enable more accurate and culturally relevant health economics studies, which will be crucial for policy-making and healthcare improvements in the region. Methods: The EuroQol EQ-VT version 2 standardised valuation protocol was used to gather EQ-5D-3L related preferences from the Georgian population. A total of 300 interviews were conducted. The interviews included both composite time-trade-off (cTTO) and discrete choice experiment (DCE) questions. Data were analyzed, accounting for the heteroskedastic nature and censoring at -1 of the cTTO data, using a Tobit model. This model was combined with a logit model for the DCE data in a hybrid model and compared to other modeling methods. The EQ-5D-3L values were used to estimate EQ-5D-5L values using a crosswalk method.Results: Within the Georgia value set, mobility level 3 had the largest disutility, followed by pain/discomfort level 3. In contrast, for level 2 responses, self-care had the largest disutility, with mobility having the lowest disutility of level 2 responses. The lowest state (33333) had a predicted value of -1.16.
