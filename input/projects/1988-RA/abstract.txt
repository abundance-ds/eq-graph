**Background:** Content validity is considered a fundamental measurement property. While guidance exists for assessing content validity of patient reported outcome measures (PROMs) as a whole, there is no best practice guidance for assessing content validity in generic preference-weighted measures (PWMs), and EQ instruments in particular, which represent a ‘special case’ of PROMs. This may lead to inaccurate conclusions that generic PWMs do not have satisfactory content validity in particular health conditions because they are evaluated inappropriately. **Aim:** Developing best practice methodological guidance is iterative. This review contributes to ‘Phase 1’ and aims to: (i) identify contemporary examples of qualitative work assessing content validity in generic adult and paediatric PWMs, including EQ measures; and (ii) map what has been done against recommendations to identify examples of good practice and gaps for future work. Guidance recommendations will be evaluated to assess whether they are appropriate for assessing content validity in generic PWMs (and in what form). **Methods:** A scoping review will be conducted using a robust search strategy developed by an Information Specialist. Eight databases (MEDLINE, Embase, CINAHL, PsycINFO, Science and Social Sciences Citation Indexes, Econlit, Tufts CEA Registry, and MATHSCINET) will be searched. Contemporary qualitative content validity studies in generic adult and paediatric PWMs will be included and relevant data extracted. A descriptive narrative synthesis will be conducted mapping content validity studies onto existing guidance and appraised in the context of the unique challenges that exist in assessing content validity in generic PWMs.
