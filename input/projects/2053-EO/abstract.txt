This IHEA pre-congress session - held on the afternoon of the 19th July in Bali - presented and promoted the use of EQ-5D instruments in the Indo-Pacific region. The focus of the workshop was to encourage capacity strengthening. The pre-congress session sought to build on the numerous EQ-5D valuation studies in the Indo-Pacific region by additionally promoting applications of the EQ instruments in national health surveys, large cohort studies and electronic health records. The pre-congress session presented three case studies that specifically focus on the development and use of EuroQol instruments in the Indo-Pacific region: Indonesia, Singapore, New Zealand. The session provided an overview of the research opportunities and research expertise that is available to support health economists to undertake research using EQ instruments.In addition to funding the pre-congress session, the grant also provided financial sponsorship to the LMIC and student attendees.
