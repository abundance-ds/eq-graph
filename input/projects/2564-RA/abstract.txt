Background: EQ-5D-3L/-5L and other preference-based outcome measures (PBMs) can be  administered using different modes of administration (MoAs), including self-complete (paper and pencil and digital survey) and interviewer administration (face to face, telephone, and computer-assisted personal interview). To compare EQ-5D-3L/-5L data obtained using different MoAs or to be able to pool data obtained using different MoAs, evidence on the extent to which they produce similar health responses is needed. Also, It is important to know whether using different MoAs affects key Consensus-based Standards for the selection of health Measurement Instruments (COSMIN) measurement properties. Aims: To assess and compare the impact of using different MoAs on intra-rater response agreement and key COSMIN measurement properties of the EQ-5D and other PBMs.Methods: A systematic search of nine bibliographic databases will be conducted using a search strategy developed in consultation with a librarian. Studies comparing health responses, measurement properties including reliability, validity, and responsiveness across different MoAs for the EQ-5D or otherPBMs will be included. Two reviewers will independently screen titles/abstracts and full texts, and disagreements will be resolved through team discussion. Information on study characteristics, response distributions, key COSMIN measurement properties across different MoAs of EQ-5D and other PBMs will be extracted in duplicate using a pretested form. Following COSMIN guidelines, two reviewers will independently evaluate the measurement properties of the PBMs and rate the quality of evidence for each instrument per MoA. The study protocol has been developed in accordance with PRISMA-P guidelines, and the final report will follow PRISMA-COSMIN standards.
