Measuring health status using a web-based implementation of EQ-5D : a multi-objective feasibility study Original objectives 1. Establishing the demographic characteristics of respondents who participate in a web-based health survey. 2. Quantifying the comparative costs/benefits and efficiency of alternative recruitment strategies 3. Comparing EQ-5D data obtained using web-based methods with corresponding information generated using traditional P&P methods 4. Measuring, on a within-respondent basis, the robustness of EQ-5D data generated by web-based methods compared with equivalent data generated by P&P method
