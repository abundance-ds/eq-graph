We conducted a cohort study in Fuzhou, China to assess the health and wellbeing of community-dwelling older adults in spring, summer, and autumn in 2023. Data collection was conducted in two residential areas. We performed analysis to assess the validity and responsiveness of EQ-5D-5L and EQ-HWB to the impact of heatwaves. We also performed a head-to-head comparison between EQ-5D_ and EQ-HWB and explored the demographic factors affecting the impact of heatwaves on older adults' heath-related quality of life.
