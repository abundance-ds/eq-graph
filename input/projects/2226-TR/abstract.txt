**Background and aim**: The EuroQol Toddler and Infant (EQ-TIPS) measure of HRQoL is currently an experimental measure which has undergone modifications and testing in a large multinational research project (EQ 365 and EQ1928). Results of the final testing of the EQ-TIPS-3L and 5L are expected at the end of 2025. Thereafter, the descriptive system will be finalised, and a dossier of evidence will be submitted to support its transition from experimental to approved status. Approved versions of any instrument require formal linguistic review and translation by translation agencies based on the appropriate forward and back translation (FT and BT), cognitive debriefing and proofreading. In a recent joint EuroQol Board and Executive meeting, the decision was made to prioritise office resources to move the EQ-HWB-9 to an approved version before focusing on the EQ-TIPS. To ensure that the EQ-TIPS is ready for launch and the timeline is not protracted, this project aims to support the translation of the definitive EQ-TIPS version, of the ten most requested language versions and reduce the associated burden on the office staff. A secondary aim is to support researcher-led translations. **Methods:** The ten most requested language versions (English - UK, USA, Australia, Canada; Spanish - Spain, USA; German, Simplified Chinese, French, Italian) have been identified by the Version Management Committee (VMC). An ISO-certified translation agency will be engaged to undertake formal translation of the finalised descriptive system of the EQ-TIPS-5L. In all instances, the EQ-TIPS-5L will be translated or linguistically validated and cognitively debriefed, and the EQ-TIPS-3L will be extracted. In each language, linguistic review or translation will cover the self-complete (SC) (digital and paper) and interviewer-administered (IA) versions. The initial translation will be for an English UK version, which will be used as the source version for the other nine languages. Following a gap analysis by the VMC-liaison available, translated language versions will undergo linguistic review without FT and BT, subject to there being no changes in the English source version, otherwise, full translation will be required. Cognitive debriefing will be undertaken with eight participants (caregivers of infants and toddlers of mixed ages, mixed gender and different socio-demographic strata and health conditions). VMC reviewers will support the translation process by reviewing reports from the translation agency at each stage of the translation. Researcher-led translations will continue to be supported for languages that are not available. Researchers will undertake translations using EQ translation guides and processes developed by the VMC. This involves FT and BT, CD and reporting on the findings, including populating templates. VMC reviewers will undertake a light review of the results of the researcher-led translations before approval for use by the VMC liaison. **Outcome:** Agency translations will be available for the EQ-TIPS-5L and EQ-TIPS-3L for the ten most requested language versions with two modes of administration (SC and IA). Each translation will be delivered for quality control and approval by the EQ office ahead of the launch of the instrument.
