We propose an online ECR thematic meeting to be held in conjunction with the EuroQol PhD Network in late September/early October 2026, timed strategically after the 2026 Plenary and before the return of the Academy meeting in 2027. This meeting aims to provide a dedicated platform for early career researchers (ECRs) to present and discuss their EuroQol-related research across five Working Group domains: Descriptive Systems, EQ-HWB, Population and Health Systems, Valuation, and Younger Populations.The meeting will adopt a structured format where ECR presenters share their work (including repurposed abstracts not accepted at the EQ Plenary 2026), followed by designated discussants from the relevant Working Group who provide constructive feedback and discussion points. This format has proven effective in online settings and fosters meaningful mentorship between senior EuroQol members and emerging researchers.Key objectives include: (1) showcasing ECR research spanning the breadth of EuroQol’s scientific agenda; (2) strengthening the pipeline of early career talent within the EuroQol network; (3) facilitating cross-Working Group dialogue and collaboration, including through the Buddy Program; and (4) coordinating with the PhD Network to maximize reach and impact. The meeting will be conducted online via Zoom to ensure global accessibility, including for researchers in low- and middle-income countries.We anticipate 80–100 attendees across two half-day sessions, featuring 12–16 ECR presentations, 4–6 senior discussants, and networking breakout rooms. The programme will include a plenary keynote on career development in health economics research, thematic parallel sessions aligned with WG priorities, and a collaborative session with the PhD Network Core Panel.
