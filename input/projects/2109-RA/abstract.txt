This research will assess the acceptability (data quality, distributional properties), reliability (test-retest, internal consistency), validity (convergent, divergent, known group, structural) and responsiveness of the EQ-5D-5L hearing bolt-on question(s). Research will be conducted alongside the NIHR-funded LUCIA trial in the UK. The EQ-5D-5L and candidate hearing bolt-on questions will be assessed at multiple time-points during the trial and be compared with the York Binaural Health Related Quality of Life measure, the Health Utilities Index mark 3, as well as a range of patient-reported hearing questionnaires and audiometric measures.
