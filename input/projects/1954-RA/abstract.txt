This project aimed to translate the EQ-HWB original (OG), EQ-HWB-9 OG, EQ-HWB Modified and EQ-HWB-9 Modified from English into the Dutch language. We followed the EQ-HWB translation guidelines developed by the EuroQol Research Foundation and were supported by the Version Management Committee (VMC). The steps of the translation process included forward translation, backward translation and cognitive debriefing interviews.
