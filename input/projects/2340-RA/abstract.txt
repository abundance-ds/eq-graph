The recent external review of the UK EQ-5D-5L valuation study identified that the Normality assumption made in the heteroscedastic Tobit models widely used to model cTTO in EQ-5D-5L valuation studies was not met; moreover this was also noted to be the case in other studies. Accordingly, the 20th RFP includes novel modelling approaches to cTTO data that can handle the asymmetric, heterogeneous, heteroscedastic and censored nature of this data as a current methodological need.We propose to use parametric survival models to fill this need. Specifically, defining indifference in the cTTO task as the event and disutility as the time, disutilities from cTTO data can be described as time-to-event data. There are well-developed parametric survival models that incorporate asymmetry and heteroscedasticity in addition to censoring; moreover, random effects can be added to accommodate heterogeneity. The proposed work will examine Weibull, Gompertz, and log-logistic survival models, and mixtures thereof. We anticipate that this approach will provide sufficient flexibility accommodate the asymmetry and heteroscedasticity present in cTTO data, thus permitting future valuation studies to produce more methodologically rigorous estimates.
