**Background:** The EQ-Health and Wellbeing (EQ-HWB-S) is a newly developed generic instrument that assesses the impacts of health, social care and being an informal carer on quality of life. It was developed to be brief and amenable for use in economic evaluations. The instrument is currently considered ‘experimental’ whilst evidence of its validity in future users is generated, and potential further modifications that have arisen in initial testing are considered.Sheffield Teaching Hospitals is a centre of excellence for neuromuscular (NM) patient care with a large group of NM patients. Facioscapulohumeral muscular dystrophy (FSHD) and Myasthenia Gravis (MG) are two rare neuromuscular conditions which have considerable health related quality of life impacts, particularly in areas of mobility, fatigue and relationships. It is anticipated that the EQ-HWB-S, which captures these domains, may be suitable to measure quality of life in these patients. **Methods:** This is a mixed methods study in which the validity and suitability of the EQ-HWB-S will be explored using both qualitative and quantitative methods. Cognitive debriefing interviews with patients and their carers will be undertaken in a clinical setting to explore their understanding of the EQ-HWB-S instrument, and how well they think it reflects relevant aspects of their health or carer related quality of life. This will include consideration of the proposed modifications to the EQ-HWB-S. The EQ-HWB-S and EQ-5D-5L will be included into the package of patient reported and clinician reported outcome measures collected during clinic visits for a 12 month period. This data will be analysed to assess the psychometric performance of the instruments, through for example, comparing the generic instruments (EQ-5D-5L and EQ-HWB-S) to condition specific measures and clinical outcomes.
