Dimension specific modules (DSMs) are ways to extend the EQ-5D core measure by adding items to each dimensions to capture a richer descriptive profile. The Deep Dive Pilot study previous set up a framework that conceptualizes the EQ-5D as a multi-level measure, with the top level at the utility level, EQ-5D items at the dimension level, and DSMs at the module level. A recently completed (cross-sectional) validation survey for the Work Disability Functional Assessment Battery (WD-FAB) included the EQ-5D-5L. The rich dataset includes the 400+ items of the WD-FAB item banks convering a large set of functional items covering a broad spectrum of severity levels. This presents an ideal opportunity to explore construction of DSMs for EQ-5D-5L core items. Additionally, the dataset will allow us to compare two different frameworks of health-related quality-of-life (HRQoL): the World Health Organization (WHO) International Classification of Functioning, Disability and Health (ICF) which is the basis for WD-FAB development, and a model suggested by Olsen et al which has been applied to the EQ-5D. The **overarching goal** of this project will be to inform approaches for developing DSMs by exploring the relationship of EQ-5D-5L items to the WD-FAB item banks. **Aim 1:** Explore the relationships of EQ-5D-5L items and the WD-FAB item banks using several EFA approaches, in order to identify items that can be structurally linked to the EQ-5D-5L. **Aim 2:** Assess the fit of two conceptual models of HRQoL using EQ-5D-5L and WD-FAB measures. **Aim 3:** Examine EQ-5D-5L item properties (using item response theory approaches) within the WD-FAB item banks to inform the coverage of EQ-5D-5L items and how EQ-5D items can be linked back to a larger item bank. Several exploratory factor analysis approaches will be used to clarify Aim 1, while structural equation modeling will be used to clarify the conceptual models for Aim 2. Item response theory will be used to examine EQ-5D-5L items in the context of relevant item banks. Individual EQ-5D-5L items will be explored with specific WD-FAB item banks: * EQ-5D Mobility item (MO) with the WD-FAB Basic Mobility item bank; * EQ-5D Self-Care item (SC) with WD-FAB Upper Body Function, Fine Motor Function and Communication & Communication item banks; * EQ-5D Usual Activity item (UA) with the WD-FAB four physical item banks as well as its Community & Cognition and Sociability& Resilience item banks; and * EQ-5D Anxiety/Depression item (AD) with the WD-FAB Mood & Emotions item bank. The results of this project will directly address aim 3 of the descriptive system working group’s call for explorative research into development of DSMs. Specifically, we will generate evidence for which sets of functional status items (WD-FAB) can be linked to EQ-5D items, and where overlaps may occur.
