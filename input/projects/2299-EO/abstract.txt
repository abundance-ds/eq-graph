I presented 3 posters at the ISPOR Europe 2025 Conference which was held between 9 and 12 November 2025, in Glasgow.My first study, “Assessing the Psychometric Performance of the Experimental EQ-TIPS (v2.0) in assessing the health-related quality of life of Stunted Children in Laos” [MSR41] assessed the known-groups validity and responsiveness of EQ-TIPS (v2.0) among 100 stunted children’s caregivers visiting emergency department in Laos. The second study, “Assessing known-groups and convergent validity of the EQ-5D-5L in assessing the health-related quality of life of individuals with obesity who have and have not received bariatric surgery” [MSR40] evaluated the known-groups and convergent validity of EQ-5D-5L among 82 patients with obesity who have not received bariatric surgery and 79 patients who have received bariatric surgery in Singapore. The third study, “Comparing Health-Related Quality of Life in Obese Patients With and Without Bariatric Surgery: A Cross-Sectional Study in a Multi-Ethnic Asian Population in Singapore” [PCR49] compared the health-related quality of life in a sample of obese patients who have and have not received bariatric surgery in Singapore using EQ-5D-5L and EQ-HWB-9.
