The visit will support three primary areas of work: (1) conducting psychometric analyses of the EQ-HWB and EQ-5D using an existing dataset collected during the COVID-19 pandemic; (2) exploring the EQ-DAPHNIE dataset, with a specific focus on outcomes related to long COVID; and (3) developing a qualitative study to investigate perceptions of quality of life and the content validity of the EQ-HWB among ethnic minority populations in China. Additionally, this visit will provide valuable hands-on experience in data collection through participation in an ongoing study involving older adults residing in social care facilities. The visit aims to enhance research skills, finalize ongoing studies, and initiate new projects related to the measurement of health-related quality of life (HRQoL), with a particular focus on the psychometric evaluation and cultural relevance of EQ instruments.
