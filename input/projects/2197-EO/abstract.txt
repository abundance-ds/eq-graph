This travel grant application seeks funding support to attend and present at the IHEA Congress 2025. The accepted presentation “Are generic preference-based measures valid for use in informal carers? A psychometric investigation“ is part of my EuroQol funded PhD project (1481-PHD).  My participation (oral presentation) in the congress is part of an organised session titled: “The Hidden Costs of Caring: Unseen Economic, Health, and Quality of Life Impacts on Informal Carers.” This is an opportunity to share the results of this study on the performance of the EQ-5D-5L in informal carers in Australia. This is the first study that evaluated the psychometric performance of the EQ-5D-5L in Australian informal carers, and also the first that evaluated the AQoL-8D performance.  This opportunity is a unique opportunity to share our findings and hear from researchers in the field of economics of informal care.
