These project are not research projects in the conventional meaning, with relevant research questions to be addressed by data, but – primarily faciliatory/supportive projects to enable the implementation of a digital survey system, assuming patient consent is given or asked for. The EuroQol grant supported implementation of digital survey & patient dashboard in Capacity2 & Defence studies, with data collection among cardiology patients as an expected follow up. EuroQol in koind support and funding was provided conditional on making sure that EuroQol was able to learn from this implementation project (technical aspect) and that the data would be made available to EuroQol researchers and other researchers for secondary use. The background of this study: Dutch sponsors (Medical Research Council, Heart Foundation) supported 2 multihospital studies on the presence of cardiovascular longcovid, its prediction and a clinical guideline for its approach. The EQ Board supported EQbased PROMs measurement in these studies as an 'add-on'. The so-called Capacity1 study was the founding study: a large multihospital cohort (patients from 2020) whose data (WHO CRF plus cardiovascular from EMR) were used anonymously. The Capacity2 study essentially uses the same dataformat, but since 2021 asks prospectively patients for consent, some additional tests, and the permission to send PROM questionnaires. The Defence study approaches all Capacity1 (retrospective) patients alive, and asks for consent for the same as Cap2.
