Dr Verstraete travelled to the University of Sheffield and ISPOR Barcelona to 1) analyse data and a draft manuscript on the multi-national content validity testing of the EQ-TIPS and 2) present EQ-TIPS work at ISPOR Europe. The research visit was fruitful as key changes to the EQ-TIPS descriptive system were discussed and could be finalised with public and patient engagement representatives and expert stakeholders. Version 3.0 of the EQ-TIPS descriptive system is currently being tested for psychometric validity.
