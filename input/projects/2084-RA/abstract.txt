This systematic review aimed to (i) identify the methods used to develop vision and hearing bolt-ons for the EQ-5D-3L and EQ-5D-5L, (ii) identify the wordings used in existing vision and hearing bolt-ons, (iii) categorise the populations in which these bolt-ons had been tested or applied, and (iv) summarise the available psychometric evidence. Following the protocol and methods described in 1703-RA, we conducted a systematic literature search in Embase, PubMed, and Web of Science in accordance with PRISMA guidelines. Findings for vision and hearing bolt-ons were summarised narratively using descriptive statistics and reported separately. Where available, psychometric properties of each bolt-on item were extracted and summarised descriptively. The review identified 21 publications relating to vision bolt-ons and eight publications relating to hearing bolt-ons.
