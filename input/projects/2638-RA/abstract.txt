Background: The EQ-TIPS is a standardized, proxy-reported experimental instrument developed by the EuroQol Group to assess health-related quality of life (HRQoL) in children aged 0–4 years. In the Netherlands, there is a lack of suitable HRQoL instruments for this age group, despite the growing importance of such measures in clinical research and economic evaluations. A Dutch version of the EQ-TIPS is needed to ensure linguistic and cultural appropriateness and to support future use in calculating quality-adjusted life years (QALYs).Aim: This study aims to translate the EQ-TIPS (5-level) from English into Dutch following EuroQol translation guidelines.Methods: The translation process includes forward translation by two Dutch-native translators, reconciliation into a first consensus version, and back translation by two English-native translators unaware of the original instrument. After producing a second consensus version, cognitive debriefing will be conducted with eight parents of children aged 0–4 years, ensuring variation in gender, education, and child health status. Findings will inform a pre-final version, followed by finalization, proofreading, and reporting.
