This project proposes a two-phase study to: (1) translate and culturally adapt the Experimental EQ-HWB-S (9-item) instrument into Estonian and Russian for use in Estonia, following the EuroQol translation methodology and ISPOR guidelines; and (2) conduct a preliminary feasibility assessment of the EQ-HWB-S within Estonia’s newly established regional integrated care service delivery networks (TERVIK).Estonia represents a strategically important setting for EQ-HWB development. The country is creating TERVIK networks which are county-level structures integrating primary healthcare, social care, and local hospital services with formal legislation anticipated in 2026. These networks are designed to manage populations with complex, multi-morbid conditions and coordinate care across health and social sectors—precisely the context for which the EQ-HWB-S was developed. Furthermore, Estonia’s substantial Russian-speaking minority (approximately 25–30% of the population) necessitates parallel language versions and offers a unique opportunity to examine cross-cultural measurement equivalence within a single-country setting.Phase 1 will produce officially approved Estonian and Russian (for Estonia) translations of the EQ-HWB-S Paper Self-Complete version through forward translation, back translation, cognitive debriefing (n=8 per language), and proofreading, with full Version Management Committee (VMC) oversight at each stage. Phase 2 will pilot the EQ-HWB-S in approximately 50 Estonian-speaking and 50 Russian-speaking respondents recruited through two TERVIK pilot sites, assessing feasibility, acceptability, response distribution, and preliminary psychometric properties. The project will deliver validated linguistic versions ready for larger-scale use, alongside evidence on the practical utility of the EQ-HWB-S in an integrated care context.
