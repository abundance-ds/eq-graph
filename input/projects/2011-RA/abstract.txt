Background: Several bolt-ons have been developed for the EQ-5D, including two developed initially for psoriasis: skin irritation (SI) and self-confidence (SC). These dimensions may be relevant in other skin conditions, such as alopecia areata (AA) and vitiligo, however currently there is no evidence of their content validity in these conditions. Objectives: To conduct qualitative research to explore the content validity of the EQ-5D-5L and the SI and SC bolt-ons in two chronic dermatological conditions: AA and vitiligo. Methods: A qualitative study will be conducted to explore the content validity of the SI and SC bolt-ons in AA and vitiligo; other bolt-ons will also be considered, based on the findings of a rapid literature review. Individual interviews will be conducted until data saturation is reached, which is expected to be when approximately 15 interviews with patients with each condition have been conducted. Recruitment will aim to include patients with different severities of each condition in the UK. In order to evaluate the content validity of the EQ-5D-5L with bolt-ons, the interviews will aim to assess the comprehensiveness, comprehensibility and relevance. Interviews will consist of concept elicitation questions to explore important areas of health-related quality of life in AA and vitiligo and cognitive interview questions about the relevance of and potential overlaps between dimensions and appropriateness of the wording and important health concepts not captured by the EQ-5D-5L and bolt-ons. Data will be analysed using thematic and content analysis.
