**Aims** To assess the presence and extent of Health Inequalities (HIs) measured through a.o. the EQ-5D in all Dutch patients (n>150.000) treated by hip&knee arthroplasty for osteoarthritis in 2016-2020. This project builds on preliminary evidence on best way to express those inequalities (EuroQol-Equimetrics (Haagsma, Janssen, Bonsel)) and the results on best econometric indicators (Bailey&Janssen 2020) under the HISIG umbrella. **Methods** Data is retrieved from the Dutch National Orthopedic Registry (LROI), with compulsory registration. Hip&knee arthroplasty patients report outcome data (EQ-5D, EQ-VAS, validated international disease-specific measures) before, shortly after, one-year after surgery. Recorded characteristics are age, gender, BMI, regional features, teaching vs. general/private hospital. Whilst maintaining privacy, SES is assigned to patients through their postal code. **Analyses** Simple and more advanced (standardized) table analysis, primarily on tLSS/index/VAS. Gini, Kakwani index (cf. advice Bailey&Janssen); Slope II & Relative II (cf. Equimetrics) Standard logistic/multivariable analysis; decomposition analysis (from Szende et al., 2014). Domain response analysis. If outcomes relate on the individual level: dedicated longitudinal analysis. Otherwise, cross-sectional analysis for inequities at 3 time moments. Above is repeated for disease-specific questionnaires (HOOS-PS, KOOS-PS, OHS/OKS). **Hypotheses** Better outcomes and higher surgical gain with higher SES levels; lower outcomes in teaching hospitals Larger HIs with EQ-5D LSS and index compared to VAS (adaptation). Results will be discussed among clinical experts to identify explanations. **Collaboration gains** Methods will be shared specifically with HISIG; other EQ-members may join with national datasets (appended project). APERSU (F. Al Sayah) group expressed willingness to accommodate a subsequent study on-site, using same methods/LROI.
