The project aims to assess the psychometric properties and performance of the best available items in the EQ-5D Bolt-on Toolbox and compare the exploratory fatigue items among adults diagnosed with haematological malignancies (HMs) across all major subtypes (leukaemias, lymphomas, and myelomas) irrespective of treatment intent or disease stage. Data will be collected through the Haematological Malignancy Research Network (HMRN), a large UK population-based registry that includes all newly and previously diagnosed HM cases within a catchment of over four million people across 14 hospitals, offering an unique opportunity to capture the full spectrum of diagnosed HM patients and obtain representative real-world data beyond trials or online panels. Online survey will be administered through HMRN’s established infrastructure at two time points, following its routine six-monthly assessment schedule. Analyses will assess distribution characteristic, convergent validity, discriminant validity, known-group validity, structure validity, exploratory power and responsiveness of EQ-5D-5L with bolt-ons, to provide evidence to support the development of the bolt-ons.
