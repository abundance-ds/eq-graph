The use of EQ-5D in health research has been steadily increasing in China, but many studies published in Chinese journals lack proper guidance in analyzing and reporting EQ-5D data. To address this gap, we undertook the translation of the book “Methods for Analysing and Reporting EQ-5D Data into Chinese”, making this essential resource accessible to Chinese-speaking researchers. The project was carried out by a team of bilingual EuroQol members, who collaborated closely to ensure the accuracy and consistency of the translation. Despite the team's expertise, challenges arose due to inconsistencies in language across sections drafted by different authors, which complicated the translation process. The use of AI tools, such as “Claude,” during the typesetting stage helped identify and correct grammatical errors and inconsistencies, significantly improving the final version. We also encountered terminological variations in the original text, such as "health state" vs. "health profile" and "EQ-5D index" vs. "EQ-5D value" vs. "EQ-5D utility," which prompted the suggestion of a standardized glossary for future work. Through this translation project, we aim to enhance the quality of EQ-5D research in China by providing researchers with clearer guidelines for analysis and reporting. Ultimately, this effort may improve the rigor and reliability of Chinese EQ-5D studies, fostering better health research and decision-making in the region.
