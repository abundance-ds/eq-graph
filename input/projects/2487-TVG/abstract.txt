This visit to King Saud University (KSU) in Saudi Arabia aimed to explore opportunities for advancing the use of EuroQol instruments in research, clinical practice, and health policy. I delivered a series of academic seminars, clinical engagements, and participated in strategic meetings with stakeholders from academia, healthcare systems, and policy organisations.I delivered two academic seminars and one clinical seminar to more than 100 participants, focusing on health-related quality of life measurement, the EQ-5D instruments, and their application in clinical and population health contexts. I observed strong interest from faculty, clinicians, and students, and a clear openness to further engaging with and adopting EuroQol instruments in both teaching and applied work.At the policy level, I met with national stakeholders, including the Center for Value in Health, where discussions focused on the growing role of PROMs in value-based healthcare, and the National Public Health Authority and the use of EQ-5D in national health surveys. I also participated in a multi-stakeholder panel on PROMs integration, which contributed to a draft white paper outlining key barriers and facilitators to implementation. These discussions further led to the initiation of a GCC-wide collaborative research proposal examining the role of EQ-5D and other PROMs in value assessment.The visit also helped catalyse several concrete research activities. I initiated a funded project on the translation and cultural adaptation of 18 EQ-5D bolt-ons for Saudi Arabia, and discussed future proposals on EQ-5D-Y-5L valuation and a national EQ-DAPHNIE survey. Overall, I consider the visit to have laid a strong foundation for sustained collaboration, capacity building, and the broader expansion of EuroQol research and impact in the region.
