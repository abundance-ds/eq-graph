The EuroQol group was one of the sponsors of the UK PROMs conference for 2026. This was held in Sheffield and organised by Professor Jill Carlton and the SCHARR Outcomes research team. The UK PROMs conference brought together clinicians, patients, policymakers, and industry partners to share the latest developments in the field of patient-reported outcome measurement. https://sheffield.ac.uk/smph/events/10th-uk-patient-reported-outcome-measures-proms-research-conferenceThe conference included 27 oral presentations and 40 posters, and had 155 registered delegates. Delegates included academics, clinicians, patients, policymakers, and industry partners.
