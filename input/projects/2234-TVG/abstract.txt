The Health Economics Study Group (HESG) UK in Brighton was held between 30th June and 2nd July.  An educational session, "Introducing an EQ-5D-5L value set for the UK: practical and methodological considerations", was held on 30th June where the lead PI, Professor Donna Rowen presented findings from the study. In addition, other study stakeholders including a representative from NICE and separately from industry presented their views. Finally, a non-study researcher presented a different view. The session was well attended and offered the opportunity for questions and answers related to the new value set. This enabled knowledge transfer in the session and in subsequent discussions outside the session. There were also other sessions focused on EQ-5D and EQ Health and Wellbeing. I chaired one session focused on severe mental health that used EQ-5D and I attended and contributed to the session on the EQ-HWB and application of Q methodology in a mixed population from China. It was also valuable to attend sessions on valuation: a) analysing DCE data, b) valuing condition specific measures, c) adolescents undertaking valuation.
