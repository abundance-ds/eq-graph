Background: he EuroQol Toddler and Infant Populations (EQ-TIPS-5L) instrument is a key advance in assessing health-related quality of life (HRQoL) in children aged 0–4 years. Its use in economic evaluation is limited by the absence of a validated valuation protocol and value set. Proxy valuation by parents or caregivers raises methodological, cognitive, and emotional challenges, underscoring the need for qualitative groundwork. This study aims to investigate the feasibility of applying DCE latent scale methodology to EQ-TIPS-5L valuation, examining how caregivers and general public respondents interpret dimensions, engage with choice tasks, and respond to alternative presentation strategies.Methods: We will conduct a qualitative, exploratory, multi-country study in the United Kingdom and Singapore, involving 30–40 participants (15–20 per site). The UK sample will focus on caregivers, whilst Singapore will include both caregivers and general public respondents to enable exploratory comparison of perspectives. Using a phenomenological approach, in-depth semi-structured interviews will combine cognitive debriefing, think-aloud techniques, and eight mock DCE tasks employing level overlap methodology to reduce cognitive burden. Cross-national comparison will assess both universal and context-specific influences on task interpretation and valuation.Implications and Future Directions: This study will generate critical qualitative evidence to inform a robust, internationally applicable valuation protocol for EQ-TIPS-5L.
