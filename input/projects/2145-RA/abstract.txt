Background: The EuroQol Data for Assessment of Population Health Needs and Instrument Evaluation (EQ-DAPHNIE) project aims to establish standardized, representative HRQoL datasets across diverse populations. Egypt, the most populous country in the Middle East and North Africa (MENA) region, presents a unique opportunity for inclusion in the EQ-DAPHNIE initiative. With over 106 million residents, significant disparities in healthcare access, and a high burden of noncommunicable diseases (NCDs), Egypt’s participation will enhance the project’s global representativeness and provide valuable insights into population health needs.Aim: This study aims to generate high-quality Health Related Quality of Life (HRQoL) data from the general adult population in Egypt to facilitate cross-country comparisons and contribute to EuroQol research.Methods: This longitudinal study will employ web-based self-administered surveys and face-to-face interviews to ensure inclusivity, particularly among elderly and illiterate populations. A quota sampling strategy will be applied to achieve national representativeness based on age, sex, and geographic distribution (urban or rural). The target sample size is 4,500 adults, with at least 500 face-to-face interviews conducted by trained interviewers. The survey will include 8 standardized HRQoL instruments namely, the EQ-5D-5L,  EQ Health and Wellbeing instrument-short (EQ-HWB-S), four EQ bolt-ons, Patient-Reported Outcomes Measurement Information System-10 (PROMIS-10), PROMIS-sleep disturbance short form, World Health Organization 5-items (WHO-5), Patient Health Questionnaire (PHQ-2), and 2-item Generalized Anxiety Disorder questionnaire (GAD-2) along with socio-demographic and health-related questions, and response validation measures. A representative subset (N=1000) of the initial sample will be followed up after 6 months to offer additional insights on changes over time.
