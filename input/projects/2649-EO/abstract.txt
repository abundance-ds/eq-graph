This grant proposal requests funding to support the dissemination of research findings through an oral presentation and a poster presentation at the ISOQOL 2026 Conference, scheduled for 18–21 October 2026 in Prague, Czech Republic. The study titled “Are Preference-Based Cancer-Specific Measures More Sensitive than Generic Measures? A Comparison of EQ-5D-5L, SF-6Dv2, FACT-8D and QLU-C10D in Gastrointestinal Cancers”  has been accepted as an oral brief presentation. This study compared the psychometric performance of the generic EQ-5D-5L and SF-6Dv2 with the cancer-specific FACT-8D and QLU-C10D in patients with gastric, pancreatic, and colorectal cancers. Findings suggest that generic measures, especially EQ-5D-5L, performed better than or comparably to cancer-specific measures, although performance varied by cancer type. A second study, titled “Construct Validity and Responsiveness of the EQ-HWB-9 in Patients with Gastric, Pancreatic, and Colorectal Cancer,” has been accepted as an in-person poster presentation. This study demonstrated that the EQ-HWB-9 shows sufficient construct validity and responsiveness in gastrointestinal cancer populations. Both studies are conducted under the EuroQol-funded project [2120-RA], “Head-to-Head Comparison of EQ-5D-5L and EQ-HWB-S in Gastrointestinal Cancer Patients.”  The project received a small grant of €24,834, which covered personnel costs only and did not include any budget for dissemination activities. The two manuscripts have been submitted to Value in Health and Health and Quality of Life Outcomes, where they have received encouraging feedback and recognition from reviewers. Recognising the importance of these findings, we seek support to present them to a broader international audience at ISOQOL 2026.
