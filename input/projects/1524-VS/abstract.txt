The aim of this study was to generate a new UK value set for the EQ-5D-5L using a representative sample of the UK general public. In total 1200 interviews were conducted, of which 1102 were conducted via videoconference using Zoom and 98 interviews were undertaken in-person. The sample of 1200 participants were representative of the UK population for age, sex, ethnicity and socioeconomic group as set according to quota groups. The sample is also proportionally representative for the four nations. Participants were interviewed using the time trade-off technique to elicit preferences for EQ-5D-5L health states. The data was modelled to generate utility values for every health state defined by the EQ-5D-5L. The study has benefitted from governance from a study Steering Group, with input from an EQ-VT support team from the EuroQol Group, a Quality Control team independent of the study team, advice from the EuroQol Valuation Working Group, and patient and public involvement.
