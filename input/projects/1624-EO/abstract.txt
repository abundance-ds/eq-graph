On 30th May 2023, Drs Nick Bansback and Nan Luo conducted a workshop at the National University of Singapore on using the EQ-5D as a PROM for improving clinical and shared decision-making. The workshop was designed based on feedback from a number of clinical groups in Singapore who have been collecting the EQ-5D and interested in how they can use the results more to improve patient centred and value based care. The workshop was split into 3 sections. The first section, led by Nan described what PROMs are and how to choose a PROM for different clinical use. This focused on using the EQ-5D as a PROM but also described how condition specific PROMs could be used in combination. The second section, led by Nick then described how PROMs could be used for clinical management and shared decision making. A number of case studies were used, including how the EQ-5D has been used to create an individualized decision aid for knee replacement surgery. The last section provided information about how to obtain the EQ-5D, and how to use and analyze it. The workshop was attended by 55 clinicians, clinical researchers, graduate students, and industry reps from Singapore.
