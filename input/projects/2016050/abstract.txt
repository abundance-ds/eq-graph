Russian researchers (in common with other countries of the former Soviet Union) have relatively little significant experience in the design and use of standardized health status measurement systems. The development of HTA and health economics methods in the Russian Federation and elsewhere in neighbouring countries requires a cadre of suitably qualified health status measurement practitioners. The leading role of EQ-5D provides the ideal basis for creating such a domestic, national resource. An entry-level workshop can provide the catalyst to encourage the adoption of EQ-5D technologies.
