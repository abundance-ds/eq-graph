The funded activity was a two-hour pre-conference session at the Health Economists’ Study Group (HESG) summer meeting in Brighton on 30 June 2025. The session, titled “Introducing an EQ-5D-5L Value Set for the UK”, aimed to disseminate early findings from the UK EQ-5D-5L valuation study and support its adoption by stakeholders. The event brought together researchers to discuss methodological considerations, governance processes, and implications for health technology assessment.Presentations covered the study’s objectives, design, and preliminary results, including the generation of a new UK value set based on preferences of 1,200 members of the public. Speakers addressed NICE’s perspective on implementation, industry concerns about predictability and impact on decision-making, and future methodological developments in health valuation. The session concluded with a panel discussion to encourage engagement and address questions from attendees. Overall, the event successfully informed participants about the forthcoming value set and fostered dialogue on its adoption and use in policy and practice.
