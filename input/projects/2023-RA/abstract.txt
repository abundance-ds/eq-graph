Study 1: Quantitative comparison of DCE with duration and EQ-VT in EQ-5D-Y-5L in Canada Following the methodology used in the Netherlands, three samples will be collected: 1) a sample of n=1000 adult respondents that complete an online survey, comprising of 15 DCE with duration (“split triplets”); 2) a sample of n=500 adult respondents that are interviewed in a personal interview, with each respondent completing 10 cTTO tasks; 3) a sample of n=1000 respondents that complete an online survey, comprising of 15 latent scale DCE pairs. All tasked will be framed from a 10-year-old child’s perspective. The DCE duration data will be analyzed using a mixed logit model with an exponential discount rate. The latent scale DCE data will be analyzed using a mixed logit model. Predicted latent scale DCE values will be anchored on the QALY scale using a mapping onto the mean observed cTTO values of each state included in the cTTO design.  Study 2: Qualitative think aloud interview study with adults (n = 20) and adolescents (n = 10) To obtain additional insight into the trade-offs made in DCE with duration split triplet vs cTTO, a qualitative study using Think Aloud cognitive interviews will be conducted among a separate sample of n=20 adults and n=10 adolescents in Canada. During the interview, adults will complete 3 cTTO and 3 DCE with duration tasks from their own perspective, and then the same tasks from a 10-year-old child’s perspective. Adolescents will complete the same tasks from their own perspective only. All participants will be instructed to verbalize their thought process. All interviews will be audio recorded and transcribed verbatim. The data will be analyzed using thematic analysis.
