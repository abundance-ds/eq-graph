This project successfully disseminated new evidence on the interpretation, responsiveness, and cross-regional comparability of EQ-5D outcomes at the ISPOR Europe 2025 conference in Glasgow, United Kingdom. Two presentations were delivered. The first, an oral podium presentation titled "Exploring Variability in EQ VAS Scores: A Systematic Review and Meta-regression of Population Health Studies", identified that comorbidity prevalence and regional differences are key drivers of variability in EQ VAS scores. This has implications for cross-country comparisons and health system benchmarking. The second, a poster presentation titled "Differential Responsiveness and Minimally Important Difference of EQ-5D: Truth or Artefact?", presented results from a spine surgery registry study. This analysis highlighted significant methodological challenges in estimating Minimally Important Differences (MIDs), including the impact of response shift and baseline severity effects. The project engaged with health economists, clinicians, and policy-makers to discuss how these methodological advances can strengthen the application of EQ-5D in value-based healthcare and global health policy.
