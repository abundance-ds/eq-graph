This project aims to develop the first nationally representative EQ-5D-5L value set for Türkiye by applying the standardized EuroQol Valuation Technology (EQ-VT) protocol. Although the EQ-5D-5L instrument is widely used in health technology assessment (HTA), cost-effectiveness analysis, and health policy design globally, Türkiye currently lacks a locally validated value set, which undermines the relevance of utility-based evaluations in national decision-making. To address this gap, we plan a cross-sectional valuation study targeting a nationally representative sample of 1,000 adults. Data collection will be carried out in seven geographical regions of Türkiye, each represented by its most populous province to ensure regional diversity and operational feasibility. Two trained interviewers will be assigned to each region (14 in total), with each interviewer expected to complete approximately 70–75 interviews. Our aim is to obtain complete and high-quality data from 1,000 adult respondents. The EQ-VT protocol will be used, applying both composite time trade-off (cTTO) and discrete choice experiment (DCE) methods. Data will undergo rigorous quality control aligned with EuroQol standards and analyzed using models that correct for heteroskedasticity, including Tobit regression and hybrid modeling techniques that integrate cTTO and DCE data. The resulting Turkish value set will serve as a robust, culturally relevant foundation for HTA, reimbursement decisions, and public health planning. Additionally, it will enable Türkiye’s formal integration into the international EuroQol data network, facilitating cross-national comparability and collaboration. All project outputs, including value sets, documentation, and training materials, will be openly accessible to promote transparency, reproducibility, and widespread use.
