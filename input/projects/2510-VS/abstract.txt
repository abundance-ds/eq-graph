The EQ-5D-Y-3L is a paediatric health-related quality-of-life instrument designed for children and adolescents aged 8–15 years. A national value set is required to compute quality-adjusted life-years (QALYs) for economic evaluations of healthcare interventions targeting younger populations in South Korea. This study will follow the international standardised protocol for EQ-5D-Y-3L valuation studies. Preferences will be elicited from a representative sample of the adult general population using two methods: (1) an online discrete choice experiment (DCE) in which 1,000 respondents each complete 15 paired comparisons, and (2) composite time trade-off (cTTO) interviews conducted with 200 respondents, each completing 10 cTTO tasks. In both tasks, respondents will be asked to consider what they prefer for a hypothetical 10-year-old child. DCE data will be analysed using mixed-logit models, and the resulting latent-scale values will be anchored to the QALY scale (0 = dead, 1 = full health) usinga mapping approach that links predicted DCE values to observed mean cTTO values across multiple health states. The resulting value set will enable cost-utility analyses of paediatric healthcare interventions in South Korea and will contribute to the growing body of international EQ-5D-Y-3L value sets.
