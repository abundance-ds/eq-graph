This proposal is a response to the current RPF with regard to valuation research for EQ-TIPS. We propose to develop a lag- time TTO (lTTO) task for valuing EQ-TIPS health states and test its feasibility and performance. We choose to study TTO because it is a hallmark valuation method of EuroQol and it is well accepted by health technology assessment bodies. We think TTO should not be denied without any empirical testing in EQ-TIPS valuation research. The aim of this proposal is to test the feasibility of a lTTO in terms of interviewers' and respondents’ feedback on the TTO valuation tasks, as well as the characteristics of the TTO values. We will conduct a low-cost, 2-phased mixed-methods study. In the first phase, we will use qualitative interviews to evaluate and refine a prototype lTTO design. In the second phase, we will interview 120 members of the general public to assess the feasibility and performance of the lTTO for the valuation of EQ-TIPS health states. This study will provide the first evidence on the feasibility and performance of TTO for valuation of EQ-TIPS. The findings will be valuable to inform the final decision about the most appropriate valuation methods for this new EuroQol instrument.
