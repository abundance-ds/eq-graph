I delivered a podium presentation and a poster presentation at the ISPOR Europe 2024 Conference held between 17 and 20 November 2024, in Barcelona, Spain. My first study, “EQ-5D-Y in Children With Asthma or Eczema: Does Perspective Matter in Proxy-Reported Outcomes Assessment of Pediatric Populations?” was identified as Top 5% Finalist for the ISPOR Europe 2024 Research Presentation Awards. The podium presentation has won the best student podium research presentations award. This study evaluates the measurement properties of two different proxy versions (proxy-proxy and proxy-patient versions) of the EQ-5D-Y-3L and EQ-5D-Y-5L, as reported by caregivers or legal guardians of pediatric patients with asthma or eczema. The project is partially funded by the EuroQol Research Foundation (EQ Project 188-2020RA; total project value: 22,120 euros [without a budget for conference]), and preliminary findings were presented at the EQ Early Career Meeting 2022. My second study, “Is a Longer Recall Period Better? A Study of EQ-5D-5L With a “1-Week” Recall Period in the General Population” [MSR31], compared the psychometric properties of two EQ-5D-5L versions differing in recall period (today vs. 1 week) in a Singaporean general population aged 15 and above (n=592) who participated in a nationwide household survey. This project is not funded by EuroQol.
