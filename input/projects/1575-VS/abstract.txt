Background: Measures of health-related quality of life require country-specific value sets to inform cost-utility analyses. Although Polish value sets exist for adult EQ-5D instruments, no value set is available for the EQ-5D-Y-3L.Aim: To estimate the Polish EQ-5D-Y-3L value set and examine methodological issues related to composite time trade-off (cTTO) valuation.Methods: Preferences were elicited from adults in Poland considering EQ-5D-Y-3L health states for a hypothetical 10-year-old child. Standard cTTO interviews were conducted with 204 respondents, and DCE survey data were collected from 1,185 respondents. An additional experimental cTTO arm tested a modified procedure omitting the comparison with immediate death. The final value set was estimated using a Bayesian model combining cTTO and DCE data, accounting for left-censoring at −1, repeated observations, preference heterogeneity, heteroscedasticity, response granularity, and non-normality of cTTO values.Results: The selected hybrid model without a constant showed satisfactory convergence and face validity. Pain/discomfort was the most important dimension, with a level 3 disutility of 0.725, followed by anxiety/depression, while self-care had the smallest impact. Level 2 contributed substantially less than level 3 across all dimensions. The utility of the worst health state, 33333, was −0.443. The experimental cTTO arm improved distributional properties for worse-than-dead states but was not used in the final value set for consistency with earlier EQ-5D-3L/5L value sets.Conclusion: This study provides the EQ-5D-Y-3L value set for Poland, complementing existing adult EQ-5D value sets and enabling cost-utility analyses in paediatric populations. The findings also support methodological refinements in future child health valuation studies.
