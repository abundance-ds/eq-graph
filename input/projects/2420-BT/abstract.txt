This cross-sectional qualitative study aims to evaluate the content validity of the best available cognition bolt-on (Toolbox v1) and two related exploratory cognition bolt-ons on thinking and memory (Toolbox v1.5) to the EQ-5D-5L health-related quality of life (HRQoL) instrument in the context of dementia care. Interviews will be conducted with people living with mild to moderate dementia, and informal caregivers and healthcare professionals of people living with mild to severe dementia. Participants will engage in concept elicitation and cognitive debriefing while completing the EQ-5D-5L, cognition bolt-ons, ASCOT, and DEMQOL instruments using think-aloud. The study will assess the relevance, comprehensibility, and comprehensiveness of the cognition bolt-on.  Thematic analysis will be applied to interview transcripts. This study uniquely incorporates perspectives from both individuals living with dementia (community-dwelling and institutionalized) and their (formal and informal) proxies to ensure the bolt-on reflects diverse lived experiences and professional insights. Findings will contribute towards the validation of the Toolbox’s cognition bolt-ons and contribute to the item’s dossier with dementia-and caregiving-specific findings.
