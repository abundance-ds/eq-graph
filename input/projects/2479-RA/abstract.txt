This study aimed to translate the 31 items of the Experimental EQ-5D Bolt-on Toolbox (versions EV1.0 and EV1.5) into Polish for use in Poland, in accordance with the EuroQol Group Experimental EQ-5D Bolt-on Toolbox Translation Guidelines (October 2025). An additional experimental item within the FATIGUE domain (“Persistent Fatigue”) was also translated. The resulting Polish version is intended for use in the EQ-DAPHNIE survey planned in Poland in Q1–Q2 2026 and for future methodological research involving the experimental EQ-5D Bolt-on Toolbox.The translation process followed the official EuroQol methodology and included two independent forward translations, two independent backward translations, Cognitive Debriefing (CD), and linguistic proofreading. Four bilingual translators fluent in English and Polish participated in the forward and backward translation stages. The translation subsequently underwent a VMC light review process.Cognitive Debriefing interviews were conducted face-to-face by a single interviewer in February and March 2026 with eight respondents recruited from the general population (4 women, 4 men; aged 20–74 years). The sample was heterogeneous with regard to age, education, and occupational background. All participants were native Polish speakers residing in Warsaw. Interviews were conducted in quiet home environments and lasted approximately three hours on average due to the extensive questionnaire burden. Respondents completed the EQ-5D-5L, both versions of the Experimental EQ-5D Bolt-on Toolbox, the additional fatigue item, and the EQ VAS.The CD process identified seven linguistic issues related to the Polish translation. One inconsistency identified in the FATIGUE items was corrected in the third consensus version, while the remaining issues were resolved in consultation with a Polish language specialist. The final Polish translation demonstrated good comprehensibility and conceptual equivalence with the source version.
