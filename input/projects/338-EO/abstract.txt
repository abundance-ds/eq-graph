Aims: To expand on the gains of the first African Academy Meeting in 2020 in building capacity in the field of measurement of HRQoL, stimulating interest in the continued and expanding use of the EQ-5D and creating opportunities for researchers in Africa. (Long term) Outcomes: : Greater utilization of the EQ-5D as a patient-reported outcome (PRO) measure across different countries and contexts; improved methodology, analysis and reporting of studies using the EQ-5D; establishment of researcher networks within and between countries; possible interest in doing national or language valuation studies; greater attention to methodological research about EQ-5D; and an increased interest in Health Technology Assessment (HTA) and Cost Utility Analysis (CUA) by health economists and policy makers.
