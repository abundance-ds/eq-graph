This proposal seeks support to disseminate findings at ISPOR Europe 2025 on country-related differential item functioning (DIF) in the EQ-5D-5L. I presented a poster titled “Country-related differential item functioning in the EQ-5D-5L: Insights from the EuroQol Data Assessment of Population Health Needs and Instrument Evaluation (EQ-DAPHNIE)”. This work used the EQ-DAPHNIE dataset and a logistic regression framework to evaluate country-related DIF in the EQ-5D-5L. The analysis showed minimal DIF among countries sharing the same or similar languages but identified meaningful DIF across countries with different linguistic and cultural contexts, particularly in the mobility, self-care, and usual activities dimensions. These findings enhance understanding of how contextual factors may affect the comparability of EQ-5D outcomes and guide the planned analyses examining DIF across other instruments in the EQ-DAPHNIE dataset, including EQ-HWB and PROMIS-10. The feedback received from HTA bodies, researchers, industry stakeholders, and EuroQol members will further inform the next phase of multi-instrument DIF analyses. In addition, I delivered a podium presentation on behalf of my colleague Thao Nguyen on “Comparative Performance of the EQ-5D-5L and PROMIS-10 in the Elderly: Data From the EQ-DAPHNIE Project in Five Countries”  further contributing to the dissemination of research on instrument performance and applications of the EQ-DAPHNIE dataset. Overall, this travel support will foster knowledge exchange, international collaboration, visibility of research on EQ-5D instruments, and reflection on next research steps.
