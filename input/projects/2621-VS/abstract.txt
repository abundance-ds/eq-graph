Estonia does not have a national EQ-5D value set for neither the 3L nor 5L version of the instrument, despite the EQ-5D-3L being included in the Estonian Survey on Health Behaviour among Adult Population (HBEP) since 2002. This absence represents one of the last remaining gaps in Europe and limits Estonia’s ability to conduct locally relevant cost-utility analyses for health technology assessment, develop valid population norms, and support evidence-based health policy. The proposed study aims to develop the first EQ-5D-5L value set for Estonia using a discrete choice experiment with duration (DCEd).The DCEd component will adopt the methodology established by the DCE duration valuation protocol, as approved recently by EuroQol’s Executive Committee, employing 15 non-randomized split-triplet choice tasks plus 8 supplemental pairwise anchoring tasks administered via an online panel to 1,000 adults representative of the Estonian population. The study will leverage the existing survey infrastructure at National Institute for Health Development in Estonia, with sample frame for the DCEd study being drawn from population registry. Data will be modelled using mixed logit with exponential discounting (DCE). The resulting value set will enable valid cost-utility analyses, support the development of Estonian EQ-5D-5L population norms, and position Estonia at the forefront of EQ-5D valuation methodology by being among the first countries to use the emerging stand-alone DCE with duration protocol.
