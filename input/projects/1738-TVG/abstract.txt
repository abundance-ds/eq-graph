A team of EuroQol researchers from National University of Singapore including Nan Luo, as the mentor, and three PhD students including Rachel Tan, Jia Jia Lee and Meixia Liao visited Canada from to 15 to 25 Oct in 2023. The team first reached Calgary to attend the APERSU 7th end-user meeting on 16-17 Oct and then the ISOQOL 30th annual conference on 18-21 Oct. Several EuroQol studies were presented by the students in the ISOQOL conference. Afterwards, the team travelled to Edmonton to visit the APERSU office on 23-24 Oct. During the visit, the team shared their studies and discussed research collaborations with Jeff Johnson, Fatima Al Salah and the APERSU team.
