The EuroQol Toddler and Infant Populations (EQ-TIPS) is a generic measure of health-related quality of life (HRQoL) developed for children aged 0–3 years. Designed to complement the EQ-5D instruments for adult and youth populations, EQ-TIPS contributes to a comprehensive measurement system across all life stages. Argentina has actively participated in the development and refinement of EQ-TIPS, contributing to its translation, adaptation, and validation. In 2024, Argentina took part in a cognitive debriefing study to assess the instrument’s face and content validity. The results of that study led to modifications in the descriptive system, item wording, and response scale labels, which were integrated into version 3.0 of the instrument. This project reports on the Spanish translation of EQ-TIPS version 3.0 (including 3L and 5L versions) for use in Argentina. These translated versions will be employed in upcoming psychometric validation studies both in Argentina and internationally.
