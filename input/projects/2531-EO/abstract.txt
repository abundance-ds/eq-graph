This project successfully disseminated six EQ-5D research studies at ISPOR 2026, held 17–20 May 2026 at the Pennsylvania Convention Center, Philadelphia, PA, USA. All six abstracts were accepted as poster presentations and were presented in person, collectively advancing the evidence base for EQ-5D measurement, valuation, and application across population health, clinical outcomes assessment, health economic evaluation, and valuation methodology. Two presenters – Dr Cheng Ling Jie (Jeremy), EuroQol Group member, and Mr Xun Li (Ethan), researcher on EuroQol-funded project 2078-RA – shared presenting responsibilities to cover two pairs of overlapping poster sessions. The studies addressed: (1) declining population health and widening socioeconomic inequalities in a multi-ethnic Asian population using nationally representative EQ-5D-5L data (EPH179); (2) factors associated with preoperative EQ-5D outcomes among 1,194 patients undergoing degenerative lumbar spine surgery (EPH106); (3) the lack of standardisation in economic evaluations of hypertensive disorders of pregnancy and the need for EQ-5D-based QALY estimation (EE173); (4) a systematic review of 138 value sets across six multi-attribute utility instruments (EPH226); (5) a methodological review of the evolution of EQ-5D-5L valuation practices and lessons from the 3L (MSR230); and (6) modelling of health system capacity and measurement dispersion as predictors of cross-national variation in EQ-VT utility values (MSR178). The project engaged health economists, clinicians, HTA practitioners, and policy-makers across dedicated discussion periods, supporting priorities of the Populations and Health Systems Working Group and the Valuation Working Group. All six posters are to be published in Value in Health. The project was supported by a EuroQol Travel Grant of €4,200 towards conference-related costs (registration, travel, and accommodation) for the two presenters.
