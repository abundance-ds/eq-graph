The Scaling Factor Approach (SFA), which applies previously estimated EQ-5D-5L coefficients as constants along with a scaling factor (λ) to bolt-on valuation data, has emerged as a promising method for producing consistent and cost-effective bolt-on value sets. While SFA has demonstrated strong predictive accuracy in previous studies, its broader adoption and methodological refinement remains limited. This research program addresses these gaps through four interrelated aims. First, we will develop a suite of user-friendly R functions to implement the SFA, complete with documentation and example datasets for cTTO and DCE applications. Second, we will explore relaxing the SFA’s core assumption by allowing separate scaling factors for different EQ-5D dimensions. Third, we will use simulation studies to identify the minimum sample size required for bolt-on valuation studies, guided by performance thresholds set by an expert panel. Together, these aims will strengthen the methodological foundation of the SFA, support its practical application, and evaluate its robustness in existing data. The findings will inform the development of a potential bolt-on valuation protocol.
