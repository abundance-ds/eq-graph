The 2025 joint meeting of the EuroQol North American Regional Meeting and the Alberta PROMs and EQ-5D Research and Support Unit (APERSU) 10th Anniversary and End-User Meeting was held at the Malcolm Hotel in Canmore, Alberta. This milestone event convened a diverse group of stakeholders, including EuroQol members, APERSU staff and students, patient partners, representatives from the Alberta Government, Alberta Health Services, Primary Care Networks, and PROMs users from across Canada. The meeting celebrated a decade of APERSU’s role as an intermediary organization connecting the EuroQol Research Foundation with non-commercial users of EQ-5D, supporting licensing, education, training, implementation, and research to advance the use of patient-reported outcome measures (PROMs) in health systems.The two-day meeting brought together researchers, clinicians, policy leaders, patient partners, and international collaborators to explore innovations in EQ-5D and PROMs, with approximately 60 in-person and 10 virtual participants per day. Evaluation findings from 31 respondents demonstrated high overall satisfaction (mean 4.1/5), particularly among in-person attendees (4.3/5), with 98% indicating they were likely or very likely to attend future meetings. Most frequently cited topics of interest included the future role of EQ-5D and PROMs in decision-making, next-generation instruments and bolt-on dimensions, and implementation examples featuring patient engagement.Key strengths included strong participant engagement, appreciation for the breadth of expertise and topics, and positive feedback on interactive discussant-style poster sessions. Constructive feedback highlighted opportunities to strengthen policy- and system-level content, enhance poster session logistics, broaden clinical and patient representation, and increase time for discussion and networking. Looking ahead, participants identified priorities for APERSU’s next decade, including supporting policy and system-level PROMs use, enhancing patient-level applications, and expanding PROMs integration in primary care. Overall, the joint meeting both celebrated APERSU’s achievements and reinforced its leadership in fostering collaboration, innovation, and patient-centered PROMs use in Alberta and beyond.
