This Project provided a comparative analysis of HRQoL and its health-related and socio-demographic correlates among Ukrainian refugees and in general population. Using data from two representative population health surveys from 2024, the Project aimed to be one of the first to use EQ-5D-3L in the context of current refugee crisis in Europe. The two deliverables - submitted manuscript of academic article and conference paper -  reported significantly lower HRQoL among refugees compared their age and sex-matched peers from general population even after adjusting for socio-demographic and health-related covariates. Project also demonstrated higher prevalence of behavioral risk factors among refugees compared to general population (female only sub-analysis) with each risk factor decreasing the EQ-5D index value by 0.04 in both groups. These findings contribute to the understanding of health impact of recent refugee crisis in Europe, as well as contribute to knowledge on the use of the EQ-5D instrument among vulnerable groups.
