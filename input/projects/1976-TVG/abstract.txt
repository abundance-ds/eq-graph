This application is for a 5 week study visit to Canada. I will be primarily based in Vancouver, and hosted by David Whitehurst (Simon Fraser University), Nick Bansback and Stirling Bryan (both University of British Columbia). I will also visit other EuroQol members including Feng Xie (McMaster), Jeff Johnson and Fatima Al Sayah (University of Alberta). All have agreed to my visit This research visit will focus on developing collaborations and proposals, the following EuroQol activities will be worked on: - Developing EuroQol proposals focused on the recall period of EQ instruments, and testing exclusion criteria and data quality in preference elicitation studies (with Whitehurst) - Developing a proposal around the use of EQ-HWB data in routine outcome assessment (with Bansback) - Developing further links between Australian and Canadian institutions with strong EuroQol connections - Strengthening collaborations between the DSWG (which i chair) and the PHS WG (Bansback is a member) - Strengthening links between the DSWG and Exec (Xie is a member) - Working on EQ-DAPHNIE related projects (with Johnson and Al Sayah - Giving seminars on ongoing EuroQol related research
