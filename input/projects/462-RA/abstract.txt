This report presents the first EQ-5D 3L population norms for Chile, derived from the 2016-2017 National Health Survey. It explains that national health surveys are essential governmental tools to assess population health and guide policies, combining interviews and biomarkers. The study details the methodology of the Chilean survey, including sampling and data collection, and analyzes the results according to sociodemographic characteristics and chronic conditions. Finally, it discusses the implications of the findings, such as the low EQ-5D VAS score in Chile compared to other Latin American countries, suggesting future research on rurality, isolation, and the relationship between depression and pain/discomfort.
