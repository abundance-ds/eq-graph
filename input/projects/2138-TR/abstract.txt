Background With the EQ-TIPS descriptive system nearly finalized, the next step is to develop value sets. Given that existing valuation protocols require major adaptation for young children, mapping to the EQ-5D-Y is being explored as a viable alternative. Mapping is widely used when instruments share similar measurement properties and is the preferred strategy of both the Valuation and Youth Working Groups for EQ-TIPS value development. Objective This proposal aims to support value set development through mapping by collecting a dataset aligned with the final EQ-TIPS descriptive system. Methods An online cross-sectional survey will be conducted among parents of children aged 24 to 59 months with a chronic health condition confirmed by a healthcare professional. The survey will use infrastructure from the EQ-DAPHNIE project and include both the EQ-TIPS-5L and EQ-5D-Y-5L, presented in random order. The questionnaire will follow a modular format, with 4–5 modules (60–70 items), covering demographics, child health, family impact, and optional topics such as healthcare use or methodological enhancements. Participants will be recruited by Dynata in 10 countries where both instruments are available in a local language. Screening, consent, and quota management will ensure a balanced sample across severity and disease types. Data Handling and Quality Control The survey will be hosted by Maths in Health, with respondent IDs deleted after panel rewards are confirmed. As this is not a research project, only data cleaning will be performed, including attention checks, response consistency, and other quality indicators.
