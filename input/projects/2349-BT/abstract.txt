: The EQ-5D is one of the most widely used tools to measure health-related quality of life; however, some important aspects of health may not be fully captured by its five core dimensions. For people with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), breathing difficulties are a central concern. The EuroQol Group has therefore developed a “Breathing” bolt-on item, which may improve how well the EQ-5D reflects the lived experience of patients with COPD.This project will test the Breathing bolt-on using a rich longitudinal dataset of about 400 patients with COPD who were followed over time. We will examine whether the bolt-on provides new and useful information beyond the existing EQ-5D dimensions, whether it reflects differences in disease severity, and whether it is reliable and responsive to clinical changes over time.By applying established psychometric methods, we aim to show whether adding the Breathing dimension improves the measurement of health outcomes in COPD. The findings will directly support the EuroQol Group’s Bolt-On Toolbox initiative, which is developing guidance on when and how to use bolt-on dimensions. This unique secondary database of a large clinical trial in COPD is an opportunity to produce evidence of responsiveness, which is essential to demonstrate the value of the EQ-5D+B in clinical trials and registries where breathing is a critical outcome for conditions such as COPD, asthma, cystic fibrosis and in many rare disease such as spinal muscular atrophy.  This will help researchers, clinicians, and policymakers better capture the impact of treatments and disease progression.
