A dissemination seminar on the development of the EQ-5D-3L and EQ-5D-5L value sets (Project ID: 1996-VS), as well as the piloting of the EQ-5D-Y (Project ID: 1646-RA), was conducted in Bangladesh. The audience included representatives from the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, potential HTA bodies (e.g., the Directorate General of Health Services, and the Health Economics Unit, Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics), researchers and academicians, national and international development partners, members of the print and electronic media, public health advocates, and civil society representatives.During the seminar, presentations covered the methodology and process of the valuation survey, challenges encountered, the resulting EQ-5D-3L and EQ-5D-5L tariffs, the feasibility of the EQ-5D-Y, and their practical applicability for HTA in Bangladesh. A Q&A session was carried out, focusing on the usefulness of these value sets. To evaluate the impact of the seminar, pre- and post-event surveys assessing participants’ knowledge of the “value of health” and the EQ-5D instrument were conducted. In addition, print and electronic media and social media covered the dissemination session.
