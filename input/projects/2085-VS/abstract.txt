This study aims to develop a Japanese value set for the EQ-5D-Y-5L following the international common protocol established by the EuroQol group, while examining the consistency between discrete choice experiment (DCE) and composite time trade-off (cTTO) valuation methods. Building on our previous success in establishing the Japanese EQ-5D-Y-3L value set and population norms, this research addresses the need for expanded measurement capabilities in pediatric health utility assessment. The study will employ a dual methodology approach: (1) face-to-face computer-assisted personal interviews for cTTO data collection (N=1000) and (2) online surveys for DCE with duration (N=2500). Participants aged 20-79 years will be recruited through quota sampling by sex and age. Methods should be compared based on such models accounting for heterogeneity, as that is the golden standard.Our research team, with extensive experience in developing EQ instruments in Japan and strong connections to the Center for Outcomes Research and Economic Evaluation for Health (C2H), is well-positioned to contribute to the broader implementation of EQ-5D-Y-5L in Japanese healthcare decision-making. This study will provide valuable insights into the comparability of different valuation methods and support the integration of pediatric health utility measurements into Japan's health technology assessment framework.
