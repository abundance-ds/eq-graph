Previous studies explored and suggested that the addition of the bolt-on items would impose improvements in EQ-5D, but only the cognition bolt-on was systematically reviewed to date. The project aimed to review the development, application, psychometric properties, and valuation of the prevalent EQ-5D bolt-ons.  A systematic review following PRISMA 2020 was conducted. Searches on PubMed, Embase, and Web of Science, supplemented by citation tracking and manual Google Scholar and EuroQol conference searches, were used. Key study characteristics, bolt-on specifications, psychometric properties, and valuation evidence were summarized narratively.  The project uncovered the abundance of EQ-5D format bolt-on wordings (sleep=12; fatigue/energy=14; social relationship=23), demonstrating the popularity of EQ-5D bolt-on research. Findings on content validity confirmed the relevance of the bolt-ons with the EQ-5D core dimensions. In many cases, the bolt-ons reduced the ceiling of the EQ-5D, and enhanced the psychometric performance of EQ-5D in terms of exploratory power, informativity, and known-group validity. Despite progress, the evidence in qualitative development, test-retest reliability, responsiveness, differential item functioning, patient-proxy agreement and valuation was still limited. The systematic review of sleep, fatigue/energy and social relationship bolt-ons demonstrated the common lack of qualitative input, but the majority of existing psychometric evidence supported their added value to the EQ-5D in specific populations. Future mixed-methods bolt-on development and refinement studies are needed for bolt-on constructs.
