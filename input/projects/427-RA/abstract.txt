The notion of adding 'bolt-on' items to the EQ-5D has been discussed since its conception. However, there remains a lack of consensus on how bolt-ons should be developed and in what circumstances they ought to be used. This project aimed to develop a foundation for future bolt-on research by identifying key conceptual questions through a literature review, workshop, and survey.Following project initiation, significant strategic developments within EuroQol substantially altered the context for this work. Specifically, the development of the Bolt-on Toolbox concept and the inclusion of bolt-on research within EuroQol's Strategic Plan for 2024-2028 represented a major shift towards centrally coordinated bolt-on development and management. These organisational changed fundamentally expanded beyond the scope envisioned in the original project proposal.The project successfully completed a systematic literature review and developed a publicly accessible bibliographic database containing >60 research items. This database catalogues studies according to bolt-on dimensions, clinical contexts, EQ-5D instruments, research methods, countries, and funding sources. The review identified key conceptual areas requiring clarification: nomenclature, definitions, areas for future research, and the purpose and role of bolt-ons.The planned workshop and survey were not conducted, as the project stalled in early 2024 as sensitivities around bolt-on development prevented us from proceeding. Discussion between the research team, EuroQol Office, and the Executive Committee recognised that the strategic landscape had evolved significantly and completion of the project was not viable, resulting in its closure.
