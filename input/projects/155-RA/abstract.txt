The primary deliverables are the results from three secondary data analyses based on the fourth wave of the Survey of Health, Ageing, and Retirement in Europe (SHARE), as described in the study proposal. Major findings will be published in international (open access) journals and presented on international conferences and the EuroQol plenary meeting. Two manuscripts are currently submitted, and a third manuscript is being prepared. Based on the planned analyses, we investigated the measurement properties of the EQ-5D-3L in the (SHARE) population of elderly Europeans (I), developed country-specific EQ-5D-3L norms stratified by age-groups and gender for the elderly Europeans (II), studied health inequalities using single and combined indicators of socioeconomic status (SES) and their relative importance as determinants of health in the ageing European population (III).
