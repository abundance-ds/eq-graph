The EuroQol Toddler and Infant Populations (EQ-TIPS) was developed as a generic measure of Health Related Quality of Life for children aged 0 - 3 years. There is ongoing work to refine the descriptive system to ensure its international validity. The development of the EQ-TIPS aims to complement the adult and youth EQ-5D instruments allowing measurement and valuation of health across the lifespan. This proposal aims to test the psychometric properties of the EQ-TIPS-3L and EQ-TIPS-5L versions to inform any further changes. Caregivers of children, aged 0-3 years, will be recruited from Australia, Canada, Singapore and Germany. Children with and without health conditions will be included to ensure responses span the severity levels. Further consideration will be given to the inclusion of children across the age categories e.g., 0-6 months, 6-12 months and 12-36 months. Changes to the descriptive system will be informed by Classical Test Theory (Known groups validity, construct validity, test-retest reliability, responsiveness), Confirmatory Factor Analysis and Item Response Theory.
