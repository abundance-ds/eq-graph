Background: The current proposal describes a replication study to the project 1923-RA (“Comparison of DCE duration and EQ-VT in EQ-5D-Y-5L in the Netherlands”). Two replication studies were planned in the original proposal (1923-RA), and preliminary accepted by the Exec in June 2024. The VWG and YPWG are jointly working on a protocol for the valuation of the EQ-5D-Y-5L. In the context of valuations of the EQ-5D-Y-5L instrument, we envisage that either an expanded version of the existing EQ-5D-Y-3L valuation protocol will be used, or a protocol that relies on DCE with a duration attribute. However, it has never been tested whether these methods produce similar results when valuing paediatric utility instruments. This study aims to make such a comparison and to develop a value set for the EQ-5D-Y-5L. Methods: Three samples will be collected: 1) a sample of n=1000 respondents that complete an online survey, comprising of 15 DCE split triplets. 2) a sample of n=500 respondents that are interviewed in a personal interview, with each respondent completing 10 cTTO tasks. 3) a sample of n=1000 respondents that complete an online survey, comprising of 15 latent scale DCE pairs. The DCE duration data will be analysed using a mixed logit model with an exponential discount rate. The latent scale DCE data is analysed using a mixed logit model. Predicted latent scale DCE values are anchored on the QALY scale using a mapping onto the mean observed cTTO values of each state included in the cTTO design. A value set is selected based consistency and discussion with the YPWG and VWG.
