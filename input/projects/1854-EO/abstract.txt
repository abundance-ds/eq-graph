SEVQoL (SEVerity and Quality of Life, EQ no. 1340-RA) investigates whether people show a preference for prioritising the 'worse off' in hypothetical healthcare allocation scenarios, using individually calibrated EQ-5D-5L health state values. An abstract for the project's first article, "A Preference for the Better Off? A Discrete Choice Experiment Using Individually Calibrated EQ-5D-5L Health State Values", was accepted as a poster presentation for the Priorities 2024 conference in Bangkok on May 7.-11. hosted by The International Society for Priorities in Health. As there is no funding for the project beyond salaries for the PhD student, we apply for additional funding to cover 50% of the conference fee, travel and accommodation for Marius Torjusen, who is the first author and PhD student on the project.
