Health state valuation using discrete choice experiments (DCEs) is increasingly applied as an alternative to traditional methods such as the standard gamble (SG) and time trade-off (TTO). A critical step in DCE studies is anchoring relative health state utilities onto the 0–1 quality-adjusted life-year (QALY) scale by including a duration-of-life attribute. Current DCE duration designs typically assume linear time preferences, despite behavioural evidence showing that individuals often value life years non-linearly. Generating DCE duration designs that account for non-linear time preferences while incorporating duration anchoring is technically complex, and no standardized, open-source tool exists to facilitate this process. This limits the broader implementation of the DCE duration protocol.This project builds on an existing DCE duration design generation package developed for the 10-item Well-being (WiX) instrument, which will be generalized and extended to support multiple health state valuation instruments, including EQ-5D and EQ-HWB. Released as an open-source, publicly accessible tool, it will simplify a technically demanding process, improve methodological rigor, enhance the behavioural realism of QALY valuations, and support the wider health economics research community in conducting DCE duration studies accommodating non-linear time preferences.
