Background The application of the EQ-5D instrument for adults has rapidly increased in Bangladesh. However, currently, there are no local EQ-5D value set(s). Objective To develop EQ-5D value sets for both EQ-5D-3L and EQ-5D-5L in the adult population in Bangladesh. Methods The standard EuroQol valuation protocol (EQ-VT) will be used. A representative sample will be recruited, including adults aged 18 years and older from eight different administrative divisions in Bangladesh. The total sample size will be 1,000. The sample will be representative of the Bangladesh population with regards to age, gender, residence area and education levels. Data collection will be conducted by the research team through the ARK Foundation in Bangladesh with qualified interviewers trained by EuroQol scientists. Outputs Value sets of EQ-5D-3L and EQ-5D-5L, publications in international peer review scientific journals, conference proceedings and workshops, reports in English and Bangla.
