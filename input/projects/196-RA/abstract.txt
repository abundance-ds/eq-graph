Aim: This study assessed and compared the validity and responsiveness of the EQ-5D-3L and the EQ-5D-5L in patients with advanced melanoma. Methods: We used data from the nation-wide Dutch Melanoma Treatment Registry (DMTR), which contains data on patient and disease characteristics and PROMs, for approximately 1280 patients including EQ-5D-3L, EQ-5D-5L, and the Functional Assessment of Cancer Therapy for Melanoma (FACT-M). In order to assess the validity of the EQ-5D, we compared content and construct validity, and responsiveness using baseline data of patients that reported perfect health (ceiling-effect) in each of the two EQ-5D versions. Discriminatory performance was assessed using Shannon entropy. Responsiveness of both EQ-5D versions was assessed for health states progression-free and progressive disease and discriminant validity for known-groups (good/intermediate and poor prognosis). Construct validity of the EQ-5D-3L and EQ-5D-5L versions was assessed by the correlation between the FACT-M sub scores with the EQ-5D dimension score using Spearman’s correlation coefficients. Results: The EQ-5D-5L showed a lower ceiling effect compared to the EQ-5D-3L. Shannon entropy showed better discriminatory performance in 5L than in 3L across all dimensions. Both EQ-5D-versions were responsive to changes in health with higher utility scores in progression-free health than in progressive disease health states. Similarly, both EQ-5D-versions discriminated between favorable and poor prognosis. The EQ-5D-5L showed clearer relationships among EQ-5D and FACT-M dimensions than the EQ-5D-3L. Conclusion: Overall, the study showed that the EQ-5D-5L displayed better content and construct validity than the EQ-5D-3L in patients with advanced melanoma.
