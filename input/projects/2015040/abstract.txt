The propositionfor this project is that patients are not sufficiently informed of the outcomes of total knee arthroplasty (TKA)in order to make an appropriate decision on whether they desire surgery. We propose that by using routinely-collected Patient Reported Outcome Measures (PROMs) from patients who have considered undergoingTKA surgery(including those whohave and have not), we can better inform patients about what they can expect from surgery, leading to improved decisions. Since patients systematically overestimatebenefits, and underestimate harms, we hypothesize this will lead to patients with more moderate pain choosing to delay having surgery
