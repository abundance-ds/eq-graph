I visited the Universities of Auckland, Otago and Macquarie during the period 8th March to 18th April 2023. This visit was facilitated by Paula Lorgelly, Trudy Sullivan, Sarah Derrett and Yuanyuan Gu to whom I am grateful. Most of the time was spent at the University of Auckland, where I engaged with a number of staff members and explored opportunities for future collaborations on EQ-related topics (e.g. collecting and linking HRQoL data as part of national surveys).
