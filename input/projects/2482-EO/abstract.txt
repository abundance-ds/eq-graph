Introduction: Participation in the VI REBRATS Congress represents a strategic opportunity to disseminate EuroQol-supported research and strengthen the visibility and uptake of EQ-5D-Y instruments within the Brazilian health technology assessment (HTA) network. The REBRATS (Brazilian Network for the Evaluation of Health Technologies) and CONITEC (National Commission for the Incorporation of Technologies in the Unified Health System) play central roles in HTA decision-making, and presenting methodological advances on EQ-5D-Y valuation and validation will foster their integration into national economic evaluations and policy frameworks. Objectives and Methods: Five studies were presented in the VI REBRATS Congress, 1) the EQ-5D-Y-3L valuation study; 2) the qualitative arm of the EQ-5D-Y-3L valuation study to explore the valuation process from different perspectives (own-adult and child); 3) the qualitative arm of the EQ-5D-Y-3L valuation study to explore the valuation process from interviewers’ perspectives; 4) the study comparing the psychometric properties measurement of the EQ-5D-Y-3L, EQ-5D-Y-5L, and CHU9D in children and adolescents; and 5) the study comparing the health-related quality of life of children and adolescents with and without musculoskeletal pain. The valuation study included discrete choice experiment (DCE) and composite time trade-off (cTTO) interviews with 1,376 Brazilian adults valuing child health states. Qualitative analyses explored participants’ reasoning and interviewers’ experiences with the valuation process. Additionally, cross-sectional psychometric studies were conducted with 441 children and adolescents (aged 8-18 years) with and without musculoskeletal pain to examine construct validity, reliability, and known-group validity and to investigate the impact of musculoskeletal pain in children and adolescents. Results and Discussion: A Brazilian EQ-5D-Y-3L value set was successfully developed (utilities ranging from 1 to -0.0059), with pain/discomfort identified as the most influential dimension. The qualitative studies identified cognitive and emotional aspects influencing valuations. The EQ-5D-Y-3L, EQ-5D-Y-5L, and CHU9D showed poor-to-moderate reliability, but EQ-5D-Y-3L, EQ-5D-Y-5L demonstrated good construct and known-group validity. Together, these studies provide the first comprehensive evidence base for pediatric health-related quality of life (HRQoL) measurement and valuation in Brazil. Impact: Disseminating these findings at REBRATS congress will promote awareness of EQ-5D-Y methodologies, support HTA capacity building in Latin America, and align Brazil’s economic evaluation practices with international standards. This presentation directly contributes to EuroQol’s mission of advancing the global use and methodological development of EQ-5D-Y instruments for children and adolescents.
