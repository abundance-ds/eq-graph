The five-level EuroQol Toddler and Infant Populations (EQ-TIPS) is under development as a health-related quality of life (HR-QoL) measure for toddlers and infant populations, aged 0 to 36 months. Each of its seven items (and response options) can be transformed into an attribute with five levels for a health valuation study. However, it is unclear whether severity (i.e., order of the five levels) concords with preferences along each EQ-TIPS domain. Furthermore, the EuroQol Group lacks a method for EQ-TIPS valuation, particularly one that works well in small samples. The primary aim of this feasibility study was to assess EQ-TIPS attributes using DCE surveys. This methods research will address three questions critical for EQ-TIPS development: (1) Whether severity concords with preferences along each EQ-TIPS domain? (2) To what extent do the EQ-TIPS attributes influence preferences? (3) To compare the performance of kaizen tasks and paired comparisons for EQ-TIPS valuation
