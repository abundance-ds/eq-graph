A PhD project was completed to investigate the challenges in valuation of worse-than-dead EQ-5D health states and to explore methods that may improve the valuation of such health states. The project started with a study to investigate whether extending the lead time in the lead-time TTO tasks will mitigate the floor effects in negative TTO values in an EQ-VT study. A qualitative study of respondents participating in another EQ-VT study was conducted to understand why negative values were given in TTO tasks. Based on findings from these two studies, a new TTO variant was developed, tested, improved, and tested again. The new TTO variant was found to be able to better measure worse-than-dead EQ-5D health states compared to the composite TTO.
