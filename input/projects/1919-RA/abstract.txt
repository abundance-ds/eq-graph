This project aims to a) extend individually adaptive D-efficient DCEs with sign and monotonicity constraints to be able to elicit individualized preferences for the EQ-5D-5L, b) to estimate individual preferences for the EQ-5D-5L - with or without making use of the exact same sign and monotonicity constraints to increase statistical power in the estimations as well, and c) to provide internal validation of the preference estimates by asking respondents to reflect on summary graphs of their estimated preferences. The ultimate goal of this project is stand-alone individual-level preference estimation for the EQ-5D-5L; however, it should be emphasized that the same methods could also provide a valuable extension to the upcoming DCE with duration protocol, given that it would accomodate the estimation of heterogeneous time preferences and statistical identification of low-quality respondents in DCE valuation studies of larger instruments, such as the EQ-HWB.
