This research explores the light cultural adaptation/cognitive debriefing, and psychometric performance of the latest cognition bolt-ons version (as provided by the DSWG-BM) for the EQ-5D, as well as of the EQ-HWB-S, in an elderly nursing home population. The study will be set in LeDor VaDor, a leading institution in Buenos Aires, Argentina, known for its comprehensive care and robust academic involvement. Housing approximately 300 residents, LeDor VaDor offers a rich setting for deploying significant health-related research due to its diverse resident profile and advanced medical facilities. Our study will initially perform a light cultural adaptation/cognitive debriefing interviews to adapt the cognition bolt-ons (and produce the changes needed to have an interviewer and a proxy -version 1- EQ-HWB-S Argentine versions), and assess the construct validity, and psychometric comparability of the EQ-HWB-S and cognition bolt-ons alongside the EQ-5D-5L. The project involves two main phases: (1) “light” cultural adaptation/cognitive debriefing interviews and content validity assessment of cognition bolt-ons in Argentina Spanish to a sample of 5-7 persons, (2) a cross-sectional psychometric validation study of the EQ-HWB-S, cognition bolt-ons, and EQ-5D-5L with a sample of 100-150 nursing home residents. If the project is successful, we plan to apply for a project extension to assess responsiveness on 80-120 subjects from this inception sample at 6-12 months follow-up. This study will contribute to further developing tools from the EuroQol set (cognition bolt-ons for EQ-5D, EQ-HWB-S) targeted to answer questions of the current RFP, for measuring quality of life in populations where the cognitive dimension is relevant.
