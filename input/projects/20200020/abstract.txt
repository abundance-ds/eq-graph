We held a one-day meeting in the UK for researchers with an interest in the development and application of the EQ-5D and other EuroQol measures. The meeting was designed to be researcher-led and aimed at UK and Ireland based researchers primarily. The meeting discussed recent developments in EuroQol instruments (EQ-HWB, the latest UK valuation study, use of large EQ-5D datasets in England, changes in health state preferences over time and recent work on personal utility functions). The meeting was mainly attended by non-EuroQol members, including early career researchers and students.
