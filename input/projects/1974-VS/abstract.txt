This study has two primary objectives: first, to develop a value set for EQ-5D-5L health states reflecting the preferences of the general population in Qatar; second, to compare value sets derived from TTO-based, hybrid, and DCE with duration (DCEd) methodologies, in order to assess the consistency and agreement among these valuation approaches. This will be a cross-sectional population-based study involving interviewer-administered online interviews following the EuroQol EQ-5D-5L valuation protocol and using modified version of the EuroQol Valuation Technology (EQ-VT). The latent scale DCE in the current EQ-VT will be replaced by the DCE with duration. A representative sample of 1000 adults will be recruited from the Qatari general population using a two-stage quota sampling, first by population distribution across the country, and then by age and sex distribution. Sampling will also take into account the population distribution of nationals and expatriates. Interviews will be conducted online by trained research assistants. EuroQol quality control processes will be followed to ensure high quality data. Data collection will be paused at 20%, 40%, and 60% to evaluate the DCE with duration data and update the experimental DCE design.  This study will provide a country-specific value set of the EQ-5D-5L for Qatar, and will add to the body of evidence on the comparative performance of the cTTO/EQ-VT approach vs. DCE with duration for generating a value set for the EQ-5D-5L.
