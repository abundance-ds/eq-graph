Background: The EQ-5D is widely used in clinical trials and economic evaluations, but its integration into routine clinical practice for supporting patient-centered care and clinical decision-making remains largely unexplored. In rheumatology, where patient-reported outcomes are part of care decisions, the EQ-5D's concise format could support patient assessment and clinical care. Aims: The aims of this proposal are to: 1) evaluate methods for summarizing and presenting EQ-5D data in rheumatology practice within U.S. healthcare workflows; 2) assess rheumatology stakeholder preferences for visual presentation formats of EQ-5D data; 3) investigate the potential role of EQ-5D in supporting clinical care in rheumatology settings. Methods: This study consist of three phases: 1) targeted literature review and environmental scan (including gray literature) of visualization approaches for PRO data in rheumatology, including evaluation of presentation formats, implementation experiences, and barriers; 2) development and internal evaluation of visualization specifications within EHR constraints by our data visualization team and EHR specialists, with feedback from the EuroQol PHS Working Group; 3) stakeholder engagement through focus groups with rheumatologists, nurses, patients with RA, and administrators to gather feedback on visualization approaches. Analysis will include thematic analysis of qualitative data following Braun and Clarke's approach. Significance: This research aligns with EuroQol PHSWG priorities. Findings will inform stakeholder preferences for different presentation formats and develop new approaches to data visualization, specifically focusing on how to effectively present EQ-5D data within electronic health record constraints.
