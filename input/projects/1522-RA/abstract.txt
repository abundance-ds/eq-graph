We developed an interactive survey tool similar to the design of the cTTO and tested its feasibility and acceptability. We tested the first version with 5 laypersons and used their feedback to improve the survey tool. We tested the improved tool with 5 more laypersons. Their feedback suggested the survey tool is ready for use in an online survey to estimate the value of child QALY relative to adult QALY using the person trade-off method.
