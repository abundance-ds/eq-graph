The EuroQol Research Foundation, established over 30 years ago, has two core activities: the global distribution of validated health measurement instruments and the funding of research that advances the measurement and valuation of health. These activities have contributed to the development of new measurement tools and a deeper understanding of measurement validity, cross-cultural applicability, and valuation methods for health-related quality of life. Despite EuroQol’s extensive reach—its instruments are now used in more than 180 countries—a formal assessment of its overall impact has never been undertaken. Without systematic evaluation, it remains unclear which aspects of our work generate the greatest value or how future investments can be optimised.This proposal seeks funding to conduct the first comprehensive impact assessment of EuroQol’s activities over the past decade (2015–2025). The study aims to understand how EuroQol’s contributions have shaped health measurement practices in clinical trials, health technology assessment, and policy decision-making, and to examine factors that drive or constrain impact.A mixed-methods approach will combine quantitative analyses of dissemination patterns and citation networks with qualitative insights from stakeholders, collaborators, and end users. The assessment will follow a four-phase approach: Phase 1 will map organizational development over the 12-year period. Phase 2 will collect data on outputs and outcomes across core activities, guided by an organizational timeline and impact pathway. Phase 3 will assess broader societal influence, examining whether expected connections to health measurement practice are evident. Phase 4 will synthesize findings into a comprehensive impact story covering 2012–2024.Findings will provide a systematic evidence base on EuroQol’s impact and identify mechanisms that may enhance influence. They will also inform the selection of indicators for consistent future monitoring, enabling ongoing evaluation of reach, uptake, and value. By learning systematically from this assessment, EuroQol can strengthen the reach, relevance, and sustainability of its initiatives—further advancing the measurement and valuation of health-related quality of life worldwide.
