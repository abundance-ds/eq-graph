In response to the limitations of the EQ-5D descriptive system, approaches such as addition of condition specific ‘bolt-on’ are being explored with the aim of improving the measurement sensitivity and relevance of EQ-5D in specific disease contexts. The Descriptive System Working Group (DSWG) of the EuroQol Group has proposed a different approach where the EQ-5D will be developed as a multi-level hierarchical instrument, where dimension specific items (or Dimension Specific Modules (DSM)) will be developed and nested below each of the existing five core dimensions. This project seeks to understand, from the perspective of key prospective users, the relevance, and expectations from such an instrument. This will be done via semi structured interviews with key international stakeholders from industry, government, academia and decision making contexts. The interviews will be guided by ‘mock DSMs’ for Mobility and Pain/Discomfort which will be developed using existing items from other instruments measuring the same, or overlapping constructs, identified by a targeted literature review. The ‘mock DSM’ will be used to facilitate the planned interviews, provide an example, and generate appropriate responses from study participants. We are proposing this approach on the basis that, provision of a ‘mock DSM’ during the interviews will put the subject matter in context and generate appropriate responses from study participants. Key prospective users of a DSM will be recruited internationally. Interviews will be conducted virtually, recorded, and transcribed for analysis. Thematic and content analysis of the transcribed data will be conducted, and the results presented in a de-identified manner.
