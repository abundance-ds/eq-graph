Background: The EQ-5D-5L has been used frequently in ophthalmology, but sometimes lacks specificity and responsiveness in demonstrating changes over time or showing differences between ophthalmological conditions. EuroQol is seeking to validate the Bolt-on Toolbox, including bolt-ons for vision. EuroQol listed validation of the vision bolt-on as a priority for this grant round, especially testing of the multiple versions supporting the finalization of item wording, and providing evidence for validity and reliability. Objectives: We aim to validate the vision bolt-on in 2 ophthalmological disease populations (glaucoma and vitreoretinal surgery) in the largest eye hospital of The Netherlands.Methods: Translation of the vision bolt-ons from the Toolbox into Dutch is currently in progress by our study team. We will validate these vision bolt-ons through a "piggyback study": we will add the vision bolt-ons to existing studies using the EQ-5D-5L and to new externally-funded studies planning to use the EQ-5D-5L. We will focus on two disease areas: glaucoma (n = 80) and vitreoretinal surgery (n > 500). Study 1 & 2: two existing RCTs in glaucomaStudy 3: cross-sectional study in vitreoretinal surgery (QOL assessment)Study 4: cross-sectional study in vitreoretinal surgery (PROM validation)Study 5: prospective cohort in vitreoretinal surgery (real-world data)In glaucoma, 2 clinical trials assess surgical outcomes. In vitreoretinal surgery, our team is validating a disease-specific PROM. We will gather evidence on construct validity (correlations with NEI VFQ, visual acuity, and other relevant clinical measures), responsiveness (before and after surgery), and test-retest reliability.
