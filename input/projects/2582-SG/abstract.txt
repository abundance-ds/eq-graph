EuroQol has funded over 900 research projects, producing a large body of publications on EQ-5D and related instruments. This literature contains detailed evidence on how these instruments perform across populations, countries, clinical areas, and methods. However, this information is distributed across individual papers and is not easily searchable or aggregable at the portfolio level.We propose to build a research knowledge graph of EuroQol's funded projects and their associated publications. Using large language models (LLMs), we will extract structured data from full-text papers: study designs, sample characteristics, instrument versions, methods, countries, and key findings. The extracted data will be stored in a graph database and made accessible through a web application that supports structured search, visual summaries, and natural-language queries.The project will proceed in three stages: (1) assembling the corpus from EuroQol's project database and identifying associated publications; (2) developing and iteratively refining the extraction pipeline; (3) building the web application. A preliminary demonstration, based on 944 project abstracts, produced a graph of over 2,000 nodes and 8,000 edges. The seed grant will go further by extracting from full-text publications and providing an interactive interface.If the approach proves useful, the knowledge graph can be expanded to cover the broader EQ-5D literature (over 17,000 publications in PubMed). The seed grant produces the working system and the evidence needed to assess whether such an expansion is warranted.
