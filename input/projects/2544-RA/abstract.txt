With the development of the EQ-5D bolt-on toolbox, decision for placement of the bolt-on items relative to the EQ VAS of the core EQ-5D measures will be an important suggestion for end users. This decision will be informed both by practicality and the impact of any ordering effects. We propose supporting decision making about EQ VAS and bolt-on order by summarizing available information on:1) ordering effects of general health indicator and health-related quality of life (HRQoL) items, and items switching response scales and 2) how other HRQoL/PROMs measures with short/long forms and/or add-on modules, or items with different response scales, have operationalized item ordering.For Aim 1, we propose a rapid review (RR) to synthesize available evidence on ordering effects of self-reported health measures. The RR will be conducted following guidance from the Cochrane Rapid Review Methods Group, and in collaboration with an experienced biomedical librarian. Aim 2 will be guided by the expertise of the EuroQol Descriptive Systems Working Group (DSWG) and broader EuroQol membership. Synthesized evidence from the RR and ordering of other measures will be summarized in a report to support decision on the ordering of EQ VAS and bolt-on.This project will be conducted in close and active collaboration with the DSWG as decisions on the search strategy and data extraction will necessarily need to be optimized to support decision making. The proposal has been extensively discussed with the DSWG, and aligns with the strategic priorities for this RFP focused on the order of bolt-ons in relation to the EQ-VAS.
