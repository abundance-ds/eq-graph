This is a formal request for support with the publication of my PhD dissertation, which describes innovative strategies for the care of children with juvenile idiopathic arthritis. Data from the EQ-5D-Y-5L questionnaire have been of tremendous value for the research presented in this thesis regarding remote monitoring of patients with juvenile idiopathic arthritis. By financially contributing to the publication costs of this dissertation you will directly support the dissemination of new insights into the most common rheumatic disease in children. Additionally, your support emphasizes the importance of these questionnaires as monitoring tools.
