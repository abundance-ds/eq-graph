This travel grant supported me to visit Rotterdam for one week, capitalising on the fact that I was already in Europe for ISPOR. Three days were spent as a visitor at ESHPM, Erasmus University. The time there was used to progress various projects I am working on in collaboration with Stefan Lipman, and to have a series of face to face meetings with Erasmus PhD students and various senior colleagues. (A detailed calendar of activities and meetings is provided as an attachment). Two days were spent as a visitor in the EuroQol offices in Rotterdam. The time was in the office divided between (a) working with Bram Roudijk to progress various projects, including progress toward the EQ-5D-Y-5L valuation protocol and (b) face to face meetings with Bernhard Slaap to discuss various Board matters.  Just as importantly, being in the office was an important opportunity to meet new members of the staff, take part in various informal team activities, and to get a good sense of how the office works as a team. I would strongly recommend such visits to future Board chairs.
