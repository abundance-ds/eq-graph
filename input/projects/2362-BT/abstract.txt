We propose a study to test the hearing bolt-on and other related bolt-ons in EuroQol's bolt-on toolbox. We will recruit individuals with clinically diagnosed hearing impairment who will receive hearing aids (n=200) or cochlear implantation (n=100) from a tertiary hospital in Finland. We will assess the patients before and after their hearing aid fitting or cochlear implantation using EQ-5D-5L, hearing bolt-on and other bolt-ons the bolt-on research programme recommends, hearing function questionnaires, and hearing tests. We will adopt the data analysis protocol developedby the bolt-on research programme to assess the acceptability, distributional characteristics, ceiling/floor effects, test-retest reliability, convergent validity, known-groups validity, and responsiveness of the bolt-ons. We will also assess the bolt-ons using exploratory factor analysis and item response theory (IRT) models. We have no plan to develop new bolt-ons, test bolt-ons outside the main and exploratory Toolboxes, or conduct qualitative assessment of any existing bolt-ons in this project, unless the bolt-on research programme wishes us to do so. We will follow the data analysis protocol developed by the bolt-on toolbox validation programme, if available and recommended, to assess the included bolt-ons.
