While health technology assessment (HTA) infrastructure is steadily advancing across the MENA region, the routine use of standardized outcome measures—particularly those enabling quality-adjusted life year (QALY) estimation—remains limited and uneven. Several countries, including Egypt, Saudi Arabia, Tunisia, Jordan, the UAE, Morocco, and Qatar, have developed EQ-5D value sets, providing a strong methodological foundation for economic evaluation. However, challenges persist in translating these advances into consistent application in practice and policy. This 3-hour workshop will introduce the EuroQol instruments and discuss how they can be more effectively integrated into healthcare decision-making across MENA. Beyond their established role in cost-utility analysis, presentations will highlight the broader use of EQ measures in value-based healthcare, real-world evidence generation, and population health monitoring within the region’s evolving health systems. The workshop will bring together regional HEOR leaders and experts to identify gaps, research priorities, and capacity-building needs to support greater adoption of EQ-5D in economic and policy contexts. Importantly, it will also provide a platform for the EuroQol Research Foundation to expand its engagement in MENA, strengthen relationships with emerging HTA authorities, and foster new collaborations that promote EQ-5D research, education, and implementation.
