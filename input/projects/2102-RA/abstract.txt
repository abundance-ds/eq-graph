Despite the increasing collection of EQ-5D data for measuring and monitoring patients' health in routine care, its implementation during clinical encounters remains limited. This project will employ a mixed-methods approach to address this issue. Work package 1 will involve creating a suite of diverse visualisation formats using real-world EQ-5D-5L data from the British Spine Registry for patients with sciatica. These visualisations will be informed by existing literature and data visualisation guidelines. Work package 2 will consist of qualitative focus groups with stakeholders (patients with sciatica, physiotherapists, and surgeons) to explore their preferences for the developed visualisation formats and to identify potential barriers and facilitators to their implementation in routine clinical use. Work package 3 will focus on integrating the preferred visualisation format into the British Spine Registry and developing best practice guidelines for EQ-5D-5L data visualisation based on the project's findings. This research seeks to create user-friendly visualisations that improve understanding and facilitate the use of EQ-5D-5L in decisions regarding patient care. The project directly aligns with EuroQol’s strategic research priorities concerning the 'reporting and implementation of EQ instruments in routine outcome assessment'.
