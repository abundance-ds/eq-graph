This grant contributed toward my meeting registration, train/bus fare, and hotel accommodations for the 16th IAHPR Meeting.  At this Meeting, I gave a 25-minute podium presentation on "Preference Reliability" during a session sponsored by the EuroQol Research Foundation. This secondary analysis utilized existing EQ-5D-5L data but was not part of any funded project. Multiple EuroQol members were in attendance, including Rosalie Viney, Exec Chair.
