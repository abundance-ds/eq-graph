Background: The Valuation Working Group (VWG) is prepairing for a preliminary discrete choice with duration valuation protocol (EQ-DCE-VT). Recent critiques of the proposed modelling approach can, at least partially, be addressed through an improved model specification combined with more efficient DCE anchoring tasks. Methods Previous analyses of DCE with duration datasets have used statistical models that either ignore heterogeneity entirely, or accommodate heterogeneity in only health preferences or time preferences—but not both. This project aims to accommodate both health and time preference heterogeneity within a single model. The proposed modeling approach implements a flexible covariance specification that allows for both random health and random discount rate parameters while enabling correlation between health and time preferences. Such a complex model requires choice data with adequate identification and statistical power beyond what has traditionally been available. However, the choice data from the recently EQ-funded RAMPACT study—which comprised efficiently optimized matched pairwise choices and improved anchoring tasks—provides sufficient separation of health and time preferences to overcome this limitation. Results: The project will develop and implement the improved model, fit it to the RAMPACT study data, both with and without the improved anchoring tasks, and investigate the impact of the improved model and anchoring tasks on DCE value sets.
