Background: Fatigue is one of the most common and burdensome symptoms experienced by persons living with multiple sclerosis (MS), yet it is not currently captured in the EQ-5D-5L. The project 2035-BT: Qualitative Study for Fatigue Bolt-on (QFSB) aims to inform the development of the fatigue bolt-on through semi-structured interviews with persons living with MS. As one of the interviewers of the project, Minh Pham (MP) conducted a research visit to the National University of Singapore to receive qualitative training under the guidance Professor Nan Luo and his PhD student, Jia Jia Lee.Aim: The visit aimed to develop and pilot qualitative research materials including the interview guide, survey instrument, and coding framework, and to receive training in interviewing, transcription, and thematic coding.Methods: During the two-week visit, the research team reviewed the study proposal and addressed feedback from the EuroQol Executive Committee. The interview guide and survey instrument were developed and refined. Mock interviews and alpha testing were conducted to assess these materials.Results: Overall, the visit provided practical experience with interview procedures and data analysis. In addition to drafting the materials, MP was able to shadow a PhD student conducting in-person qualitative interview and assisted a nursing student in coordinating two group interviews. MP also received training in transcription using otter.ai and thematic coding using atlas.ti. During the visit, MP began drafting the protocol paper for the study, which is planned for abstract submission to the 2026 EuroQol Plenary Meeting.
