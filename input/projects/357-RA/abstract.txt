Our 2020 study demonstrated some inconsistencies among 11 well known measures of inequality when applied to EQ-5D-3L data from a large study of 22 countries. The 2020 study raised several concerns and questions which led to the development of the current (2024) project in which a closer look is taken at these 11 measures and their performance with EQ-5D data . We compared the 11 measures in terms of outcomes, theoretical bases, underlying assumptions and performance using a large data set of 37,000 individuals in 22 countries. This study also considered some other related issues, such as applying a new approach to inequality using ordinal data and opportunities for investigating and presenting inequality findings using newly developed data visualization tools.
