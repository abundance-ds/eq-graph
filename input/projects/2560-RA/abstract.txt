The EuroQol Research Foundation has recently developed the EQ-5D-5L Bolt-On Toolbox to enhance the instrument’s ability to capture health dimensions not fully represented in the core system, such as sleep, cognition, and fatigue. However, the global adoption of these bolt-ons depends on the availability of high-quality translations and culturally appropriate adaptations. Arabic is a strategic priority given its widespread use across the Middle East and North Africa, with Modern Standard Arabic enabling cross-country comparability. Saudi Arabia represents a particularly relevant context due to ongoing health system transformation under Vision 2030, emphasizing value-based care and patient-reported outcomes.This study aims to translate and culturally adapt 18 EQ-5D-5L bolt-on items into Modern Standard Arabic for use in Saudi Arabia, following EuroQol’s standardized translation and linguistic validation protocols under the supervision of the Version Management Committee. A subset of eight core and ten exploratory items will be translated and adapted to ensure conceptual equivalence and cultural relevance. The resulting Arabic toolbox will support future empirical validation through planned national studies and facilitate broader use in clinical research, health technology assessment, and real-world evidence generation across the region.
