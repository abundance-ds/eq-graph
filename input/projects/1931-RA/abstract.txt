Population norms derived from EQ-5D data play a pivotal role in health-related quality-of-life assessment, serving as standardized benchmarks for comparing individuals' health status across diverse populations. However, methodological inconsistencies and a lack of established guidelines pose challenges to their reliability and usefulness. Building upon prior research efforts, this multidimensional project aims to address these challenges and enhance the accessibility and usability of EQ-5D population norms. The specific aims of the project are to systematically review existing literature on EQ-5D population norms (Aim 1), develop comprehensive guidelines for their development and reporting (Aim 2), and establish an interactive website on the EuroQol platform for easy access to norm data (Aim 3). Part 1 involves a systematic review adhering to PRISMA guidelines to identify relevant literature. Part 2 aims to formulate recommendations through a modified Delphi process, informed by the systematic review findings. Part 3 entails the creation of a beta website, guided by principles of user-centered design and iterative development. This project represents a concerted effort to refine and expand the scope of EQ-5D population norms. It aligns with the EuroQol Group's mission of advancing high-quality research and evidence-based decision-making in healthcare.
