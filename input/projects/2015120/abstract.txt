The EQ-5D research community continues its endeavor in search for innovative methods to elicit preferences for health status measures such as the EQ-5D-5L, given the limitations and concerns among existing methods. Best worst scaling (BWS) is another choice-based technique which presents one health state at a time. This could potentially make the valuation task less cognitively demanding. Similar to other choice-based techniques, BWS also derives weights on a latent utility scale and thus cannot be directly used in quality-adjusted life year (QALY) calculation. In this proposal, we explore a new approach called BWS-VAS to directly deriving the impact weights on the full health-dead scale.
