The EQ-DAPHNIE Project is well known to the EQ Executive. It was originally funded as a major infrastructure investment for the EuroQol Group. There are two major aspects to this infrastructure: 1) the data arising from the measurement of population health with EQ instrument-based surveys in countries around the world; and 2) the survey and data storage infrastructure that can be used by EQ members for future population-level studies. This later aspect is analogous to the investment made in valuation infrastructure to support the creation of preference-based value sets for EQ instruments.
