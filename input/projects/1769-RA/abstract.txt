**BACKGROUND** Each bolt-on item (and its response options) may be transformed into an attribute and appended to the EQ-5D-5L descriptive system for a health valuation study. Although many have shown that it is technically feasible to produce de novo value sets for bolt-on attributes, this approach seems impractical given the possible number of bolt-ons. At present, the EuroQol Group lacks a valuation method for bolt-ons that does not rely on potentially invalid assumptions (e.g., scale-factor model). **AIMS:** The primary aim of this study is to assess the feasibility of DCE with kaizen tasks for bolt-on valuation. Furthermore, this feasibility study addresses two questions in bolt-on valuation: (1) Whether a bolt-on attribute is more or less important than the core dimensions (i.e., importance)? (2) Does a bolt-on attribute overlap with the other core dimensions or attenuate their importances (i.e., shrinkage)? More specifically, the proposed study examined two descriptive systems with cognition bolt-ons: (5D+1) an EQ-5D-5L system with a single composite bolt-on attribute: Memory/Concentration: (no, slight, moderate, severe, extreme) problems remembering things or concentrating. (5D+2) an EQ-5D-5L system with two component bolt-on attributes: Memory: (no, slight, moderate, severe, extreme) problems remembering things. Concentration: (no, slight, moderate, severe, extreme) problems concentrating. These bolt-ons were selected largely for pragmatic reasons. The levels are on a severity scale as recommended by Mulhern et al. 2022 and their descriptors were adapted from Sampson et al. 2020. METHODS: Following a published protocol, we conduct six surveys (N=1268), each with 2 discrete choice experiments (DCE) back-to-back. The DCEs differed by their attributes: EQ-5D-5L only, EQ-5D-5L with 1 bolt-on (Memory/Concentration), EQ-5D-5L with 2 bolt-ons (Memory, Concentration). For each DCE, we estimated US values on a quality-adjusted life year (QALY) scale for 10-year episodes by maximum likelihood using conditional logit models and compared them with prior US value sets. In response to the US Inflation Reduction Act of 2022, we also produced EQ-5D-5L values on an experience scale.
