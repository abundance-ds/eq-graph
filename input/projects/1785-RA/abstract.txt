The EQ-5D-5L has shown potential limitations in capturing key aspects of health-related quality of life (HRQoL) in dermatological diseases. The project investigated the psychometric properties of the EQ-5D-5L and five bolt-ons – skin irritation (itching, IT), self-confidence (SE), social relationships (SR), social participation (SP) and feeling connected (FC) – in two prevalent chronic inflammatory dermatological conditions, atopic dermatitis and psoriasis. A cross-sectional online survey was conducted with n=180 German atopic dermatitis (mean age: 37 years) and n=285 psoriasis patients (46 years) including the EQ-5D-5L, five bolt-ons, and various (skin-specific) patient-reported outcome measures (e. g., Dermatology Life Quality Index (DLQI), Skin Shame Scale (SSS-24), Patient-Oriented Eczema Measure (POEM), Psoriasis Signs and Symptoms Diary (PSSD), Patient Health Questionnaire (PHQ-4)). Analyses comprised distribution patterns (including number and proportion of "no problems" (ceiling) and missing values (feasibility)), convergent, divergent and known-groups validity, as well as explanatory power.
