Numerous studies have evaluated the impact of long COVID on health-related quality-of-life (HRQoL) using the EQ-5D, and while there is an evidenced decline in HRQoL, the key symptoms of long COVID – brain fog, fatigue and breathlessness – may not be effectively captured by the EQ-5D. This chronic condition thereby offers an opportunity to evaluate items in the EQ-5D Bolt-On Toolbox. This project uses mixed methods to test bolt-on items for the EQ-5D-5L in a cohort of individuals with long COVID. The project will utilise Mātauranga Raranga | Long COVID Registry Aotearoa New Zealand, a registry which longitudinally tracks HRQoL using the EQ-5D-5L. Mixed-methods research will be employed, where the nature of the pschyometric testing will vary according to the bolt-on and in consultation with the DSWG Bolt-on sub-team using standardised protocols and analysis templates. This project will test the validity of cognition, fatigue and breathing bolt-ons in a population with a new and significant chronic condition, thereby contributing to the EuroQol Group’s scientific understanding and strategic positioning regarding bolt-ons.
