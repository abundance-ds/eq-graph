Between Dec 9, 2021, and Apr 30, 2022, we collected survey responses from 3,269 pregnant and postpartum women on EQ-5D-5L measures, prenatal care experiences, birth experiences and mental and physical health of mother and baby. Results from analysis of this cohort of pregnant and postpartum individuals indicates that at an individual-level, concerns about SARS-CoV-2 variants are associated with lower HRQoL (predominantly increased problems with anxiety/depression). However, at an aggregate-level, HRQoL was lowest for individuals birthing at the beginning of the pandemic, prior to the dominance of SARS-CoV-2 variants.
