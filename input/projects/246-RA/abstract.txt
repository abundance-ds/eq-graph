Background: The outbreak of COVID-19 global pandemic hit hard on global economy. Different countries implemented various policies in response, ranging from maximum, partial, to no lockdowns. Study objectives: To assess the impact of COVID-19 on population health using the repeated surveys in Sweden and Norway, and then perform a cross-country comparison with the data from US. Proposed methods: The same survey instrument was used in all 3 countries. The US study was funded by the EuroQol Group (84-2020RA) for 3 waves of data collection. To align with US, the 1st wave data in Sweden and Norway was collected also in April, 2020, and was paid out-of-pocket by the researchers. Findings from the 1st wave had been analysed, a working paper has been developed and available at: https://ihe.se/en/publicering/covid-19-quality-of-life/. Additionally, a comparison study between the 3 countries using the 1st wave data are currently being drafted, along with two manuscripts that are under peer review. To understand the change of COVID-19 impact over time, we aim to collect another wave of data in Sweden and Norway and then compare with US. The next wave of data collection will be aligned with the 3rd wave in US post-election. Main instruments used included: the EQ-5D-5L, the Veterans RAND 12 item health Survey (VR-12), the WPAI, and the PHQ-4. Others questions included respondents’ background, social-economic status, current known COVID-19 status, behaviour changes, and employment status changes due to COVID-19 were also included in the survey.
