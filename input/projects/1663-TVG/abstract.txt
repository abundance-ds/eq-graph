This final report documented my month-long residency with the Alberta PROMs and EQ-5D Research and Support Unit (APERSU) from September to October 2023, I engaged in a productive research exchange and internship at the School of Public Health, University of Alberta. This period was marked by significant growth in my skills, knowledge, and research capabilities related to the applications of EQ-5D and other Patient-Reported Outcome Measures (PROMs) in clinical practice and health systems. The objectives were successfully met through a series of comprehensive activities. Firstly, I enhanced my research expertise by participating in an internship program, where I had the opportunity to learn from leading experts in the field. I attended and contributed to research seminars, presenting my PhD research proposal and the preliminary findings to a scholarly audience. Secondly, I visited and interacted with experts from APERSU and the Institute of Health Economics in Edmonton. These visits facilitated the exploration of PROMs data integration into health systems, aimed at promoting sustainability and efficiency. Advocacy for routine collection of PROMs data within healthcare system in Singapore was a significant aspect of these discussions, paving the way for future collaborative efforts. Thirdly, my attendance at the APERSU Annual End-user Meeting and the ISOQOL Annual Conference was instrumental. Not only did I stay updated with the latest research and practices in the field, but I also contributed significantly by presenting my PhD work alongside two oral and one poster presentation. Additionally, it provided an excellent platform for networking and establishing collaborations with renowned professionals and researchers, thereby contributing to my professional growth and the expansion of my research network. The completion of this funded travel has been a cornerstone in advancing my career as a researcher. It has equipped me with invaluable insights and expertise on PROMs in clinical practice and health systems. The visit has not only enhanced my capacity to contribute to improving patient outcomes and satisfaction through evidence-based decision-making but has also laid a robust foundation for my forthcoming post-doctoral pursuits and the achievement of my long-term career objectives. The benefits reaped from this grant-funded travel will resonate throughout my future endeavors in healthcare research.
