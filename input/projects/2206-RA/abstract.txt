Comparison of EuroQol instruments of health outcomes across populations defined by clinical, demographic or socioeconomic characteristics is potentially confounded by reporting heterogeneity (RH), stemming from variations in respondents' reference points, perceptions, and biases. The anchoring vignette method can identify and adjust for RH, but its application to EuroQol instruments remains limited. Moreover, the validity of the method depends on two assumptions—Vignette Equivalence (VE) and Response Consistency (RC)—for which empirical support is mixed. This project will both extend application of the method to EuroQol instruments and investigate whether its validity is improved by presenting vignettes in visually enhanced formats, rather than text, with the aim of reducing variability in interpretation and aligning reporting styles. An experiment, employing within- and between-subject designs, will evaluate validity of the VE and RC assumptions with visual and text-only vignette presentations. Visual vignettes will be created using innovative AI tools, facilitating flexibility and broad applicability. Both qualitative and quantitative analyses will assess assumption validity. The experiment data will be used to expand knowledge on the nature and drivers of RH. This project aims to improve comparability of measures derived from EuroQol instruments – such as EQ-5D and EQ-VAS -and develop better-performing vignette designs that can be used, with EuroQol instruments, in health surveys.
