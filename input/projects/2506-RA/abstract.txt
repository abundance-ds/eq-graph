The project that we are proposing is a psychometric analysis of the EQ-5D-5L and the CarerQoL instruments, for measuring the quality of life of informal caregivers. We are aware that relevant data has already been collected from the BIG CACTUS study (Palmer et al 2019). The BIG CACTUS study was a UK-based trial investigating a computer-delivered speech and language therapy intervention for patients with aphasia following a stroke.Participants: 278 patients were included in the study.Caregiver Involvement: Where available, a maximum of one informal caregiver per patient was invited to participate in self-reported data collection regarding their own quality of life.Measurement Tools: EQ-5D-5L: self-reported by informal caregiversCarerQol-7D: A specific instrument designed to measure the care-related quality of life of informal caregivers.Additional Data: The study also collected data on CaCoast, patient self-reported EQ-5D-5L, and patient primary endpoints.Following a 12-month follow-up period, complete EQ-5D-5L data was collected from 226 patients, and complete EQ-5D-5L data was collected from 125 informal caregivers. This dataset would make it possible to evaluate the construct validity and responsiveness of both the EQ-5D-5L and the CarerQol-7D for informal caregivers of post-stroke aphasia patients.The Lead Investigator of the BIG CACTUS study has already granted permission to obtain and use the dataset for this proposed analysis.
