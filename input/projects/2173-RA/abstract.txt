Bolt-ons have attracted growing interest for their potential to enhance the EQ-5D’s sensitivity in capturing health outcomes in specific populations and contexts. This study aims to evaluate the psychometric performance of eleven bolt-ons—tiredness, energy, fatigue, exhaustion, sleep, vision, hearing, cognition, memory, concentration, and social relationships—in a sample of older adults. The project will build on an existing study comparing six preference-weighted measures in older adults in Australia. An online survey is currently being prepared, which will include the EQ-5D-5L, EQ-HWB-S, ASCOT, ICECAP-O, WOOP, and QOL-ACC, alongside the eleven bolt-ons, the OPQOL-Brief, the Personal Wellbeing Index (PWI), and the 3-item Loneliness Scale. A total of 800 adults aged 65 years and older will be recruited through the survey company Dynata. The psychometric evaluation of the bolt-ons will include analysis of missing data, distribution characteristics and informativity, known-group validity, divergent and convergent validity, and structural validity.
