The EQ-HWB-9 descriptive system was finalized in September 2025. However, supporting evidence for certain methodological aspects requires further testing in the coming year to facilitate the planned launch in 2027. In web-based questionnaire design, the grid/matrix and item-by-item formats are two common approaches for presenting items with shared response scales. Although each offers distinct advantages, they also have limitations. It is essential to understand the differences between these formats in terms of response distributions, completion time, data quality, respondent burden, and device compatibility, particularly for online surveys using the EQ-HWB-9. Additionally, all EuroQol instruments—including the adult and youth versions of the EQ-5D, as well as the EQ-TIPS—incorporate a visual analogue scale (VAS). However, no VAS has yet been proposed for the EQ-HWB(-9). This study aims to explore the impact of layout changes (item-by-item vs. grid) on the measurement properties of the EQ-HWB-9; and the measurement performance and potential differences between two versions of an EQ-HWB VAS in a representative sample of the Hong Kong general population. This proposed analysis will provide empirical evidence on how the EQ-HWB-9 item format and modifications to the cognition response options affect its psychometric properties in the general population. Moreover, this study is among the first to compare two versions of the HWB VAS within the same population. It will also investigate the relationship between the HWB VAS and the EQ VAS of the EQ-5D-5L, along with their broader associations with the EQ-HWB-9 and EQ-5D-5L items.
