Aim: To enhance the sensitivity of the EQ-5D-5L in assessing skin conditions, the EuroQol Group developed two skin-specific "bolt-on" dimensions -- skin irritation and self-confidence. In response to the EuroQol's 18th Call for Proposals by the Descriptive System Working Group, this study aims to evaluate the psychometric properties of the skin bolt-ons added to the EQ-5D-5L in patients with mild-to-moderate psoriasis. Specifically, we will assess construct validity, responsiveness to clinical changes over time, and measurement invariance across demographic subgroups. Methods: We will perform a secondary analysis of pooled data from three phase III international clinical trials (PSO-FAST, PSO-ABLE, PSO-LONG) involving 1,331 patients with mild-to-moderate psoriasis collected over 56 weeks. Measures include the EQ-5D-5L plus two bolt-ons, Dermatology Life Quality Index (DLQI), Psoriasis Symptom Inventory (PSI), Psoriasis Quality of Life (PQOL-12) and clinical assessments such as the Physician Global Assessment (PGA) and modified Psoriasis Area and Severity Index (mPASI). Statistical analyses will follow COSMIN guidelines, assessing construct validity through convergent and known-groups validity, responsiveness using anchor-based methods, and measurement invariance via differential item functioning analysis. Significance: We will compare the EQ-5D-5L with the EQ-5D-5L plus two skin bolt-ons to assess whether the bolt-ons improve overall psychometric performance across three countries (UK, US and France). Findings will inform whether the skin bolt-ons have the ability to detect meaningful changes in dermatological conditions within a previously under-researched group of mild-to-moderate psoriasis patients. The results will support the integration of these bolt-ons into the EQ-5D framework, facilitate their advancement from Beta to Approved status, and encourage their adoption by the pharmaceutical industry in future clinical trials.
