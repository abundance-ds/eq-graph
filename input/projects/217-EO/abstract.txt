To facilitate the adoption and application of the EQ-5D instruments among diverse stakeholders in China, two Europol group members (Feng Xie and Xuejing Jin) successfully organized and hosted two specialized workshops. The first workshop, a half-day online session conducted in Mandarin, took place on April 17th, 2022. It encompassed four key thematic areas: 1) an updated overview of Patient-Reported Outcome Measures (PROMs) and EQ-5D instruments; 2) procedures for accessing EQ-5D instruments for both non-commercial and commercial purposes; 3) the collection, analysis, reporting, and interpretation of EQ-5D data; 4) leveraging EQ-5D data in economic evaluations to support drug reimbursement decisions. Prior registration for this online workshop exceeded 300 participants. Notably, the session was fully open to the public online, allowing individuals to tune in directly without pre-registration. The second workshop, an in-person half-day event also delivered in Mandarin, was held on December 28th, 2025. Its focus on three advanced topics: 1) preference-based measures and their application in cost-utility analysis; 2) mapping methodologies to EQ-5D for supporting cost-utility analysis; 3) utilizing EQ-5D data to inform medical decision-making across different levels of the healthcare system. Approximately 50 people (including researchers from research institutes, clinicians, pharmacists, postgraduate students in related majors, as well as representatives from industry) participated in this in-person workshop. The two workshops mentioned above have further enhanced the understanding of various stakeholders in China regarding the EQ-5D instruments and their application scenarios.
