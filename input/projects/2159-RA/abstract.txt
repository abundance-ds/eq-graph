Background: The EuroQol Group is exploring extending the EQ-5D with bolt-ons and embarked upon developing entirely new instruments, such as the EQ-HWB. Evidence on psychometric performance of bolt-ons alongside EQ-5D-5L and the EQ-HWB(-S) in general and patients populations is needed to ensure their reliability and validity before they can be adopted in clinical practice or research. Aims: The aims of this proposal are to evaluate and compare the psychometric properties of the EQ-5D-5L, five bolt-ons and the EQ-HWB-S in injury patients and determine factors associated with worse scores in these instruments and bolt-ons. Methods: We will use data from a stratified sample of patients who presented to the emergency department because of an injury. The 12 month follow up survey will include the EQ-5D-5L, five bolt-ons and the EQ-HWB-S. We will evaluate and compare distributional characteristics, structural validity, differential item functioning, known group validity, exploratory power analysis and analysis of interchangeability. Where possible, psychometric properties will be evaluated for the total sample and for specific sub-groups (e.g. those with low health literacy level). We will use linear regression models to determine factors that are associated with worse EQ-5D-5L, EQ-5D-5L + bolt-ons and EQ-HWB-S scores. Deliverables: The project will produce at least three scientific manuscripts. In addition, the project will produce evidence on the psychometric performance of EQ-5D-5L, bolt-ons and the EQ-HWB-S in a diverse patient population.
