The pharmaceutical industry and patient advocates argue that QALY-based HTA and the EQ-5D in particular serve to limit patients' access to care. Debates around QALY-based HTA, cost-effectiveness thresholds, and recommendations for the use of EQ-5D are ongoing in several countries.The Annual Meeting of the Society for Medical Decision Making is in Oslo, Norway, and I have been provided an opportunity to arrange a 75 minute panel debate around these issues, with representatives from the industry, HTA, academia, and EuroQol. The aim is to have an informed and exciting discussion, highlighting areas of controversy and how these should be tackled.SMDM is broadly attended by people involved in health-care decision making, including health economists, clinicians, and researchers.
