This visit to Singapore included meetings with researchers and policymakers at NUS, Duke NUS, NUHS and Singhealth. I gave a presentation at the School of Public Health, NUS, and started the development of a new research grant on population norms for older adults. I was able to move forward some ongoing research grants using the EQ-5D and develop a number of opportunities with members of the HTA community for using the EQ-5D.
