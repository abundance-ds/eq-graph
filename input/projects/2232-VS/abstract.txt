This project aims to develop the EQ-5D value set for Kazakhstan general population. In Kazakhstan, the implementation of the health technology assessment (HTA) process began in 2010, with reports providing decision-makers information on the clinical and economic effectiveness of technologies, as well as organizational, legal, and ethical aspects. However, to support economic evaluations such as cost-utility and cost-effectiveness analyses, a national EQ-5D-5L value set is needed, which has not yet been developed for Kazakhstan. The study will follow the EuroQol Group’s Valuation Technology (EQ-VT) protocol and apply data quality monitoring methods. A cross-sectional survey using the EQ-VT software will target a representative sample of 1,000 respondents from five regions of Kazakhstan, both Kazakh and Russian language versions of the instrument. Value sets will be modelled using time trade-off (TTO) data, discrete choice experiment (DCE) data, and a combination of both. By integrating this methodology into national research and policy frameworks, the project will contribute to health decision-making and enable assessments of healthcare interventions. Furthermore, the findings will support international comparisons of health state preferences, facilitating a better understanding of similarities and differences across countries. As part of the project, training will be provided to national stakeholders in valuation methodology, ensuring sustainability and the continued integration of preference-based measurement in future health research and policy planning in Kazakhstan.
