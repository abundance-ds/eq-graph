A number of possible bolt-ons to the EQ-5D-5L/3L have recently been propsed for the bolt-on toolbox. Testing the content validity of  proposed bolt-on items is important. This includes qualitative content validity testing. This project will produce a package of standardised guidance to support EuroQol funded research projects aiming to test the content validity of bolt-ons. This will be beneficial a) to ensure high quality data and analysis b) to share resources where it is cost-effective to do so c) to make it easier for findings across qualtiative studies to be synthesied.
