All City of York schools were invited to participate in a survey of children aged 11-16. A total of 5,750 children completed a specially designed questionnaire incorporating EQ-5D-Y and a graphic 0-100 rating scale. Results indicated higher rates of problems for the last 3 dimensions in girls compared with boys. This difference was most dramatically apparent in older girls on the worried/sad/unhappy dimension, where rates were double those of boys of the same age.Comparative data from surveys conducted some 10 years earlier in many of the same schools, deterioration in self-reported health status despite targeted programmes designed to mitigate mental health problems in the school population.
