The project titled “Building the health-related quality of life evidence for Bangladesh’s children: piloting the EQ-5D-Y Bangla version instrument for Bangladesh” was approved by the EuroQol Group and received a grant of €12,000 to support the translation of the Bangla version of the EQ-5D-Y-3L, both self-complete and proxy versions. The project aimed to translate and pilot the Bangla version of the EQ-5D-Y in Bangladesh to inform a large household survey across multiple administrative divisions, enabling the collection and analysis of child health data using the tested instrument.In addition to this support, and with funding from a Deakin University post-doctoral fellowship, the project was implemented in collaboration with the ARK Foundation, Bangladesh. For the first time, the study piloted both the self-complete and parent-proxy Bangla versions of the EQ-5D-Y-3L with 240 parent–child dyads to evaluate its feasibility, reliability, and validity. Following the successful completion of this pilot (100% response rate with strong reliability), the validated EQ-5D-Y-3L Bangla version was incorporated into a larger household survey, which covered more than 500 households. In this survey, 712 respondents-either children or their parents, completed the EQ-5D-Y-3L tool, enabling the measurement of health-related quality of life (HRQoL) among Bangladeshi children.
