BackgroundThe EuroQol Health and Wellbeing 9 (EQ-HWB-9) and EQ-HWB long form measures cover dimensions relevant for healthcare and social care users, and their caregivers. After initial testing of the experimental version (v1.1, 2022), areas for possible refinement were identified in item wording, response option labels, and item ordering. A modified experimental version (v1.2) was introduced in 2024. This study examines the psychometric performance of the EQ-HWB-9 v1.2 and v1.1 in the Netherlands (NL) and the United States (USA).MethodsData from 3,783 adult participants (NL n=1,571; USA n=2,212) were collected via an online panel using quota sampling. All participants completed EQ-HWB-9 v1.2 as part of a longer survey and a subset was randomized to one of four arms, including EQ-HWB long form, EQ-HWB-9 v1.1 and its variants. Response distribution was explored, with instrument-level ceiling and floor effects defined as >15%. Known group validity was assessed using Mann-Whitney U test across five pre-defined subgroups, and convergent validity was assessed using Spearman’s correlations between EQ-HWB-9 versions, EQ-HWB long form, and EQ-5D-5L level sum scores.ResultsInstrument level floor/ceiling effects were not observed across versions, except for a marginal ceiling effect above threshold in the NL v1.1 (15.3%). v1.1 presented a higher proportion of Level 1 responses, particularly for day-to-day activities (USA - v1.2: 51.9%; v1.1: 60.5%; NL - v1.2: 51.8%; v1.1: 60.1%), and control (USA - v1.2: 53.4%; v1.1: 63.0%, v1.2: 56.0%; v1.1: 59.5%). All versions significantly discriminated across all known groups in both countries with large effect sizes in four out of five groups. v1.2 showed very strong cross-version correlations with v1.1 (rho=0.88 USA; rho=0.85 NL). Convergent validity was also demonstrated with external measures (EQ-5D-5L, EQ VAS, global health satisfaction, SWLS), with slightly higher correlations in v1.2 in both countries. Item-level convergent validity between matched v1.2 and v1.1 items was very strong to strong in both countries (rho=0.57 to 0.82) as well as with conceptually matched EQ-5D-5L items.ConclusionEQ-HWB-9 v1.2 demonstrated at least equivalent or slightly superior psychometric performance to v1.1 across all properties and consistently in both countries, supporting the modifications adopted in v1.2.
