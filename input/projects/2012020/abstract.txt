The initial preparation of the study was undertaken between November 2012 and September 2013. Due to the results of the first wave of EQ-5D-5L valuation studies in 2012/2013and the following moratorium, it was decided to postpone the data collection of the main study in Germany and to conduct an initial experimental pre-studyto test the protocol updates in Germany.The final report was already signed-off and the results were published (Ludwig et al. Valuation of the EQ-5D-5L with composite time trade-off for the German population -an exploratory study. Health Qual Life Outcomes 2017;15(1):39).The results of the pre-study contributed to the development of EQ-VT version 2.0 (inclusion of feedback module). After consulting the Valuation Methodology Working Group in September 2014, it was decided to apply the improved valuation protocol2.0 in the German EQ-5D-5L valuation study for the first time worldwide. Interviewer training and pilot interviews were carried out in November2014. Data were collected from December 2014 to March 2015by 19 professionalinterviewers in six cities and surroundslocated in different parts of Germany. A detailed analysison the complete data set was carried out from May2015to March 2017 and (preliminary)results were stepwise presented on several conferences. A manuscript for journal submission was prepared and submitted in August 2017.
