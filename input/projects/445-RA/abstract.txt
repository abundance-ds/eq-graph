We completed the elements of the project that were feasible within the funding period. As noted in our interim report last year, the full implementation of the patient decision aid by the Alberta Bone & Joint Health Institute (ABJHI) was delayed, thus making it impossible to complete our originally planned evaluation of its implementation. Instead, we focused on the revision and enhancements of the decision aid itself, with an extension of the usability testing we completed with patients. For this work, the decision aid had additional elements of revised visualization of the EQ-5D data for patients, and inclusion of additional items about patients' preferences over wait time and levels of symptoms/limitations. We prepared two manuscripts to date, one on patients' perceptions of the EQ-5D data presentation, on on the overall decision aid usability (currently under review). The research team agreed on two additional manuscripts regarding the treatment preference data and the inclusion of non-surgical PROMs data collection.
