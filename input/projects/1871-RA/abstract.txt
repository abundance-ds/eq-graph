The EQ-DAPHNIE Project aims to establish infrastructure for the EQ Group to assess population health using EQ instruments. It was funded for 3 years (2021-2024), with an intention to consider a second 3-year phase depending on the success of Phase 1. We have completed Round 1 survey in 5 countries, with predominantly English-speaking populations. This request is a 1-year bridge grant to maintain momemtum on the EQ-DAPHNIE project. Requested funds would support the EQ-DAPHNIE Team in 3 areas: 1) survey development and depolyment for Round 2 countries; 2) EQ member access to EQ-DAPHNIE data; 3) data analysis & knowledge translation. Remaining funds for the original EQ-DAPHNIE 3-year Phase 1 grant will be used to support the ongoing data collection for the planned 10 countries in Round 2.
