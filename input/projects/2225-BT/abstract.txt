Background:EuroQol has developed a global cognition bolt‑on for the EQ‑5D‑5L, alongside multiple exploratory cognition subdimension items (e.g., memory, concentration, decision making), which are now part of the experimental EQ-5D Bolt-on Toolbox. Parkinson’s disease (PD)—a condition with heterogeneous cognitive profiles and frequent proxy reporting—is a well-suited population to psychometrically test these items including alternative item wordings, global, subdimension and composite cognition bolt-on items versions and potential overlaps with existing EQ‑5D dimensions (e.g., usual activities, anxiety/depression).Objectives:(1) Evaluate the content validity of the global cognition item from the EQ-5D Bolt-on Toolbox and seven additional cognition items from the ‘exploratory’ Toolbox in PD via interviews with patients, caregivers, and clinicians; (2) evaluate the convergent validity, known‑group validity, test–retest reliability, responsiveness, and proxy–patient agreement.Methods:Mixed‑methods. Qualitative cognitive interviews with ~10 patients, ~7 caregivers, and ~7 clinicians following EuroQol topic guide. Quantitative prospective cohort of ~200 PD patient-proxy dyads (patients + their family members) at a tertiary hospital in China (baseline + 3 months follow-up). Measures: EQ‑5D‑5L (with EQ VAS), EQ-5D Bolt-on Toolbox (global cognition item and seven exploratory cognition items), the cognition items of Parkinson’s Disease Questionnaire-39 (PDQ‑39), Movement Disorder Society–Unified Parkinson’s Disease Rating Scale (MDS‑UPDRS), Montreal Cognitive Assessment (MoCA). The EQ-5D Bolt-on Toolbox cognition items are currently being translated to Simplified Chinese under VMC oversight as part of EQ project 2183-RA (PI Xu) and will be available by the start of this project.Findings will provide evidence on the content validity and psychometric performance of cognition bolt-on items in Parkinson’s disease, informing the final selection of cognition bolt-ons for the EQ-5D.
