A complete draft of the book was completed in 2019. The deliverable from the project was a book, published by Springer in 2020, called 'Methods for analysing and reporting EQ-5D data'. Publication of the book was accompanied by a Euroqol-hosted online book launch/event where the authors presented. The book is available open access, and has been widely accessed and cited since its publication: https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-030-47622-9. To date, the book has been downloaded 70k times, and has an altmetric score of 41; in google scholar it has 220 citations.
