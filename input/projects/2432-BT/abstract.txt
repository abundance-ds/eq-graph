Background: Currently, two versions of the EQ-5D Bolt-on Toolbox have been developed (the ‘best available’ and ‘exploratory’ versions). The ‘exploratory’ version of the Toolbox includes a range of other bolt-ons or modifications to the ‘best available’ that will be tested in future work to inform the ‘final’ set of Toolbox bolt-ons. As the Toolbox includes bolt-ons drawn from different sources, and developed following different procedures, there has been no systematic investigation of the bolt-on wording in the source language (UK English) using cognitive debriefing methods.Objectives: To conduct cognitive debriefing of the experimental ‘best available’ and ‘exploratory’ Bolt-on Toolbox in UK English in people who report problems on one (or more) of the bolt-ons. Methods: A two-phase study will be conducted. The first phase of the study will consist of an online survey.  A large sample of participants will complete the EQ-5D-5L and all of the bolt-ons, followed by demographic questions.  The target sample size will be N=500 older adults from the UK general population. Survey responses will be used to identify individuals reporting problems on each of the bolt-ons; these individuals will be invited to participate in an interview. The second phase of the study will consist of qualitative cognitive debriefing interviews. Interview participants will be asked to complete the bolt-on items (for which they had previously reported problems) whilst thinking aloud about how they are completing each question. Follow up questions will also be asked to ensure that relevant, appropriate and comparable information is elicited for each bolt-on, starting with the ‘Best Available’ bolt-on for each concept. The Skin Irritation and Self Confidence bolt-ons will be excluded given the extensive qualitative evidence supporting the current versions and their status as beta items with more than 50 existing translations.
