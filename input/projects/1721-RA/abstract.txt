It is expected that the status of the EQ-5D-Y-5L will be raised to 'approved' in Q2 2024. It is deemed necessary to make value sets available soon after this status change. To be able to deliver a large number of value sets quickly after the official launch of the instrument and at a relatively low cost, the Exec, VWG and YP WG agreed to develop a crosswalk dataset between the Y-5L and Y-3L. A team for conducting a mapping study has been established and has submitted a proposal for that mapping study (ID: 1650-RA). Access to high quality data is required to support mapping between the Y-5L and Y-3L. While some data are already available to support this research, more data are required and therefore the YP WG called for a collaborative effort to conduct parallel collection of Y-5L and Y-3L data in pediatric cohorts in clinical settings. This current proposal presents the offers from potential collaborators, motivates a selection, and asks for funding to support their work.
