A presentation entitled Mixed-Method Exploration of Visual Analogue Scale (VAS) Variations to Measure Health, Social Care, and Carer Related Quality of Life was delivered on November 10th, 2025 as part of the programming for the 2025 ISPOR Europe Annual Meeting in Glasgow, Scottland. The presentation was part of a moderated session entitled "Incorporating Patients' Perspectives in HTA" with three other research presentations. The presentation lasted approximately 12 minutes with 3 minutes for questions from the audience. The presentation was attended by approximately 40 people and was also recorded for future viewing within the digital conference portal. The presentation showcased results from prior research into how the choice of VAS constructs and recall periods influence respondent comprehension of a VAS presented in conjunction with the EQ-HWB. This dissemination was important to highlight the ongoing development of the EQ-HWB, bring awareness to the instrument, and highlight usefulness of a potential VAS with the measure.
