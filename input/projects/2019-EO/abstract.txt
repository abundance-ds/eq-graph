We developed and delivered a 2-hour workshop titled "Measurement and Valuation of Children's Health-Related Quality of Life (HRQoL) for Health Technology Assessment: Methods and Instruments" on 19 Aug 2026 in Singapore, as a preconference workshop for the 12th HTAsianLink conference. We also had several face-to-face meetings with HTAsiaLink leaders and members to explore research collaborations with them at the institution and individual levels.
