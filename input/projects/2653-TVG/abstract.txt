This travel grant will facilitate achievement of three objectives:To give a keynote talk on my research on using EQ-5D PROM data in the context of incentivising value-based care at the annual APERSU end-user conference in Edmonton, Canada. The conference will take place on the 15-16 October 2026. To act as a representative of the EuroQol Executive Committee at the APERSU end-user conference.To spend time with Fatima Al Sayah and team to discuss possible funding applications in support of a new research agenda around value-based care and value-based payments.
