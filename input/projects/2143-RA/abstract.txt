In valuation studies, the interactions between core EQ-5D-5L dimensions are largely ignored: the utility is derived from individual dimensions in additive way. Such approach has pragmatic merits – e.g., a reduced number of parameters – but it is being questioned whether it can safely be extended to bolt-on valuation in view of possibly large overlap between bolt-ons and core dimensions. Meanwhile, a robust estimation of the interaction effects is challenging; as shown in the proposal, the interactions may be confounded with other phenomena, e.g., time preference. We aim to develop a method to test for the presence of interactions between a selected bolt-on and core dimensions using choices involving lotteries. The respondents will make choices from pairs of two-outcome, fair odds lotteries with outcomes being health profiles involving problems with none, exactly one, or two dimensions. The respondents will be interviewed online. Indifference points will be identified to verify how the disutility of combined problems relates to the sum of disutilities of individual problems, for instance, if disutility of 31111 health state plus the disutility of 13111 health state is equal to the disutility of 33111 (hence, if the dimensions interact in preferential sense). Based on test power calculations, we plan to recruit 130 respondents via Prolific. Additionally, to put the possible interactions between core dimensions and the bolt-on in a context, we will add tasks to elicit the interactions within the core dimensions.
