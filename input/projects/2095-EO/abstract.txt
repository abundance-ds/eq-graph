This education and outreach project explored targeted support mechanisms for the International Academy of Health Preference Research (IAHPR) 2025 meeting, with the aim of increasing visibility and engagement of EuroQol within the health preference research community. EuroQol’s support was formally acknowledged throughout the conference, including sponsor recognition in the programme and presentation materials, verbal acknowledgements by session chairs during relevant sessions, and a post-conference LinkedIn communication. Registration data confirmed uptake of the EuroQol free registration code by two participants, with an estimated additional six to seven EuroQol members attending the meeting, although precise numbers were difficult to ascertain due to multiple institutional affiliations. Overall, the grant supported enhanced awareness of EuroQol among attendees and strengthened links between EuroQol and the IAHPR community.
