This pilot seed grant study describes the process of implementing the EQ-5D in an Intensive Care setting and the differences EQ-5D responses between proxies. We report the pitfalls for implementation in this specific clinical setting and show that 217 out of 762 eligible patients could be included, resulting in 270 EQ-5D assessments. There are small but distinct differences between answers from the patient and the proxies (family members, physicians, general practitioners). When combining the logistical challenges of contacting healthcare professionals and the results of our study, family members appeared to be the most feasible and accurate proxies for estimating pre-admission QoL in incapacitated patients.
