The standard reference period of the EQ-5D is ‘your health today’. However, the ‘true’ reference period that is used by the respondent when filling out the EQ-5D may be shorter or longer than ‘your health today’, or may focus on specific (peak) health experiences in that time period. The aims of this study were to determine the actual reference period that was used by trauma patients when thinking of 'your health today' and the heuristic "aggregation of time" rule that was used to combine the full experience of that actual reference period in one overall 'signifying' number or level. A total of 531 trauma patients completed the 1 week, 3 month and 12 month questionnaires. The 3 and 12 month questionnaires included the EQ-5D-3L and cognitive debriefing questions. The most frequently reported reference time span when reporting health status was either "the last 24 hours" or "today". Most patients reported their health as an average moment. Age and education level were key factors influencing the reference period and heuristic rule, respectively. These findings highlight the importance of considering time reference and individual factors, such as age and education level, when interpreting EQ-5D data.
