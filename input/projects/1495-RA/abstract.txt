This qualitative study aimed to examine the content validity of the EQ-5D in a sample of Chinese patients with Duchenne muscular dystrophy (DMD). The research team collaborated with Beijing Dear Mom & Dad DMD patient centre, China's largest DMD patient organization, to recruit participants. Thirty participants—15 adult patients and 15 children and adolescent patients—who met the inclusion criteria were invited to participate in web-based one-on-one, in-depth interviews. The interviews included concept elicitation to identify patient-perceived DMD impact, and cognitive debriefing to assess the relevance and acceptability of the EQ-5D-5L and EQ-5D-Y, respectively. Overall, DMD patients' health and quality of life were affected in three areas: physical health, mental well-being, and social relationships. Nearly all patients agreed that the EQ-5D represents most aspects affecting their quality of life, but it may lack components such as social relationships. Moreover, items related to physical health were not very appropriate for patients with DMD, which could affect the use of EQ-5D in this population, especially for adult patients.
