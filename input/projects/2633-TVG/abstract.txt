This travel grant is on behalf of the YPWG and DSWG who coordinated the topics, speakers and abstract submission for two symposia at the 33rd Annual ISOQOL Conference (18-21 October 2026, Prague, Czech Republic). These symposia are respectively titled:- Challenges and triumphs of multi-national generic patient-reported outcome (PRO) measure development (Focus on EQ-HWB-9 and EQ-TIPS).- Introducing the EQ-5D Bolt-on Toolbox: The EuroQol Groups' new approach to the development, valuation and use of bolt-ons to the EQ-5D-5L
