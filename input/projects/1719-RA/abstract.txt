This study investigates an extended version of the EQ-5D-5L in a cohort of health care workers and medical workers, who reported a work-related COVID-19-infection. About 20,000 randomly selected persons from an ongoing research project funded by the German Federal Ministry of Education and Research will answer an online-questionnaire including the EQ-5D-5L, two bolt-on items (cognition, social participation, EQ-5D+), and several other health- and infection-related questions such as vaccination status, present health state and health state 12 months before the most severe COVID-19 infection, Long COVID symptoms (bolt-on-like items), chronic conditions, ability to work, need for rehabilitation. Persons with ongoing symptoms will be surveyed after a 12 month follow-up, which allows for longitudinal analysis of the EQ-5D+ and other variables. Psychometric analysis of the EQ-5D+ comprise analyses of:  distributional properties (i.e., ceiling of EQ-5D-5L, EQ-5D+, each single dimension, and bolt-on-like items) feasibility (proportion of missing responses on each dimension and bolt-on(-like) item) construct validity (correlation with other measures supposed to assess a (dis-)similar construct) known groups (comparisons between subgroups according to level distribution of responses across dimensions and the mean EQ VAS with formulated hypothesis regarding differences between groups) responsiveness of EQ-5D, bolt-on(-like) items, EQ VAS, EQ-5D index values, in the subgroup of HCWs and medical workers with ongoing symptoms dimensional dependency  multivariable regression analysis on the impact of the 5 dimensions, and bolt-on(-like) items and to predict group membership (e.g., persons with and without Long COVID) Results will be compared with other COVID-19 cohorts (e.g., POPCORN, CORFU) and will gain insight into the suitability of two candidate bolt-ons and bolt-on-like items in a German COVID-19 population, presumably as well as infectious diseases in general.
