We prepared an issue panel for discussing the necessity of using culture-specific preference-based measure versus generic PBMs in the ISPOR real world evidence conference in Tokyo, Japan on 30 September 2025. After the issue panel proposal was accepted, we had several zoom meetings to discuss the content and structure of the 1-hour issue panel. The final issue panel consisted of three presentations and a general discussion with audience. The first presentations provided information about two Asian culture-specific PBMs, namely, CHROM-G for China and AP-7D for Asia.
