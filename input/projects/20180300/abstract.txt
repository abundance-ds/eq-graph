The aim of this study was to develop vision and cognition bolt-ons for the EQ-5D-5L. As part of this work, we also developed a multi stage standardised bolt-on development and testing protocol that was adapted for each construct, and can be adapted for future bolt-on work. The development protocol includes four stages. These are: Development of bolt-on descriptors using literature reviews and focus groups Qualitative assessment and further refinement of draft bolt-on descriptors  Quantitative analysis of descriptors Triangulation of results to inform bolt-on selection and recommendations We followed this development protocol, with some adaptations, for both vision and cognition. This is described in more detail below. Development work was conducted in both the UK and Australia. The set of descriptors we developed at Stage 2 and during pilot work for Stage 3 were highly influential in the development of the vision and cognition elements of the best available and exploratory bolt-on Toolbox.  The vision candidates we developed focused on measuring vision and sight, with and without inclusion of aids, and using both problem and difficulty framing. A vision related activity item was also developed. For cognition, we developed items assessing overall cognition and a range of aspects of the overall construct, for example memory and concentration. Alignment with the development of the Toolbox meant that the full Stage 3 data collection could include both the best available and exploratory Toolbox candidates in the survey. Therefore this work directly informs both the initial development of the toolbox, and the subsequent testing, and potential refinement of, the candidates. Results suggest positive classical psychometric properties. IRT analyses support their use as general indicators of vision related QoL impairments.
