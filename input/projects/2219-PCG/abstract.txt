Kemri-Wellcome Trust Research Programme and Euroqol Research Foundation set up a collaboration agreement, to establish the necessary arrangements for a synergistic partnership between the Parties in areas of mutual interest in order to promote public health in Africa through enhancing capacity of KWTRP to address public health challenges and ultimately promote the achievement of Africa’s health and wellness goals. The scope of the collaboration between Parties consists of the following: • Develop EQ-5D-5L value sets for selected countries (Kenya and Zambia) and an EQ-5D-Y-3L value set for Nigeria • Build capacity for African researchers and policy audiences to implement the collection and use of data from EQ-5D instruments.
