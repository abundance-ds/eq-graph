The study collected EQ-5D-5L and 3L from the UK population in an online survey conducted in the early weeks of the coronavirus pandemic in 2 waves about a month apart. These data were compared with EQ-5D-5L data collected as part of the Health Survey for England (2017) survey. Additional 3L data were collected during both waves, including a modified form derived from 5L. The second wave also fielded EQ-HWB. Spin-off activity from this study continues in other derivative studies.
