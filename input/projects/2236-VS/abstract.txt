This study aims to develop a Nigeria-specific QALY value set using EQ-5D-Y-3L instruments for children and adolescents, incorporating societal preferences for various health states. The societal preferences in sample selection will consider age, sex, geographical locations (as per EuroQol criteria), and income level of parents/guardians. Data will be collected across six states, strategically selected to represent the six geopolitical zones in Nigeria. This approach will ensure national representation by accounting for the diversity across Nigeria geopolitical zones and economic strata. We will employ composite Time-Trade Off (cTTO) and Discrete Choice Experiment (DCE) tasks to elicit health state utilities, after deploying EQ-5D-Y-3L and EQ-VAS to assess the respondents’ quality of life. The adoption of a hybrid approach is intended to validate the valuation process, as the resulting value set will enhance the validity of cost-effectiveness analyses to a valid and relevant basis for evaluating healthcare interventions and policies within the Nigerian context. This will ultimately contribute to more efficient and equitable healthcare resource allocation in the country.
