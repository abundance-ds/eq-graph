This project was not a research project in the conventional meaning, with relevant research questions to be addressed by data. Instead, the primary goal was to facilitate/support implementation of a digital survey system to collect data from patients with long covid. This project (645-RA) was tied to the so-called CORFU study, funded by ZONMW in The Netherlands. Corfu can be described as a prospective cohort study on the scope and severity of post-COVID symptoms across diverse patient groups in the Netherlands-insights from the CORFU study. A connection between CORFU and EuroQol was made in project 645-RA to ensure that a survey instrument and data collection structure developed in context of other projects (popcorn and 512-RA) could also be leveraged in this ambitious project.
