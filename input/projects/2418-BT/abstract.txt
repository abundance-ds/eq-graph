We propose to test breathing and fatigue bolt-ons in the main toolbox and exploratory toolbox in lung, blood, and gastrointestinal cancers. In this multi-centred study, we will consecutively recruit 200 non-small cell lung cancer patients, 200 lymphoma patients, and 200 gastric/colorectal cancer patients who will receive active anticancer treatment in three different tertiary cancer centres in China. All eligible patients will complete EQ-5D-5L and 10 fatigue bolt-on variants before and after treatment, together with cancer-specific QLQ-C30 and a fatigue scale. Lung cancer patients will also complete the QLQ-C30 lung cancer module (LC13) and 2 breathing bolt-on variants. Patients' clinical characteristics and performance status before and after treatment will be evaluated or retrieved from medical records. We will evaluate the distributions, ceiling/floor effects, content validity, convergent validity, known-groups validity, and responsiveness of the breathing and fatigue bolt-ons. We will also evaluate fatigue bolt-ons by pooling data collected from the three cancer groups.
