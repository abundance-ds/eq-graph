Background: The EuroQol Group has expressed interest in adding a visual analogue scale (VAS) to the EQ-HWB, the only EuroQol instrument not to have a VAS at present. Previous quantitative research on potential EQ-HWB VAS versions has only asked people to rate their own health and/or wellbeing. This means it is not possible to observe the underlying state being rated, nor is it possible to see how scales respond to changes in health and non-health aspects of life. We will address this lack of evidence by asking survey participants to rate hypothetical vignettes using a range of potential VAS versions, in addition to their own situation.Aims: To compare how individuals rate vignettes of health and non-health aspects using EQ VAS and several alternative VAS versions. We will assess whether these alternatives possess two key properties indicating they capture a broader concept than the EQ VAS:Participants should rate vignettes differently using an alternative VAS compared to the EQ VAS.Ratings using an alternative VAS should be more sensitive to changes in non-health aspects of the vignettes compared to the EQ VAS.We will also examine the suitability of the EQ VAS for use with the EQ-HWB-9 by assessing whether it captures the entire EQ-HWB-9 construct.Methods: Vignettes will feature three aspects: health, described using an EQ-5D-5L state; loneliness, described using the EQ-HWB-9 item; and financial situation, described using the wording of a widely used survey item. Each aspect will have three levels: no problems, moderate problems, and extreme problems.Online survey participants will rate a series of vignettes, as well as their own situation, using the EQ VAS and three alternative VAS versions measuring (i) health and wellbeing, (ii) wellbeing, and (iii) quality of life.Whether vignette ratings differ between VAS versions will be assessed using Mann-Whitney U tests, Wilcoxon signed-rank tests, and Tobit regression models. The extent to which each VAS version captures the EQ-HWB-9 construct will be assessed using Tobit models with VAS self-ratings as dependent variables and EQ-HWB-9 responses as independent variables.Recommendations about the next steps in adopting a VAS for the EQ-HWB-9 will be provided based on the project’s results.
