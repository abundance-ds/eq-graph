Health Survey of England (HSE) data were  used  to explore the relationship between AD responses and (a) a standard measure of mental health (GHQ-12) and (b) a recognized measure of wellbeing (WEMWBS). Respondent-level data obtained in a nurse-led intereview conducted as part of the HSE survey, were used to create empirically defined categories of "caseness" which were used to verify psychometric performance of both these measures and dichotomised AD responses. The study also examined the feasibility of synthesizing AD responses using respondent-level covariate data. The study results show that AD performs well as a single-item indicator of depression “caseness” – outperforming both GHQ-12 and WEMWBS. However, analysis of potential association with anxiety proved infeasible. Most importantly, AD demonstrates a strong association with negative-worded items in GHQ-12, suggestive of it measuring psychological distress. However, the results of multidimensional analysis places AD centrally between GHQ-12 and WEMWBS, and lends support for identifying AD as a hybrid construct.
