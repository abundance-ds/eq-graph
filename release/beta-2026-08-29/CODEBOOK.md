## Identifiers and joins

- `publication_id` is `doi:<DOI>` when a DOI exists, otherwise an opaque key. `publications.doi` holds the bare DOI.
- `study_id` and every item id (`finding_id`, `use_id`, `sample_id`, ...) start with the paper key: `P00088d2d4963:S1`, `P00088d2d4963:F1`. Use ids only for joins.
- `person_id` prefixes show the origin: `person:openalex:`, `person:eqmember:`, `person:leader:`, `person:orcid:`, `person:author:`, `group:author:`.
- `registry_id` is `<type>:<slug>`, for example `instrument:eq-5d-5l`. `registry_entities.parent_registry_id` links a variant to its parent.
- Multi-value cells in the analysis files are joined with `|` and sorted.
- `*_json` columns hold JSON; their keys are listed under each table.

## Counting rules

- A project-publication link exists only when the full text confirmed it; a missing link is not evidence of no relation.
- One primary research family per study (`study_types`). Purposes, countries, instruments, and methods are multi-valued; totals exceed the study count.
- Filter `scientific_uses` on `context` before counting. `DIRECT_CURRENT_ACTIVITY` and `CURRENT_STUDY_OBJECT` mean used or examined by the study; `DISCUSSION_ONLY`, `SOURCE_STUDY_ACTIVITY`, `PLANNED_ACTIVITY`, and `INPUT_DATA_PROVENANCE` do not.
- Aggregate on `canonical_label` or `registry_id`, not `source_label`.
- `NOT_REPORTED`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, `UNMAPPED_VALUE`, and gap rows are explicit states, not scientific categories.
- Citation counts are one OpenAlex snapshot, dated in `publication_citations.retrieved_at`. `NOT_FOUND` rows carry no count.
- A language model extracted findings and limitations under the ontology; deterministic validation checked them. They are selected, source-faithful, not verbatim, and not exhaustive.
