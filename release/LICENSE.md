## Third-party content

The grant covers EQ-Graph's structure, extraction, resolution, and curation.
Reproduced text and records keep their source terms:

- Publication titles, abstracts, author keywords, and funding statements: Crossref, Europe PMC, and OpenAlex; property of their publishers and authors.
- Project titles, abstracts, investigators, working groups, budgets, and years: the EuroQol Research Foundation's funded-projects table.
- Citation counts: OpenAlex.
- Person identifiers: ORCID and OpenAlex. Memberships: the EuroQol Group's members list.

Findings, limitations, interpretations, and all typed evidence are source-faithful paraphrases under the EQ-Graph ontology, not verbatim quotations.
They do not replace the original publication.

## People

Researchers appear with the public identifiers and affiliations printed on publications, ORCID, OpenAlex, and the EuroQol members list.
The database records professional roles only.
Write to the project team to correct or remove a personal record.
