# Frozen retrieval and comparison probes

Status: frozen operator record, 2026-08-16; excluded from development agent context.

These probes test whether a lineage makes useful distinctions in its paper applications.
They do not prescribe ontology fields.
Comparison records missed, ambiguous and false matches without a combined score.

1. Find the papers that use DCE with duration as a primary valuation method and distinguish them from standard DCE, cTTO, PTO and EQ VAS work.
   Expected focal papers: `10.1016/j.jval.2024.05.016` and `10.1177/0272989x251325828`.
2. Find the paper that compares unsupervised online cTTO with supervised face-to-face cTTO and state the material mode differences.
   Expected focal paper: `10.1007/s11136-020-02712-1`.
3. Find papers with instrument translation or language adaptation and distinguish instrument language from article language and study country.
   Expected focal papers include `10.1186/s41687-025-00985-z` and `10.1186/s12955-024-02290-7`.
4. Find papers where adults, proxies or caregivers report on or value another person's health and distinguish respondent, referent, perspective and target population.
   Expected focal papers include `10.1007/s40273-022-01216-9`, `10.1007/s11136-025-04150-3` and `10.1186/s12955-022-01996-w`.
5. Find papers that use the same instrument family in different roles, such as study target, comparator, data source, scoring input or output.
   The answer must not treat every instrument mention as the same use.
6. Find the Graves' disease psychometric study and state which properties had stronger or weaker support without reducing the result to one validity label.
   Expected focal paper: `10.1186/s12955-023-02177-z`.
7. Find the study that maps source measures to utilities and then uses them in QALY and cost-effectiveness analysis.
   Distinguish direct responses, mapped utilities, QALYs and modeled results.
   Expected focal paper: `10.1007/s10198-018-0987-x`.
8. For a valuation paper, identify the valuation task, protocol, health-state design, administration mode and main statistical model, and explain the purpose of the model.
   Use `10.1007/s40258-021-00639-3` and `10.1016/j.jval.2025.01.003` as checks.
9. Distinguish a protocol or planned product from a completed, selected or implemented output without using one broad completion label.
   Expected focal papers include `10.1136/bmjopen-2025-100897`, `10.1007/s11136-025-03983-2` and `10.1186/s12955-024-02266-7`.
10. Retrieve the principal finding, author interpretation, limitation and stated future work for a paper without returning every reported estimate.
    Use `10.1007/s11136-025-04074-y` and `10.1007/s11136-025-03996-x` as checks.
11. Distinguish author-reported implications, documented use and extractor inference.
    Do not convert expected or proposed impact into observed impact.
12. Find papers that reuse or extend the same respondents, value set, protocol or analytic input and distinguish a new contribution from independent evidence.
    Use the Trinidad and Tobago and EQ-DAPHNIE paper groups as checks.
13. Find source conflicts, unclear procedures and transfer limits without merging them into the authors' conclusions.
14. Use the populated applications to identify a corpus-bounded gap by a combination of instrument, language, population, method or setting.
    Do not present absence in the selected papers as absence from all research.
