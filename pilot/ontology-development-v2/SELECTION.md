# Paper-selection record

Status: operator record; not part of development agent context.

## Development rounds one to three

Reuse the exact 30 papers and order from version 1.
This permits a controlled comparison of the open architecture task and the fixed paper-first task.

The set covers value sets, instrument development, psychometrics, reviews, HTA practice, population norms, inequality, qualitative work, implementation, data quality, health economics and burden work.
It has weaker coverage of PTO, direct EQ VAS valuation, administration-mode comparisons, proxy versus self-report, language-version management, detailed mapping chains and observed implementation.

## Round-four calibration

Ten additional papers were selected before the version-2 run to exercise the weak areas:

- PTO protocol with online, think-aloud and focus-group work;
- experience-based EQ VAS valuation;
- unsupervised online versus supervised face-to-face cTTO;
- youth proxy versus self-report;
- English language adaptation in Singapore;
- online personal utility functions for EQ-HWB-S;
- EQ-TIPS development with kaizen and paired-choice DCE tasks;
- EQ-VT versus crosswalk value-set comparison;
- uncertainty propagation from health-state values to QALYs and cost effectiveness;
- co-designed clinical use of EQ-5D-Y-5L.

These labels support selection only.
They do not appear in the agent manifest.

## Holdout

The version-1 holdout remains a historical input.
Version 2 uses a new ten-paper purpose-stratified holdout from project groups that do not occur in development or calibration.
It contains only EuroQol-related or directly relevant domain papers.

The ten papers test:

- Moroccan EQ-5D-5L valuation with cTTO, DCE and hybrid modeling;
- Indonesian youth-value mapping from DCE to TTO;
- Amharic youth self-report and caregiver-proxy psychometrics;
- Chichewa child and teen instrument translation and adaptation;
- qualitative EQ-HWB proxy completion by dementia carers;
- routine quality-of-life measurement in residential aged care;
- caregiver-proxy instrument development and Rasch analysis;
- longitudinal EQ-5D and cancer outcomes;
- tele-TTO compared with personal TTO;
- systematic review and meta-analysis of EQ-5D during COVID-19.

All ten project groups are unique.
They are disjoint from the 40 development and calibration papers by project group.
