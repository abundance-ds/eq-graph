# Harmonization direction

Status: awaiting human approval.

The anonymous comparison found that all three architectures fit the 30 development papers.
The papers do not select one architecture.

## Recommended direction

Use a compact paper-first meta-research description.
Keep the measurement or derivation chain as an optional provenance structure, not as the main ontology backbone.

- Use paper-local study components when phases, samples, tasks or outputs differ.
- Represent a comparison as a relation with essential alignment context by default.
- Keep response integrity, sample fitness and analytic adequacy as related but distinct facets.
- Keep funding, institutional relations, conflicts and data or code access as optional meta-research context.
- Use a small inferential-aim vocabulary and typed uncertainty.
- Preserve the shortest material derivation chain when an output depends on mapping, scoring or modeling.
- Preserve paper terms for constructs. Do not impose a construct hierarchy yet.
- Do not define universal transfer, agreement, quality or representativeness thresholds.
- Keep a brief result summary. Do not create a detailed claim-evidence-finding structure.

This direction is an instruction for harmonization. It is not the final ontology.
