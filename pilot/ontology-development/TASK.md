# Common ontology agent task

Develop or extend a simple, practical ontology that helps researchers describe and compare the assigned papers for meta-research.

Use your judgment.
Apply the ontology to the papers as you work.
Add, remove, rename, merge or split elements when this improves the ontology.
Do not force information into the current structure.

Keep the ontology intelligible to a researcher.
Record its application to each paper, important changes, and unresolved cases.
Connect important decisions to the papers that motivated them.
Keep distinctions only when they help researchers use or compare the papers.

Do not create a detailed claim-evidence representation of study results.
Do not reconstruct bibliographic or other metadata that the supplied structured record already contains.
Flag conflicts, gaps or uncertainty in the supplied metadata.

Use clear Markdown.
Choose the structure that best communicates the work.
You can add another representation or a small script if it is useful.

Work only from this lineage's current files and the assigned batch.
Do not inspect another lineage.
Do not inspect prior project ontologies, graph schemas, legacy extractions or files outside the supplied worktree.
Treat the supplied task, structured records and papers as the complete research context.
State if the task or supplied material limits the work.
