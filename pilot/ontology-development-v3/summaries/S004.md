## Aim, setting and sample

The study developed a value set based on preferences of the UAE general adult population. The UAE has seven emirates and a distinctive population: about 11.8% Emirati nationals and 88.2% expatriates. Eligible participants were adults aged 18 years or older who were Emirati nationals or non-nationals resident in the UAE for at least five years and able to complete the interview in Arabic or English. Two-stage quota sampling stratified by emirate, age and sex. Recruitment used networks, snowball sampling and public locations. Practice interviews were conducted November–December 2022 and main data collection January–August 2023. Of 1145 interviews, 1005 respondents were included after 140 practice interviews were excluded. The Results report mean age 32.1 years (SD 11.4), 44.4% female and 11.4% nationals; the abstract reports average age 39 years (SD 10.8), 44.5% female and 11% nationals. This age discrepancy is source-reported and unclear (Abstract; Sampling; General Characteristics).

The UAE Arabic EQ-5D-5L version was used. English-speaking participants used the UK-validated English version because no UAE-specific English version was available. EQ-5D-5L contains mobility, self-care, usual activities, pain/discomfort and anxiety/depression, five severity levels and 3125 health states; EQ VAS ranges 0–100 (Descriptive System; Data Collection).

## Elicitation and quality control

Interviews used EQ-VT and were interviewer-administered face-to-face or online. cTTO used conventional TTO for BTD states and lead-time TTO for WTD states, followed by a feedback module. Ten cTTO health states were valued, including wheelchair examples and three practice states. DCE consisted of seven paired comparisons from 196 pairs in 28 blocks. Interview materials were translated into Arabic; in-person and online formats were combined after QC found minimal differences (Preference Elicitation; Data Collection).

Fourteen interviewers received two-day training, five practice interviews and online review. QC officers reviewed compliance, interviewer effects and face validity. Data collection was paused at 25%, 50% and 75% for interim review. Four of seven retained interviewers were fluent in Arabic and English. The paper reports that 1018 cTTO responses were flagged in the feedback module; 285 participants had at least one inconsistency, reduced to 160 after feedback (Interviewer Training and QC; Results).

## Models and value set

Ten models were compared: random-intercept cTTO; random-intercept Tobit cTTO censored at −1; heteroskedastic cTTO; heteroskedastic no-constant cTTO; heteroskedastic Tobit no-constant cTTO; DCE conditional logit; standard hybrid; hybrid Tobit censored at −1; hybrid heteroskedastic; and hybrid heteroskedastic Tobit censored at −1. Models used 20 main-effect dummy variables (levels 2–5 for each dimension); hybrid models estimated a scaling parameter to align cTTO and DCE. Sensitivity analyses used all observations, removed 55555 inconsistencies, removed feedback-flagged values, or removed both groups (Data Analysis and Model Selection).

The preferred model was the hybrid heteroskedastic Tobit model censored at −1. It used both elicitation methods, addressed cTTO censoring and heteroskedasticity, and had comparable RMSE/MAE with other Tobit models while outperforming them on AIC/BIC. Predicted utilities ranged from −0.654 for 55555 to 0.962 for 11211, with 11111 fixed at 1. The dimension ranking was mobility, pain/discomfort, anxiety/depression, self-care and usual activities. Table 3 gives examples of level coefficients: MO2 0.075, MO3 0.100, MO4 0.261, MO5 0.438; SC2 0.057, SC3 0.082, SC4 0.196, SC5 0.268; UA2 0.038, UA3 0.062, UA4 0.159, UA5 0.232; PD2 0.047, PD3 0.072, PD4 0.261, PD5 0.359; AD2 0.062, AD3 0.129, AD4 0.268, AD5 0.357. A state is scored by subtracting applicable coefficients from 1 (Preferred Model and Value Set; Table 3).

Of 10,050 cTTO and 7035 DCE responses, 25.7% of cTTO values were considered worse than dead. Values clustered at −1.0, −0.5, 0, 0.5, and 1.0 at 7.6%, 3.9%, 2.9%, 7.4%, and 12.5%, respectively. The mean observed cTTO value across the 86 states was 0.303 (SD 0.657), ranging from −0.538 for 55555 to 0.948 for the very mild states (21111, 12111, 11211, 11121, 11112). Quality parameters showed minimal differences between Arabic and English and between in-person and online interviews, so the data were combined. The sample matched age, sex, emirate and nationality quotas, but illiterate, rural and low-literacy participants were under-represented. Limitations include use of the UK English EQ-5D-5L version, the unvalidated five-year expatriate criterion, language/context effects and the dynamic nationals/expatriates population. The intended use is local cost-effectiveness analysis, population health assessment and PROM applications (Data Collection; Results; Discussion; Conclusions).
