# B02: Chichewa PedsQL cultural adaptation and validation

## Identity and study type

- DOI: `10.1186/s41687-024-00761-5`.
- Purposes: cross-cultural adaptation; translation; measurement-property
  evaluation.
- Design: two-stage adaptation and cross-sectional psychometric study.
- EuroQol evidence: an author reports EuroQol project `20190200` funding. The
  paper does not use an EQ instrument for the main reported analyses.

## Population and samples

- Target: Chichewa-speaking children and adolescents in Malawi.
- Cognitive interviews: ten healthy participants, five for each age version.
- Psychometric sample: 289 literate participants aged 8 to 17 years in
  Blantyre; 98 child-version and 191 teen-version completions.
- Recruitment included school and hospital settings; 95 participants were
  healthy, 155 had an acute condition, and 39 had a chronic condition.

## Concepts and themes

- child and adolescent health; cross-cultural comparability; linguistic and
  conceptual equivalence; low- and middle-income countries; health literacy;
  self-completion burden.

## Instruments and administration

- PedsQL 4.0 Generic Core Scales child self-report, Chichewa (Malawi), ages 8
  to 12.
- PedsQL 4.0 Generic Core Scales teen self-report, Chichewa (Malawi), ages 13
  to 17 in this study.
- Source versions: US English child and teen self-report forms.
- Twenty-three items; five response levels; total, physical, psychosocial, and
  four subscale scores.
- Participants self-completed paper questionnaires in a classroom or after
  clinical care. Some needed a clear explanation of the instructions.

## Methods, protocol, and task design

- Standard PedsQL forward and back translation with reconciliation.
- Iterative one-to-one cognitive interviews tested item meaning and proposed
  wording.
- Mapi Research Trust approved the pre-final versions.
- Item analysis tested missingness, extreme and adjacent endorsement,
  redundancy, and item-scale relations.
- Reliability and convergent, discriminant, and known-groups validity were
  assessed.

## Analysis and statistical models

- Cronbach alpha; item and subscale correlations; independent-sample t-tests;
  ANOVA; Cohen effect sizes; modified multitrait-multimethod ratios.
- Analyses were separate for the child and teen forms.

## Products

- Chichewa (Malawi) PedsQL 4.0 GCS child self-report: culturally adapted and
  psychometrically evaluated.
- Chichewa (Malawi) PedsQL 4.0 GCS teen self-report: culturally adapted and
  psychometrically evaluated.

## Outcomes or measurement properties

- Linguistic and conceptual equivalence; feasibility; missing data; response
  distribution; redundancy; internal consistency; convergent validity;
  discriminant validity; known-groups validity; item-scale validity.

## Principal findings and interpretation

- Six items required cultural adaptation. Cognitive interviews found that the
  first translation of `take a bath or shower` could refer to menstruation.
- Some younger participants selected every response box until an interviewer
  explained the task. Missingness and sparse adjacent response use also caused
  concern.
- Cronbach alpha values were 0.84 to 0.94 for subscales and summary scores; the
  total-score values exceeded 0.94.
- Convergent validity was generally strong. Discriminant and known-groups
  results were mixed.
- The authors recommend cautious use in Malawi and further work on content,
  test-retest reliability, and responsiveness.

## Limitations and source issues

- Cognitive adaptation included healthy participants only.
- COVID-19 restrictions limited collection to one time point. Test-retest
  reliability and responsiveness were not assessed.
- The funding statement is at author level. It does not state that the
  EuroQol grant funded the full study. The portfolio link must keep that exact
  support scope.
