# B18: Korean public concepts of health and EQ-5D

## Identity and study type

- DOI: `10.3961/jpmph.22.151`.
- Purposes: content-validity research; qualitative concept elicitation;
  cultural interpretation.
- Design: face-to-face, semi-structured, in-depth qualitative interviews.
- The article and JATS metadata report no EuroQol funding. Any project link
  needs separate authoritative portfolio evidence.

## Population and samples

- Korean general public, purposively sampled.
- Twenty-two people contributed; two helped develop the guide and 20 completed
  the main interviews.
- Main sample: ten women and ten men; ages from 30s or younger to 60s or older;
  16 urban and four rural residents; seven with a disease.

## Concepts and themes

- meaning of health; physical, mental, and social health; cultural relevance;
  family relationships; dementia; vitality; positive health; content validity;
  scale interpretation.

## Instruments and administration

- Korean EQ-5D-5L and EQ VAS were shown for comment, not used as quantitative
  outcome measures.
- Individual, face-to-face interviews in a quiet public room or participant
  home; 30 to 60 minutes.

## Methods, protocol, and task design

- COREQ-guided semi-structured interview guide.
- Explored health concepts, important health problems, EQ-5D-5L content, and
  EQ VAS use.
- Recorded and transcribed interviews; participant and external checks
  supported credibility.

## Analysis and statistical models

- Directed content analysis of transcripts and field notes.
- One researcher coded, a second checked categories, and the team assessed
  saturation.
- No statistical model.

## Products

- Cultural content evidence and candidate improvement topics for EQ-5D and EQ
  VAS; no revised instrument or bolt-on was produced.

## Outcomes or measurement properties

- Perceived relevance, comprehensibility, content coverage, response-scale use,
  and conceptual meaning of health.

## Principal findings and interpretation

- Participants saw health as physical, mental, and social, with physical health
  as the base requirement.
- Social relationships, vitality, and positive mental state were important.
  Dementia was often the most concerning condition.
- EQ-5D-5L was generally simple, understandable, and relevant; usual activities
  was often seen as especially important.
- Participants proposed more social and mental content.
- EQ VAS was visually acceptable, but participants questioned between-person
  comparability, viewed 100 as nearly unreachable, and preferred five- or
  ten-point increments and more reference labels.

## Limitations and source issues

- A small purposive qualitative sample does not give population prevalence.
- Proposed extra dimensions and scale changes are interpretations, not tested
  instrument revisions.
- Folder placement alone cannot establish funding.
