# B15: CREATE valuation-study reporting checklist

## Identity and study type

- DOI: `10.1007/s40273-015-0292-9`.
- Purposes: reporting-guideline development; valuation-method research;
  research-quality improvement.
- Design: modified two-round Delphi process with expert panel and member survey.
- EuroQol support: study support reported in the Funding section.

## Population and samples

- Expert panel: five health-utility researchers.
- Survey: 16 EuroQol Research Foundation members from eight countries; half
  were women.
- Target users: developers, reviewers, and users of valuation studies for
  multi-attribute utility-based instruments.

## Concepts and themes

- transparent reporting; critical appraisal; reproducibility; value-set
  quality; reporting bias; health utility measurement; reimbursement decisions.

## Instruments and administration

- The work covers EQ-5D, SF-6D, HUI, AQoL, and other MAUIs.
- No health instrument was administered to measure participant health.
- Candidate checklist ratings were collected by email survey.

## Methods, protocol, and task design

- Began with 35 items from a systematic review of EQ-5D valuation reporting.
- Expert review reduced the set to 26 candidate items.
- Survey respondents rated items required, recommended, or optional.
- Majority rule and final panel deliberation produced the checklist.

## Analysis and statistical models

- Categorical consensus counts and threshold rule; no statistical model.

## Products

- CREATE: 21-item Checklist for REporting VAluaTion StudiEs.
- Seven sections: descriptive system, health states valued, sampling,
  preference-data collection, study sample, modeling, and scoring algorithm.

## Outcomes or measurement properties

- Item retention; consensus; checklist content and intended use.

## Principal findings and interpretation

- Twenty-two candidate items exceeded the initial majority threshold. Final
  deliberation produced 21 items.
- CREATE is method-oriented rather than organized by generic article sections.
- It aims to improve reporting, appraisal, design, and defensibility of value
  sets.
- The authors propose use for generic and disease-specific utility instruments
  and expect updates as methods change.

## Limitations and source issues

- The survey was small and included only EuroQol members.
- The categorical majority threshold was arbitrary.
- The checklist reflects the theory and methods available at publication time.
- The Markdown conversion duplicates several headings and paragraphs. The JATS
  source should control deterministic structure and de-duplication.
