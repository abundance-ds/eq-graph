# B16: retracted Egyptian EQ-5D-5L valuation article

## Identity and study type

- DOI: `10.1007/s40273-021-01002-z`.
- Publication status: retracted. The local title states this, but the local JATS
  record does not supply a retraction notice, reason, or date.
- Purposes: value-set development; valuation study.
- Design: national cross-sectional, interviewer-administered valuation survey.
- EuroQol support: study funding, project `20180130`.

## Population and samples

- Target: adult Egyptian general population with quotas for age, sex, and
  geographic distribution.
- Interviews: 1,378; pilot: 216; final analysis: 974; incomplete or
  noncompliant exclusions: 188.
- Rural and illiterate people were underrepresented. COVID-19 stopped data
  collection before all quotas were filled.

## Concepts and themes

- national value set; Arabic-language valuation; cultural adaptation; states
  worse than dead; interviewer quality control; health technology assessment;
  retracted evidence.

## Instruments and administration

- EQ-5D-5L and EQ VAS; Egyptian Arabic EQ-VT software 2.1.
- Computer-assisted, face-to-face, interviewer-administered survey.
- Participants first reported own health, then valued hypothetical EQ-5D-5L
  states.
- Visual aids and interviewer reading supported participants with low literacy.

## Methods, protocol, and task design

- EQ-VT 2.1; cTTO and DCE; feedback module; EuroQol quality-control tool.
- Ten cTTO states and seven DCE pairs per respondent.
- Pilot replaced the wheelchair example with migraine after cultural testing.

## Analysis and statistical models

- cTTO: generalized least squares, Tobit, and heteroskedastic interval-regression
  models.
- DCE: conditional logit model.
- Combined data: four hybrid models.
- Model selection used logical consistency, significance, AIC, BIC, RMSE, MAE,
  prediction range, censoring, and heteroskedasticity.

## Products

- Retracted article's claimed Egyptian EQ-5D-5L value set.
- Status: not approved for operational scoring or evidence synthesis while the
  retraction status is unresolved.

## Outcomes or measurement properties

- Health-state utility; dimension importance; logical consistency; model fit;
  prediction error; protocol compliance; inconsistency; worse-than-dead rate.

## Principal findings and interpretation

- The article selected heteroskedastic cTTO model 4.
- Claimed predicted range: -0.933 for 55555 to 1 for full health; 36.3% of all
  predicted states were worse than dead.
- Reported dimension order was mobility, pain/discomfort, anxiety/depression,
  self-care, and usual activities.
- Forty-one percent of unflagged cTTO observations were worse than dead.
- These are findings of a retracted article and must carry that warning in every
  result or product view.

## Limitations and source issues

- Publication is retracted. The supplied sources do not explain why.
- Sample quotas for rurality, literacy, marital status, and insurance differed
  from population data.
- Visual support for illiterate participants was not fully validated.
- The pandemic stopped recruitment.

## High-value exact terms

- retracted article; EQ-5D-5L; Egyptian value set; EQ-VT 2.1; cTTO; DCE;
  heteroskedastic interval regression; conditional logit; hybrid model;
  feedback module; states worse than dead.

## Extraction fit

- Publication status is a mandatory search and safety field. A retracted
  product must not appear as a current value set without a clear warning.
- The system needs a relation to the retraction notice when that source becomes
  available.
- Source: Title; Abstract; Methods; Results; Discussion; Funding.
