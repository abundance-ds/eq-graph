## H07 — Systematic review of measurement properties

**Verdict: PASS WITH MINOR CORRECTIONS.** The record correctly represents this as a systematic review/evidence synthesis, preserves the distinction between 79 papers and 1,504 review-level study units, records COSMIN and GRADE methods, separates measurement properties from evidence quality, and avoids treating aggregate review ratings as one underlying study’s findings. The principal results and limitations are supported.

### Exact corrections

1. In “Instruments and administration,” state explicitly that EQ-VAS is not itself a PBM; the review included it because it is part of EQ-5D. The article makes this exception explicit (article, Methods—“Identification and Selection of Studies”). This distinction prevents an ontology instrument class from treating every searched measure as a PBM.
2. Replace “Recall period, setting, respondent perspective, and time point: not pooled as ontology fields by the review” with “The review does not report pooled values for recall period, setting, respondent perspective, or time point; individual source-study details are not reproduced in this review-level record.” “Ontology fields” is an internal description, not a source term (article, Methods—“Data Extraction” and “Assessment of the Preference-Based Measures”).

### Omissions, terminology, and fit

- No material numerical omission or overstatement was found. The 79 papers/1,504 studies, 80%/79%/57% positive-result rates, 99%/61%/23% methodological-quality rates, EQ-5D country/disease-group ratings, EQ-VAS and SF-6D findings, HUI and QWB findings, and author recommendation are supported (article, Abstract; Results after Table 1; Results paragraphs for EQ-5D, EQ-VAS, SF-6D, HUI, and QWB; Discussion; Conclusions).
- The record correctly preserves the review’s deliberate pooling decisions: EQ-5D-3L with EQ-5D-5L, HUI2 with HUI3, and no separate analysis by language or administration mode (article, Methods—“Assessment of the Preference-Based Measures”). These are review design choices, not source conflicts.
- The record correctly states that no pooled participant count exists. A paper may contain multiple individual study units because each hypothesis, ICC, or SES value is counted as a study (article, Methods—“Data Extraction”).
- The review-level product and ratings fit the ontology, but the ontology needs explicit provenance links from each aggregate property rating to its underlying paper/study set. Without that link, “sufficient,” “inconsistent,” and “insufficient” can be mistaken for a single-study result.
- “Statistical model: no pooled meta-analysis model or regression model” is useful as a source-status note. It should not be required as a normal model record for systematic reviews using rule-based synthesis.

## Cross-record ontology observations

- Proxy studies need controlled fields for proxy type, proxy perspective, proxy-person versus proxy-proxy instructions, and response-process effects such as heuristic or midpoint selection.
- Population-health studies need a product type for population reference data that is distinct from a value set, and they need explicit sample-stage links for recruited/respondent, screened, complete, and outcome-specific samples.
- Systematic reviews need separate review-paper, underlying-study-unit, property-rating, evidence-quality, and source-set provenance records. Aggregate ratings must not become direct findings of one paper or participant sample.
- No required ontology concept was found to be missing for H05–H07. The only unnecessary fields observed were the H06 “interviewer” study-type label and H07’s internal phrase “not pooled as ontology fields.”
