## S002 — EuroQol Protocols for Time Trade-Off Valuation of Health Outcomes

- **Classification:** `METHODS_RESEARCH`. Ranked purposes: `METHOD_OR_PROTOCOL_QUALITY`,
  `VALUATION_METHOD_EVALUATION`. Publication form: `CONCEPTUAL_ARTICLE`.
- **Part and data:** P1 is `CONCEPTUAL`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, `BETWEEN_METHOD`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, with
  `synthesis_design=NOT_APPLICABLE`. Data uses are `DOCUMENTARY_SOURCE`/`DOCUMENT` and
  `CONCEPTUAL_MATERIAL`/`DOCUMENT`.
- **Uses:** The `Measurement and Valuation of Health (MVH) protocol`, `Paris protocol`, and
  `EuroQol Valuation Technology (EQ-VT) protocol` are `CURRENT_STUDY_OBJECT` protocol uses. Their
  function is `VALUATION_PROTOCOL`. `Time trade-off (TTO)`, `conventional TTO`, and `composite TTO` are
  `CURRENT_STUDY_OBJECT` method uses for `PREFERENCE_ELICITATION`.
- **Outcomes and principal findings:** The outcomes are protocol quality, feasibility, and comparability.
  MVH and Paris use conventional TTO for EQ-5D-3L. The authors recommend Paris for EQ-5D-3L. EQ-VT uses
  composite TTO, computer-assisted personal interviews, interviewer training, and quality control. The
  authors recommend EQ-VT for EQ-5D-5L. Protocol variation can reduce cross-study comparability.
- **Interpretation and limitations:** The authors call for standard protocols and more research on
  iteration, time horizon, and direct protocol comparisons. They report no gold standard. Some MVH design
  choices did not have an empirical basis. Direct Paris-versus-MVH evidence was absent. Interviewer
  effects and protocol adherence remained concerns for EQ-VT.
- **Products and concepts:** No product is created by this paper. Concepts: TTO protocol standardization,
  states worse than dead, interviewer effects, and valuation-task design.

## S017 — A new method for valuing health: directly eliciting personal utility functions

- **Classification:** `METHODS_RESEARCH`. Ranked purposes: `VALUATION_METHOD_EVALUATION`,
  `HEALTH_STATE_VALUATION`, `VALUE_SET_DEVELOPMENT`. Publication form: `ORIGINAL_RESEARCH_ARTICLE`.
- **Part and data:** P1 is `QUANTITATIVE_EMPIRICAL`, `CROSS_SECTIONAL`, `WITHIN_PERSON`, `NONRANDOMIZED`,
  with `synthesis_design=NOT_APPLICABLE`. Its data use is
  `CURRENT_STUDY_COLLECTION`/`PARTICIPANT_RESPONSE`. The study completed 76 UK general-public interviews.
  Sixty interviews contributed to the reported social utility function after one interviewer's data were
  excluded. One further outlier was excluded from the reported value set.
- **Uses:** `personal utility function (PUF)` and `social utility function (SUF)` are
  `CURRENT_STUDY_OBJECT` methods for `PREFERENCE_ELICITATION` and `MAPPING_OR_DERIVATION`. Exact tasks
  include `swing weighting`, dimension ranking and rating, level weighting, `paired comparisons`, a
  TTO-type personal location of dead with bisection, and interaction questions. The source label
  `a simplified 3-level version of EQ-5D using EQ-5D-5L labels` is a `DIRECT_CURRENT_ACTIVITY` instrument
  use for `VALUATION_TARGET` and `CURRENT_HEALTH_MEASUREMENT`. `EQ VAS` measures current health.
- **Outcomes and principal findings:** The outcomes are preference or utility, feasibility, and method
  performance. Pain/discomfort and mobility were most important on average, but level ratings varied.
  Eighteen of 76 participants reported no EQ-5D state worse than dead. Fifty-five of 76 reported that the
  task was easy to understand. The illustrative SUF ranged to -0.667, and its value for 11112 was 0.85.
  Results also showed interviewer effects.
- **Interpretation and limitations:** The authors conclude that direct PUF elicitation is feasible. They
  report problems with validation tasks, the duration used to locate dead, states below 33333,
  interaction questions, interviewer influence, the basic Excel implementation, a 45-minute task,
  interpersonal comparability, and the small nonrepresentative pilot.
- **Products and concepts:** A `PROTOCOL`, the PUF elicitation and calculation procedure, and a
  `CHECKLIST_OR_TOOL`, the Excel PUF tool available on request, have development assertions. No formal
  approval, validation, or deployment assertion is made. Concepts: personal utility, social utility, dead
  anchoring, preference heterogeneity, and interviewer effects.

## S024 — A Head-On Comparison of EQ-VT- and Crosswalk-Based EQ-5D-5L Value Sets

- **Classification:** `METHODS_RESEARCH`. Ranked purposes: `PREFERENCE_COMPARISON`,
  `MAPPING_OR_CROSSWALK`, `METHOD_OR_PROTOCOL_QUALITY`. Publication form: `ORIGINAL_RESEARCH_ARTICLE`.
- **Parts and data:** P1 compares 19 value-set pairs from 18 countries. It is `QUANTITATIVE_EMPIRICAL`,
  `VARIABLE_SOURCE_TIME`, `BETWEEN_METHOD`, and `NOT_APPLICABLE`. Data uses are
  `PUBLISHED_MODEL_INPUT`/`MODEL_PARAMETER` and `PRIOR_RESEARCH_COLLECTION`/`AGGREGATE_ESTIMATE`. P2
  applies each pair to reused patient data. It is `QUANTITATIVE_EMPIRICAL`, `CROSS_SECTIONAL`,
  `BETWEEN_METHOD`, and `NOT_APPLICABLE`. Its data use is
  `PRIOR_RESEARCH_COLLECTION`/`PARTICIPANT_RESPONSE`. The application sample was 7933, with severe-state
  subgroups of 305 and 28.
- **Uses:** `EQ-VT-based EQ-5D-5L value sets` and `crosswalk-based EQ-5D-5L value sets` are
  `CURRENT_STUDY_OBJECT` scoring uses. The exact mapping source is the
  `crosswalk developed by van Hout et al.`. `EQ-5D-5L` is a `DIRECT_CURRENT_ACTIVITY` `SCORING_INPUT` in
  P2. `EQ-5D-5L` and `EQ-5D-3L` are also `INPUT_DATA_PROVENANCE` instruments for `MAPPING_SOURCE` and
  `MAPPING_TARGET`. Exact analyses include Spearman correlation, Pearson correlation, Lin's concordance
  correlation, mean absolute difference, paired t tests, scatterplots, and Bland-Altman plots.
- **Outcomes and principal findings:** The outcomes are preference or utility, agreement, and method
  performance. Spearman correlations ranged from 0.831 to 0.989. Eleven of 19 were below 0.9. Mean
  absolute difference averaged 0.149, and only five pairs were below 0.1. The percentage of negative
  states differed by +22.5 to -18.8 percentage points. Every country changed at least one dimension rank.
  A distinct mobility-level-5 band occurred in 15 of 19 comparisons. All patient-application mean
  differences were significant.
- **Interpretation and limitations:** The authors conclude that the two value set types are not
  interchangeable. Crosswalk use is acceptable only when direct valuation is not feasible. Only 19 pairs
  were available. The study could not separate effects of time, label changes, and valuation method.
- **Products and concepts:** No new value set or mapping product is claimed. Concepts: crosswalk use,
  value-set comparability, mobility wording, and severe health states.

## S031 — A qualitative study to understand public views on the relative value of health gains for children and young people in Australia compared to adults

- **Classification:** `METHODS_RESEARCH`. Ranked purposes: `PREFERENCE_COMPARISON`,
  `HEALTH_STATE_VALUATION`, `VALUATION_METHOD_EVALUATION`. Publication form: `ORIGINAL_RESEARCH_ARTICLE`.
- **Part and data:** P1 is `QUALITATIVE_INQUIRY`, `CROSS_SECTIONAL`, `WITHIN_PERSON`, and `RANDOMIZED`.
  Its data use is `CURRENT_STUDY_COLLECTION`/`QUALITATIVE_MATERIAL`. Forty-one participants were
  analyzed: seven adolescents, 11 adults without children, eight parents of healthy children, and 15
  parents of children with a health condition. Participants were randomized to forced-choice or
  unforced-choice task arms.
- **Uses:** `Person Trade-Off (PTO)` is a `DIRECT_CURRENT_ACTIVITY` method for `PREFERENCE_ELICITATION`.
  `think-aloud` and `semi-structured interviews` are methods for `QUALITATIVE_DATA_COLLECTION`.
  `framework analysis`, thematic analysis, deductive coding, and inductive coding are methods for
  `QUALITATIVE_ANALYSIS`. Seven PTO tasks compared health gains for adults and people aged one month to
  24 years. Three attitudinal questions followed.
- **Outcomes and principal findings:** The outcomes are preference or utility, feasibility, and method
  performance. Nine main themes covered life extension, quality-of-life effects, effects beyond the
  patient, decision patterns, assumed long-term effects, difficulty with scenarios, discomfort,
  PTO-versus-attitudinal differences, and equality or fairness. Parents often considered adult caregiving
  roles. Adolescents often prioritized younger people. Very young-child mental-health scenarios were
  difficult to imagine. Four of 19 participants in the unforced arm selected no preference for all PTO
  tasks.
- **Interpretation and limitations:** The authors describe complex and divergent reasoning. Reported
  limits include selection bias, unequal group sizes, limited transfer outside Australia, all-female
  interviewers, no formal reflexive journal, limited interview depth, one unanswered objective, and broad
  age categories.
- **Products and concepts:** No product is claimed. Concepts: child health, fair innings, age weighting,
  equality, resource allocation, caregiver effects, and emotional response.

## S040 — Examining the Association Between Equity-Related Factors and EQ-5D-3L Health Utilities of Patients with Cancer

- **Classification:** `UNMAPPED_VALUE` for primary research family. The main contribution is patient
  health-outcome analysis. It is not population norms, measurement performance, or economic evaluation.
  Ranked purpose: `OUTCOME_DESCRIPTION`. Publication form: `ORIGINAL_RESEARCH_ARTICLE`.
- **Part and data:** P1 is `QUANTITATIVE_EMPIRICAL`, `CROSS_SECTIONAL`, `BETWEEN_GROUP`, and
  `NONRANDOMIZED`. Data uses are `CURRENT_STUDY_COLLECTION`/`PARTICIPANT_RESPONSE` and
  `ROUTINE_SERVICE_COLLECTION`/`DOCUMENT` for cancer site. The full analyzed sample was 170. The analysis
  that excluded sex-specific cancers included 111 participants.
- **Uses:** `EQ-5D-3L` and `EQ-VAS` are `DIRECT_CURRENT_ACTIVITY` instruments for
  `CURRENT_HEALTH_MEASUREMENT`; EQ-5D-3L utility is the `OUTCOME_MEASURE`. The
  `Canadian EQ-5D-3L time-trade-off (TTO) scoring model` is the scoring use. Exact analysis labels are
  `ordinary least squares (OLS) multivariable regression`, `analysis of variance (ANOVA)`,
  `Bayesian Information Criterion (BIC)`, and `Spearman's rank correlations`.
- **Outcomes and principal findings:** The outcomes are preference or utility and health status. Compared
  with income of at least CAD 150,000, the lowest and undisclosed income groups had utility decrements of
  0.202 and 0.123 in one model, and 0.163 and 0.106 in the other model. Lower income correlated with
  greater pain/discomfort and anxiety/depression problems. Colorectal cancer had higher adjusted utility
  than the reference cancer site.
- **Interpretation and limitations:** The authors state that family income can affect cancer utility
  estimates and equity-informed HTA. Reported limits are EQ-5D-3L ceiling effects, unmeasured comorbidity
  and cancer stage, a small single-site urban pilot sample, and wide intervals in the 111-person model.
- **Products and concepts:** No product is claimed. Concepts: health inequality, socioeconomic status,
  cancer utility, and equity-informed HTA.

## S052 — Exploring the content validity of the EQ-5D-5L and four bolt-ons in atopic dermatitis and chronic urticaria

- **Classification:** `MEASUREMENT_PROPERTY_EVALUATION`. Ranked purposes: `CONTENT_VALIDITY_EVALUATION`,
  `MEASUREMENT_PROPERTY_EVALUATION`. Publication form: `ORIGINAL_RESEARCH_ARTICLE`.
- **Part and data:** P1 is `QUALITATIVE_INQUIRY`, `CROSS_SECTIONAL`, `BETWEEN_GROUP`, and
  `NONRANDOMIZED`. Its data uses are `CURRENT_STUDY_COLLECTION`/`QUALITATIVE_MATERIAL` and
  `CURRENT_STUDY_COLLECTION`/`PARTICIPANT_RESPONSE`. Thirty people were analyzed: 15 with atopic
  dermatitis and 15 with chronic urticaria. Twenty- four interviews were online and six were in person.
- **Uses:** `EQ-5D-5L` and the exact bolt-ons `skin irritation`, `self-confidence`, `sleep`, and
  `social relationships` are `DIRECT_CURRENT_ACTIVITY` `CONTENT_TEST_OBJECT` uses. `EQ VAS` is a current
  health measure. `Patient-Oriented Eczema Measure (POEM)`, `Urticaria Control Test`, and a
  `10-point NRS` characterize the sample. Exact methods are concept elicitation, think-aloud,
  retrospective probing, cognitive debriefing, content analysis, thematic analysis, saturation grids, and
  MAXQDA. `Standards for Reporting Qualitative Research (SRQR)` is a `REPORTING_GUIDELINE`.
- **Outcomes and principal findings:** The outcome is content validity. Itch was reported by all
  atopic-dermatitis participants and 93% of chronic-urticaria participants. Skin irritation was the most
  relevant bolt-on. Skin irritation and self-confidence increased comprehensiveness in both groups. Sleep
  also had support. Social relationships had less clear support. Participants reported some overlap
  between pain and skin irritation, and between anxiety and self-confidence, but they also described
  distinct content.
- **Interpretation and limitations:** The authors support skin irritation and self-confidence as
  additions and recommend more work on sleep and social relationships. Limits include simplified
  self-reported severity screening, self-reported diagnoses, imbalance in urticaria type, and geographic
  and cultural limits.
- **Products and concepts:** No product is claimed. Concepts: itch, bolt-ons, skin irritation,
  self-confidence, sleep, and social relationships.

## S071 — A Systematic Review of Studies Comparing the Measurement Properties of EQ-5D-3L and EQ-5D-5L

- **Classification:** `EVIDENCE_SYNTHESIS`. Ranked purposes: `EVIDENCE_SYNTHESIS`,
  `MEASUREMENT_PROPERTY_EVALUATION`. Publication form: `REVIEW_ARTICLE`.
- **Parts and data:** P1 is `EVIDENCE_SYNTHESIS`, `VARIABLE_SOURCE_TIME`, `BETWEEN_INSTRUMENT`, and
  `NOT_APPLICABLE`, with `synthesis_design=SYSTEMATIC_REVIEW`. P2 is the pooled ceiling analysis. It is
  `EVIDENCE_SYNTHESIS`, `VARIABLE_SOURCE_TIME`, `BETWEEN_INSTRUMENT`, and `NOT_APPLICABLE`, with
  `synthesis_design=META_ANALYSIS`. Data uses are `REVIEW_EXTRACTED_EVIDENCE`/`DOCUMENT` and
  `REVIEW_EXTRACTED_EVIDENCE`/`AGGREGATE_ESTIMATE`. Twenty-four studies were included.
- **Uses:** `EQ-5D-3L` and `EQ-5D-5L` are `CURRENT_STUDY_OBJECT` `EVIDENCE_SYNTHESIS_TARGET` and
  `COMPARATOR` uses. Exact methods include PubMed, EMBASE, PsycINFO, and EuroQol website searches;
  Shannon's H' and J'; the Janssen et al. inconsistency method; effect size; standardized response mean;
  probability of superiority; ICC; kappa; weighted kappa; percentage agreement; and random-effects
  logit-transformed pooled proportions. The `9-item critical appraisal tool`, based on the NHLBI
  `Quality Assessment Tool for Observational Cohort and Cross-Sectional Studies`, is a
  `CRITICAL_APPRAISAL_PROTOCOL`.
- **Outcomes and principal findings:** The outcomes are measurement property and agreement. Missing
  values and floor effects were usually below 5%. Inconsistencies ranged from 0 to 10.6% and were usually
  below 5%. Shannon's H' was always higher for 5L. Nineteen of 22 studies reported a lower 11111 ceiling
  for 5L. Pooled ceiling proportions in patient samples were 0.23 for 3L and 0.18 for 5L; population
  estimates were 0.53 and 0.43. Evidence for responsiveness and test-retest reliability was mixed and
  insufficient.
- **Interpretation and limitations:** The authors conclude that 5L performs as well as or better than 3L
  for most reviewed properties. Heterogeneous populations, languages, value sets, administration orders,
  and designs limited synthesis and transfer. The authors also report an absence of established standards
  for preference-based measurement studies.
- **Products and concepts:** No product is claimed. Concepts: ceiling effect, floor effect,
  informativity, response inconsistency, responsiveness, reliability, and cross-cultural response
  patterns.

## S084 — Selecting Bolt-on Dimensions for EQ-5D with Pairwise Choices

- **Classification:** `METHODS_RESEARCH`. Ranked purposes: `VALUATION_METHOD_EVALUATION`,
  `PREFERENCE_COMPARISON`, `INSTRUMENT_DEVELOPMENT`, `HEALTH_STATE_VALUATION`. Publication form:
  `ORIGINAL_RESEARCH_ARTICLE`.
- **Parts and data:** P1 is face-validity work. It is `QUALITATIVE_INQUIRY`, `CROSS_SECTIONAL`,
  `NONCOMPARATIVE`, and `NONRANDOMIZED`. It included five members of the public and six patients with
  chronic conditions. P2 is the preference experiment. It is `QUANTITATIVE_EMPIRICAL`, `CROSS_SECTIONAL`,
  `WITHIN_PERSON`, and `RANDOMIZED`. It analyzed 1040 UK general-public respondents. P1 uses
  `CURRENT_STUDY_COLLECTION`/`QUALITATIVE_MATERIAL`. P2 uses
  `CURRENT_STUDY_COLLECTION`/`PARTICIPANT_RESPONSE`. The integration is `SEQUENTIAL`.
- **Uses:** `EQ-5D-5L` and the exact candidate bolt-ons `hearing`, `sleep`, `cognition`, `energy`, and
  `relationships` are `CURRENT_STUDY_OBJECT` `DEVELOPMENT_OBJECT` and `VALUATION_TARGET` uses.
  `pairwise choices` are a `DIRECT_CURRENT_ACTIVITY` `PREFERENCE_ELICITATION` method. Face-validity focus
  groups and content analysis are current qualitative methods. Exact models are
  `logistic regressions with clustered sandwich estimators`, the main-effect bolt-on model, the
  bolt-on-level main-effect model, and separate models by pair, bolt-on, and severity.
- **Outcomes and principal findings:** The outcomes are preference or utility, content validity, and
  method performance. Level-1 additions usually had no significant effect. Levels 3 and 5 reduced
  preference for the state with the bolt-on. At level 5, the rank was hearing, cognition, relationships,
  energy, then sleep. At level 3, cognition had the largest effect. Effects changed by severity and
  health-state pair.
- **Interpretation and limitations:** The authors conclude that pairwise choice is a flexible, low-cost
  input to bolt-on selection. It does not replace psychometric evidence or a full revaluation. Limits are
  only three health- state pairs, no levels 2 or 4, insufficient power for interactions, fixed
  within-block order, possible focusing effects, and no comparison with TTO.
- **Products and concepts:** Five candidate bolt-on descriptor sets are `INSTRUMENT_VERSION` products
  with development and face-testing assertions. They are not final approved bolt-ons. Concepts: bolt-on
  selection, severity- dependent preference, focusing effects, and full instrument revaluation.

## S089 — Design and implementation of data quality controls in the EQ-DAPHNIE study

- **Classification:** `METHODS_RESEARCH`. Ranked purpose: `METHOD_OR_PROTOCOL_QUALITY`. Publication form:
  `ORIGINAL_RESEARCH_ARTICLE`.
- **Parts and data:** P1 is the UK pilot. It is `QUANTITATIVE_EMPIRICAL`, `CROSS_SECTIONAL`,
  `BETWEEN_METHOD`, and `RANDOMIZED`. It analyzed 3012 completed surveys across six survey versions. P2
  is the 15-country rollout. It is `QUANTITATIVE_EMPIRICAL`, `CROSS_SECTIONAL`, `BETWEEN_CONTEXT`, and
  `NONRANDOMIZED`. The abstract reports 68,411 completed surveys. Both parts use
  `CURRENT_STUDY_COLLECTION`/`PARTICIPANT_RESPONSE`. The parts have `SEQUENTIAL` integration.
- **Uses:** The `EQ-DAPHNIE survey` is the `CURRENT_STUDY_OBJECT`. Direct measures include `EQ-5D-5L`,
  `EQ VAS`, `EQ-HWB (short or long versions)`, the source-described
  `2-item versions of the PHQ-9 and GAD-7`, and selected country measures such as `ASCOT` and `WHO-5`.
  Exact quality-control methods are `Google reCAPTCHA v3`, Dynata IP-address monitoring, Dynata
  `QualityScore`, a five-minute speeding threshold, duplicate checks, repeated- question agreement,
  missing-data review, outlier review, quota sampling, soft launches, and real-time monitoring
  dashboards.
- **Outcomes and principal findings:** The outcome is method performance and data quality. Completion
  rates ranged from 22.9% to 60.8%. Bot exclusions averaged 3.0% and reached 11.7% in China. Speeding
  averaged 0.3%. Mean completion time ranged from 18.3 to 31.4 minutes. Missingness ranged from 0.0% to
  48.7% across variables and countries. Average proportional quota achievement ranged from 68.7% to
  98.6%. Repeated marital status agreed in 92.8% to 98.9% of responses; age agreed in 92.3% to 98.7%.
- **Interpretation and limitations:** The authors state that the controls produced generally high-quality
  data, but country differences must inform norms and cross-country comparisons. Limits include
  nonprobability-panel selection bias, unequal panel reach, residual bias after weighting, missing data,
  incomplete quotas, and the inability to assess Dynata-panel representativeness directly.
- **Products and concepts:** The restricted `EQ-DAPHNIE dataset` is a `DATASET` with collection and
  quality-control assertions. Concepts: online- panel quality, bot detection, speeding, missing data,
  quota achievement, survey burden, and cross-country comparability.

## S091 — Cross-sectional and longitudinal comparison of health outcomes with and without post COVID-19 condition

- **Classification:** `UNMAPPED_VALUE` for primary research family. The main contribution is longitudinal
  patient health-outcome comparison. It is not a measurement-property study or a population-norm study.
  Ranked purpose: `OUTCOME_DESCRIPTION`. Publication form: `ORIGINAL_RESEARCH_ARTICLE`.
- **Part and data:** P1 is `QUANTITATIVE_EMPIRICAL`, `LONGITUDINAL_REPEATED`, `BETWEEN_GROUP`, and
  `NONRANDOMIZED`. Its data use is `CURRENT_STUDY_COLLECTION`/`PARTICIPANT_RESPONSE`. Of 19,902 T1
  completers, 4999 completed T2 and were analyzed: 240 with post COVID-19 condition, 107 with acute
  COVID-19, 1798 with chronic conditions, and 2854 classified as healthy.
- **Uses:** `EQ-5D-5L`, `EQ VAS`, `World Health Organisation-Five (WHO-5) Well-Being Index`,
  `Patient Health Questionnaire (PHQ)-9`, and `General Anxiety Disorder (GAD)-7` are
  `DIRECT_CURRENT_ACTIVITY` `OUTCOME_MEASURE` uses at T1 and T2. The `US value set` is the EQ-5D-5L
  scoring use in all six countries. Exact analyses include one-way ANOVA, Bonferroni post-hoc tests,
  paired-samples t tests, stacked bar charts, a Sankey plot, Venn diagrams, and the `eulerr` R package.
- **Outcomes and principal findings:** Outcomes are health status, EQ VAS, and mental well-being. At T2,
  mean EQ-5D-5L values were 0.70, 0.73, 0.75, and 0.92 across the four groups. Mean EQ VAS scores were
  66, 65, 68, and 81. Post COVID-19 participants had the worst overall mental-well-being scores. The
  chronic-condition group had the largest EQ-5D-5L decline. The acute- COVID group had the largest EQ
  VAS, WHO-5, and PHQ-9 deterioration. Forty- eight percent of the post-COVID group came from the T1
  chronic-condition group.
- **Interpretation and limitations:** The authors interpret pre-existing chronic disease as a probable
  reason for the high T1 burden and smaller measured change in the post-COVID group. Limits include
  self-reported and probable infections, no verification of chronic conditions, no adjustment for
  condition count or type, different T1 and T2 disease definitions, 25% follow-up, differential
  attrition, and limited generalizability.
- **Products and concepts:** No product is claimed. Concepts: post COVID-19 condition, chronic disease,
  health trajectories, mental well-being, comorbidity, attrition, and pandemic effects.

## S099 — Measurement of health-related quality of life from conception to postpartum

- **Classification:** `POPULATION_REFERENCE_DESCRIPTION`. Ranked purposes: `POPULATION_NORMS`,
  `OUTCOME_DESCRIPTION`. Publication form: `ORIGINAL_RESEARCH_ARTICLE`.
- **Part and data:** P1 is `QUANTITATIVE_EMPIRICAL`, `CROSS_SECTIONAL`, `BETWEEN_GROUP`, and
  `NONRANDOMIZED`. Its data use is `CURRENT_STUDY_COLLECTION`/`PARTICIPANT_RESPONSE`. Of 12,733
  invitation respondents, 6661 completed the survey and 5250 entered analysis. The final sample included
  3618 pregnant and 1632 postpartum adults across two survey waves.
- **Uses:** `EQ-5D-5L` and `EQ visual analog scale (EQ VAS)` are `DIRECT_CURRENT_ACTIVITY`
  `CURRENT_HEALTH_MEASUREMENT` and `OUTCOME_MEASURE` uses. `Patient Health Questionnaire-4 (PHQ-4)` and
  `Generalized Anxiety Disorder-7 questionnaire (GAD-7)` are direct current measures and subgroup
  measures. The `US-based value set for the adult population` is the scoring use. `quantile regression`
  with month of pregnancy or postpartum as a cubic spline is the primary model. Wave, conception quarter,
  and conception year analyses are validation and sensitivity models.
- **Outcomes and principal findings:** Outcomes are health status, EQ VAS, and preference or utility.
  Problems increased through pregnancy and declined in the first postpartum month. Median utility was
  lowest in pregnancy month 9 at 0.78. Utility declined by 0.21 through month 9, increased by 0.10 in the
  first postpartum month, and then stabilized. EQ VAS did not change significantly by month.
  Anxiety/depression was most common, but pain, activities, mobility, and self-care drove the gestational
  pattern.
- **Interpretation and limitations:** The authors state that studies must account for pregnancy stage.
  They do not claim that this was a psychometric study. Limits include underrepresentation of Black
  participants, selection bias, self-report and recall bias, cross-sectional rather than within-person
  trajectories, no test-retest analysis, and possible pandemic effects.
- **Products and concepts:** Monthly pregnancy and postpartum EQ-5D-5L estimates are
  `POPULATION_REFERENCE_DATA` with a development assertion. The authors state that they can inform future
  reference, but they do not call them national norms. Concepts: pregnancy, postpartum recovery,
  gestational stage, ceiling effects, maternal health, and pandemic exposure.

## S100 — Statistical Methods for Analyzing EQ-5D in Randomized Clinical Trials

- **Classification:** `EVIDENCE_SYNTHESIS`. Ranked purposes: `EVIDENCE_SYNTHESIS`,
  `METHOD_OR_PROTOCOL_QUALITY`. Publication form: `REVIEW_ARTICLE`.
- **Part and data:** P1 is `EVIDENCE_SYNTHESIS`, `VARIABLE_SOURCE_TIME`, `BETWEEN_METHOD`, and
  `NOT_APPLICABLE`, with `synthesis_design=SYSTEMATIC_REVIEW`. Data uses are
  `REVIEW_EXTRACTED_EVIDENCE`/`DOCUMENT` and `REVIEW_EXTRACTED_EVIDENCE`/`AGGREGATE_ESTIMATE`. Searches
  identified 11,633 records. The review included 2125 unique randomized clinical trials.
- **Uses:** `EQ-5D dimension responses`, `EQ-5D utilities`, and `EQ visual analog scale (EQ VAS)` are
  `CURRENT_STUDY_OBJECT` `EVIDENCE_SYNTHESIS_TARGET` uses. Exact evidence-identification sources are
  `MEDLINE`, `EMBASE`, `ClinicalTrials.gov`, and publication searches in `PubMed`. `Covidence` supported
  duplicate screening. `Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses` is a
  `REPORTING_GUIDELINE`. Statistical methods were categorized as descriptive, bivariate, multivariable,
  and survival analysis, with fixed-effect, mixed-effect, generalized-estimating-equation, and other
  exact model labels retained in the extracted method registry.
- **Outcomes and principal findings:** The outcome is method performance and data quality. Utility was
  analyzed in 1592 trials, EQ VAS in 1197, and dimension responses in 385. Linear fixed-effect models
  were most common for one postbaseline utility measure. Linear mixed-effect models were most common for
  repeated postbaseline utility measures. Only 221 of 2054 trials with numerical EQ-5D checked
  assumptions, and 438 adjusted for baseline. Only 661 trials assessed missing data explicitly. Of these,
  347 used imputation; multiple imputation and last observation carried forward were most common.
- **Interpretation and limitations:** The authors find large model variation, sparse baseline adjustment,
  and inadequate missing-data handling. They call for comparative method research and analysis guidance.
  Limits include the long search period, changes in practice over time, sparse reporting of secondary
  outcomes, possible undercounting of analysis details, and possible publication bias. The review did not
  appraise trial risk of bias because its target was statistical-method use, not treatment effects.
- **Products and concepts:** No product is claimed. Concepts: trial estimands, baseline adjustment,
  missing data, model assumptions, ceiling effects, statistical reporting, and PRO analysis.

## Proposed changes — proposals only

1.  **Proposal only: add `HEALTH_OUTCOME_RESEARCH` to `primary_research_family`.** Define it as primary
    empirical research whose main contribution is a patient or clinical-population health outcome,
    utility outcome, symptom outcome, associated factor, or outcome trajectory. Do not use it for
    explicit population norms, measurement performance, evidence synthesis, or economic decisions. S040
    and S091 support this proposal.
2.  **Proposal only: add a `TaskDesign` record.** It should link a protocol or method use to the task
    unit, target profiles, attributes, levels, duration, comparator, block, order, randomization unit,
    indifference rule, and number of tasks. S017, S031, and S084 support this proposal.
3.  **Proposal only: add `StudyFactor` with controlled analytic roles.** Test roles such as condition or
    exposure, comparator, stratifier, effect modifier, and target stage. Keep the exact factor label and
    level open. S031, S040, S091, and S099 support this proposal.
4.  **Proposal only: add `StakeholderInvolvement`.** Link an agent to an activity, study stage,
    involvement role, and reported influence on the output. S057 and S058 support this proposal and
    repeat the round-1 G168 gap.
5.  **Proposal only: add `issuer` and `evidence_basis` to a product-state assertion.** This must not turn
    approval, validation, and deployment into one maturity scale. S062 supports this proposal.
