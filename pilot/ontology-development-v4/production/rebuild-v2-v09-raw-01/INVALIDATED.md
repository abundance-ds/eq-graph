# Invalidated version-0.9 rebuild

This run stopped after 48 saved records. None of its records can enter version
2 or a later rebuild.

gap. Different papers reported an eligible administrative cohort, eligible
expressions of interest, and title-and-abstract screening, but version 0.9 had
no general `IDENTIFIED`, `SCREENED`, or `ELIGIBLE` sample stage. The 209-paper
source set contains eligibility language in 65 papers and screening language
in 61 papers. Version 0.10 adds these three general stages. It does not add a
new entity, relation, study family, or paper-specific value.
