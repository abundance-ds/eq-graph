# Version-0.10 focused result

The three test records passed deterministic validation. The new `IDENTIFIED`,
`SCREENED`, and `ELIGIBLE` sample stages represented all three source flows.

One test paper also exposed two broad outcome gaps. Clinical complications and
readmission had no suitable family. Corpus text contains clinical-event terms
in 77 papers and health-service-use terms in 24 papers. Version 0.11 therefore
adds `CLINICAL_EVENT_OR_MORBIDITY` and `HEALTH_SERVICE_USE`. Version 0.10 is a
successful intermediate test, not the rebuild input.
